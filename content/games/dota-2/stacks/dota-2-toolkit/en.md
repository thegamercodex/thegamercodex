Dota 2 is one of the most complex games out there: 120+ heroes, thousands of item combinations, and a draft that can win the game before the first creep. The client gives you the bare minimum, so players who improve lean on external tools for each phase. This five-tool stack covers the full cycle without redundancy.

## A game's cycle

**In the draft**, **DotaPicker** helps you counter-pick: which heroes answer what the enemy picked and which to avoid. Once you have your hero, **Dota2ProTracker** shows what pros are building and skilling on it right now —item and skill builds updated to the patch.

**During the game**, **Dota Coach** works as an overlay: hero-specific tips, rune timings, stacking reminders, and other things easy to forget in the heat of the match.

**After playing**, two complementary tools: **Dotabuff** is the classic stats hub —your per-hero win rate, match history, and opponent scouting— and **STRATZ** adds the interpretive layer with AI predictions, suggestions on what to improve, and a mobile app to review from your phone. Having both covers the "what happened" and the "what to do about it."

## Why these five

Each solves a distinct phase:

- **Draft and counter-pick** → DotaPicker
- **Hero builds** → Dota2ProTracker
- **Live coaching** → Dota Coach
- **Stats and scouting** → Dotabuff
- **Insights and interpretation** → STRATZ

You can play Dota with fewer, but this is the set that covers every moment of the match. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
