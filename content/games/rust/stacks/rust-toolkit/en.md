Rust is played in wipes: each server periodically resets progress, and the first days define the whole playthrough. Picking the right server, knowing the map, raising a defensible base, and understanding raid costs aren't details, they're the difference between thriving and dying naked on the beach. The game gives you none of it. This six-tool stack covers the full wipe.

## Before the wipe

Preparation starts with picking where to play. **Just Wiped** lets you find servers by wipe schedule, filtering by size, region, and reset time. **BattleMetrics** complements it with server and player tracking: historical population, player search, and live status, useful for evaluating a server or following someone. And before joining, **RustMaps** shows you the server map by seed —monuments, resources, and routes— so you plan where to settle.

## During the wipe

Once in, **Fortify** lets you design your base and plan defense or raids before spending resources building blindly on the server. For combat and economy decisions, **RustLab** is the reference: crafting, loot tables, and raid costs, so you know what it costs to break a wall or what's worth it. And **Rust+**, the official app, connects you from your phone with smart device alarms, the server map, and messaging.

## Why these six

Each covers a part of the wipe:

- **Picking a server** → Just Wiped + BattleMetrics
- **Previewing the map** → RustMaps
- **Base design** → Fortify
- **Costs and crafting** → RustLab
- **Companion** → Rust+

You don't need all six to survive, but together they're what separates a planned wipe from an improvised one. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
