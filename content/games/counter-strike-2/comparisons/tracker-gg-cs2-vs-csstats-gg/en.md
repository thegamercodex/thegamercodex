---

To review your CS2 match history and stats outside the client, there are two free options that compete directly: **CS Stats** (csstats.gg), a lightweight, CS-native tracker centered on fast Steam ID lookups, and **Tracker.gg CS2**, the CS2 section of Tracker Network — the same network behind the Valorant, Apex, and Marvel Rivals trackers. They cover the same basic need — seeing how you're doing — but from different philosophies.

## Focus: CS-native vs multi-game

CS Stats is built around CS and nothing else. That shows in the speed and the details: instant lookup by pasting a Steam ID with no login, ESEA data integration, and a layout built for the stats a CS player actually looks at (KDA, per-map win rate, ELO evolution, HS%). It's the tool the CS community links when someone says "drop your csstats."

Tracker.gg CS2 is a section inside a platform that covers many games. Its strength isn't beating CS Stats on CS depth, but consolidation: if you already use Tracker Network for other titles, your CS2 stats live under the same account, the same app, and the same UI. It also offers the interface in several languages (es, pt, fr, de, ja, ko), which CS Stats doesn't.

## Speed and friction

CS Stats wins on zero friction: you don't need an account to see your profile or another player's, just the Steam ID. To quickly scout a teammate or opponent, it's the shortest path.

Tracker.gg CS2 works without login for basic lookups, but its real value appears when you link your network account — that's where the cross-game consolidation pays off. If you only play CS, that extra layer doesn't add much.

## When each one wins

| Scenario | Better option |
|---|---|
| Quick Steam ID lookup with no login | CS Stats |
| Consolidate CS2 with Valorant / Apex / Marvel Rivals | Tracker.gg CS2 |
| ESEA integration and CS-native data | CS Stats |
| Interface in Spanish or another language | Tracker.gg CS2 |
| Familiarity and adoption in the CS2 community | CS Stats |

## Verdict

For pure CS2, **CS Stats** is the short path: faster, no login, and with the familiarity the community already has. **Tracker.gg CS2** makes sense when CS isn't the only game you track — if you already live in the Tracker Network through Valorant, Apex, or Marvel Rivals, having CS2 in the same place with the UI in your language is the convenience that justifies it. It's not that one is a better tracker in the abstract: it depends on whether CS is your only game or one among several.
