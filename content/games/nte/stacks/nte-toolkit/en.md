Like any open-world title with a gacha system, Neverness to Everness mixes exploration, character building, and managing limited resources. Making good decisions early —who to invest in, what to prioritize, when to pull— saves a lot of time and frustration. The game gives you no tools for that, so the community built them. This stack gathers the five most useful.

## How you use it

To get off to a good start, **NTE Guide** offers general and progression guides that orient you in the first hours. Once you have characters to build, **Prydwen (NTE)** gives you per-character guides and tier lists —who each one combines with and what to prioritize— and **Zero Luck (NTE)** goes deeper with builds and theorycraft to optimize your mains.

For exploration, the **interactive map** shows collectibles, resources, and points of interest, which saves you wandering the world blindly. And since gacha is central, **NTE Wiki Tools** helps you track banners and plan your pulls following pity, so you spend your premium currency wisely.

## Why these five

Each covers a layer of the game:

- **Early progression** → NTE Guide
- **Character guides** → Prydwen (NTE)
- **Builds and theorycraft** → Zero Luck (NTE)
- **Exploration** → Interactive map
- **Gacha and banners** → NTE Wiki Tools

Being a relatively new game, this ecosystem keeps growing, but these five already cover the essentials to optimize your account. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
