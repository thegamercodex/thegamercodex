## What it is

The Neverness to Everness Wiki on Fandom is the standard community-edited wiki for the game, hosted on the Fandom platform (formerly Wikia). Any registered user can edit; content emerges from community contributions. Sections cover characters (Espers with stats, skills, lore, voice actors), Hethereau locations, game mechanics, items, enemies, and events.

It's the most encyclopedic NTE source — covering breadth of info that editorial sites like Prydwen ignore (lore, voice acting, trivia, character relationships).

## What problem it solves

When you want a character's background lore, voice actor name, or secondary narrative details, editorial wikis (Prydwen, NTE Guide) don't cover that. Fandom does: each character has a page with history, relationships, quotes, voice cast.

It also works as a "factual" info repo that rarely changes: Manifestation lore, zone descriptions, items, and crafting. Since it's community-edited, depth can be uneven but coverage is broad.

## Differentiation

**Against Prydwen/Game8/GameWith**: Fandom is encyclopedic; the others are editorial with meta focus. Different purposes.

**Against NTEGame**: NTEGame is more structured and curated (community hub with builds, leaderboards), Fandom is a wikipedia-style collaborative encyclopedia.

## What people use it for

- **Lore deep dives**: reading an Esper's personal history, their relationships with other characters, and role in the main narrative.
- **Voice acting credits**: discovering who voices each character.
- **Detailed mechanics**: descriptions of systems other sites don't cover (how X buff works, what Y status does).
- **Exhaustive lists**: items, enemies, locations in navigable list format.
- **Trivia and references**: easter eggs, cultural references, fun facts.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you're looking for optimized builds or tier list, Fandom might have that info but it's usually outdated or inferior to Prydwen/NTE Guide. If you want a calculator, there isn't one. If you want Discord or community discussion, it's not that kind of site.

## How it's used in practice

1. You go to `neverness-to-everness.fandom.com` and the wiki home has featured categories.
2. Search a character by name in the search; it opens a page with stats, story, relationships sections, etc.
3. For mechanics, there are categories like "Combat Mechanics" or "Esper Powers" in the sidebar.
4. To contribute, register and edit pages like any wiki.

## Honest limitations

- **Uneven content**: as community-edited, some pages are extensive and others are stubs.
- **Very invasive ads**: Fandom monetizes aggressively, especially autoplay video ads on mobile.
- **Not optimized for meta**: builds, tier lists, team comps are weak vs editorial sites.
- **Dated layout**: the Fandom platform isn't the most modern UX; navigation can feel heavy.

## How to get started

No signup for reading. Go to `neverness-to-everness.fandom.com` and browse. To edit or contribute, free signup on the Fandom platform.
