## What it is

NTE Wiki (ntewiki.online) is a fan wiki focused on tools — specifically five calculators dedicated to numeric planning:

1. **Pity Calculator**: estimates how many pulls you need to Board Modification, hard pity, or featured SS-Class guarantee.
2. **Pull Calculator**: converts different currency types (Stellar Crystals, banner tickets) into pulls per banner.
3. **Team Builder**: lets you select your Espers and suggests recommended team comps by role.
4. **Build Planner**: connects characters, Arcs, and stat priorities for coherent team comps.
5. **Material Planner**: calculates resources needed to level and promote characters and Arcs.

Fan-made, fully free, no official Hotta Studio affiliation.

## What problem it solves

Before calculators, players did math in their own spreadsheets or in their heads: "I have X currency, how many pulls is that? Do I hit pity?" or "I need to level three characters to 70, how many total materials?". NTE Wiki Online automates that.

The difference with having a calc inside NTE Guide or Zero Luck is that NTE Wiki Online specializes: each of its five tools is well-built and not an add-on inside a larger site. If gacha planning is the focus, this is the best focused option.

## Differentiation

**Against NTE Guide / Zero Luck**: those have calculators included but as a feature inside a bigger site. NTE Wiki Online is 100% calculators, no editorial distraction.

**Against Neverness Nav**: Nav has a pull simulator that simulates real odds by pulling. NTE Wiki Online calculates deterministically (how many pulls you reach with your currency). Complementary.

## What people use it for

- **Pre-banner planning**: before a banner, seeing if your current currency reaches pity.
- **Save calculations**: planning how much to save between banners to reach a future character.
- **Material farming planning**: knowing how many weeks of daily quests you need to get all materials.
- **Team optimization**: putting in your current roster and seeing which team comps are available.
- **Reroll planning**: for new accounts, planning the optimal reroll.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you only want tier list or builds, that's not what's offered. If you want dense editorial, Prydwen. If you want community tools or sharing, Zero Luck or NTEGame. NTE Wiki Online is for the "I need to do gacha math fast" moment.

## How it's used in practice

1. You go to `ntewiki.online` and the home has the 5 calculators as tiles.
2. For Pity Calc, you enter pulls done and currency available; it returns how close you are to hard pity.
3. For Material Planner, you pick characters and target levels; it sums total materials.
4. For Team Builder, you mark your Espers and it suggests comps by role.
5. Build Planner: combine character + Arc + Cartridges and see aggregate stats.

## Honest limitations

- **No account sync**: you have to enter your data manually each time; nothing saved unless you create an optional account.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Simple UI**: functional but basic aesthetic; doesn't compete with Zero Luck or Prydwen polish.
- **SS-Class coverage limited to current meta**: if a newly-released character isn't in the dataset, they don't appear until the team updates.

## How to get started

No signup. Go to `ntewiki.online`, pick the calc you need. Recommended bookmark if you use it before each banner.
