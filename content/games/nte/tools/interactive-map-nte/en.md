## What it is

Interactive Map App (interactivemap.app) is a multi-gacha map platform — covering Genshin, HSR, Wuthering Waves, and added NTE at global launch. Its distinctive value: marker volume (4,000+ in Hethereau), native mobile support, and translations into 13 languages.

Markers cover chests (common, rare, exquisite, luxurious), world bosses, elite monsters, side quests, NPCs, farming materials, anomalies, and unique collectibles. The interface lets you filter by marker type, mark items completed, and save progress locally.

100% free.

## What problem it solves

Hethereau is a dense open world with material hidden behind exploration. Without a map, finding the last chest in a district can take hours of wandering. Interactive Map App centralizes the info: you filter by "uncompleted chests" and see exactly where they are.

The high marker count (4,000+ vs Zero Luck's 2,968 or more curated sets from NTE Guide) means more exhaustive coverage: it covers what other sites may have missed or not updated.

## Differentiation

**Against Zero Luck and NTE Guide**: both have maps but less exhaustive. Interactive Map App is map-first: map quality and coverage is its differentiator.

**Against Neverness Nav**: Nav has a map but as part of a wider stack. If you only want the map, interactivemap.app is the better focused option.

## What people use it for

- **Completionism**: marking every chest and collectible in the game to reach 100%.
- **Material farming**: locating specific enemies and plants for required drops.
- **World boss farming**: locating bosses and seeing respawn timers.
- **Quest hunting**: finding NPCs with side quests.
- **Multi-account**: progress is local, so players with multiple accounts can track them separately.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you don't play completionist and just want the main quest, you don't need it. If you want a site with builds + tier list + map all in one, Zero Luck or NTE Guide are better options. If you need builds or math tools, this isn't the site.

## How it's used in practice

1. You go to `interactivemap.app/neverness-to-everness/maps/` and the Hethereau map loads.
2. You filter by marker category in the sidebar (chests, bosses, collectibles).
3. Click a marker to see description, screenshot, and instructions if applicable.
4. Mark completed so it disappears from your view (local state).
5. To change language, header selector.
6. For mobile, the interface adapts automatically.

## Honest limitations

- **No account sync**: progress saves in local storage. Switch browsers or clear cookies and you lose tracking.
- **Occasionally outdated markers**: with each new event or map expansion, it takes time to add new markers.
- **Visible ads**: the site is ad-monetized.
- **Localization mismatches**: marker names sometimes don't match 1:1 with the game's Spanish client.

## How to get started

No signup. Go to `interactivemap.app/neverness-to-everness/maps/`. For a different language, top-right selector. For mobile, open directly in the phone browser — no app to install.
