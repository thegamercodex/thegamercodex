## What Neverness to Everness is

Neverness to Everness, shortened to NTE, is an open-world action RPG with anime aesthetic and supernatural urban setting. Hotta Studio (a Perfect World subsidiary, known for Tower of Fantasy) built it as their second global gacha bet, this time with much stronger identity: instead of a classic fantasy world, NTE drops players into Hethereau, a contemporary city where supernatural phenomena called Manifestations distort reality.

The player takes on the role of an Appraiser — an investigator specialized in containing anomalies. Alongside a team of Espers, characters with unique powers derived from their personal history with the supernatural, they navigate the city investigating cases, fighting hostile entities, and dismantling rituals gone out of control.

## Why it matters

The April 2026 launch marked one of the year's most discussed gacha debuts: CN¥100 million in revenue on day one, an official Discord community of 300k+ members in its first month, and broad coverage in western gaming media. The game entered a crowded slot (Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Wuthering Waves, Zenless Zone Zero) but differentiated on two fronts: the supernatural urban setting with no clear precedents in the genre, and the combat system mixing fast character-swap with chargeable abilities and unique anomaly mechanics.

Full cross-platform from day one (PC, PS5, iOS, Android, Mac) also helped expand the base faster than competitors who staggered their releases.

## How the ecosystem works

The game loop combines open-world exploration (Hethereau has 5 districts with secrets, NPCs, cases, and collectibles), a banner system for pulling new Espers and Arcs (the weapons), and an endgame focused on Anomaly Containment — boss-style encounters requiring specific team comps and rotations.

Monetization follows the standard gacha pattern: premium currency for pulls, monthly battle pass, daily login monthly card, and cosmetic skins. Like its genre peers, it's fully completable as F2P, but securing specific characters can require significant spending when they appear on limited-time banners.

## Tool ecosystem status

Because it's so new (one month since global launch when this codex was assembled), NTE's third-party tool ecosystem is still forming. What's already here covers the essentials: tier lists updated week to week (Prydwen, Game8, GameWith, NTE Guide), interactive maps with thousands of markers (interactivemap.app, Neverness Nav), build calculators with verifiable math (NTE Wiki Online, Zero Luck), and the official community centralized around the 300k+ member Discord.

Mature tools in the Genshin Optimizer or Akasha mold are missing — the Cartridge and Arc equivalents will probably surface in the coming months once someone commits the time. In the meantime, NTE Guide and Zero Luck cover most of the workflow with native calculators, and the community uses Discord and Reddit to fill the gaps.
