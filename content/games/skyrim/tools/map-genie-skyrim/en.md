## What it is

Map Genie is an interactive-map platform for open-world games (Skyrim, Witcher 3, Elden Ring, Cyberpunk 2077, Hogwarts Legacy, GTA, and more). The Skyrim map covers the full world (Skyrim + Solstheim from the Dragonborn DLC) with every relevant location marked.

Functionally: a zoomable Google Maps-style view, filters by category (shouts, dragons, treasure maps, books, ingredients, smithing materials, daedric quests, etc.), search by name, per-marker "found/not found" tracking. Accessible free tier, optional Premium.

## What problem it solves

Skyrim has 500+ relevant locations for 100% exploration:
- 30+ shouts (each at a specific location).
- 80+ books that grant skill bonuses.
- Quality smithing materials (e.g., Quicksilver Ore) hidden in specific caves.
- Daedric quest triggers (e.g., where to kill the specific noble).
- Treasure maps the default game map doesn't show.
- Aetherium Shards, Dragon Priest masks, Stones of Barenziah.

Memorizing all those locations is impossible. Map Genie is the organized visual database.

## Differentiation

Versus **UESP**: UESP has the info as text but not visualizable. If you want the shout "Whirlwind Sprint", UESP tells you "at High Hrothgar" but not which part. Map Genie shows you the exact pin.

Versus **in-game map**: the game's map only marks locations you've already visited. Map Genie shows everything from the start (with the option to filter found items).

Versus **"iHUD" and "More Informative Console" mods**: these give on-the-fly info but require a modlist. Map Genie is web-based, works for console players too.

## What people use it for

**100% completion runs**: if you want every achievement and finding, Map Genie is the visual ordered checklist.

**Find specific locations**: "where is the Become Ethereal shout" → search → pin on map.

**Track collectible progress**: with a free account, you mark each item as found. Syncs across devices.

**Plan efficient routes**: if you're doing a "collect all Stones of Barenziah" run, the map shows all of them simultaneously and you can plot the optimal route.

**Reference for guides**: when following a guide that says "go to the cave north of X", Map Genie helps you locate X visually.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you like blind exploration (no spoilers, discover everything by chance), Map Genie ruins the experience. It's for completionists.

If you play a single playthrough without aiming for 100%, you don't need Map Genie.

If your modlist adds new cells or drastically changes locations, Map Genie reflects vanilla — it doesn't apply for Enderal, Beyond Skyrim, etc.

## How to use it in practice

1. Open `mapgenie.io/skyrim` in any browser.
2. First load shows the full zoomable map.
3. Sidebar filters: check the categories you care about (Shouts, Dragons, Daedric Quests, etc.). The rest hides.
4. Click a pin for info: name, short description, link to UESP for deep detail.
5. For tracking, create a free account. Each pin has a "found" checkbox — check it when you find the item in-game.
6. Premium ($5/month or $20 lifetime) unlocks advanced features: per-pin notes, more maps, no ads.

## Honest limitations

**Vanilla-only**. Mods that change world layout aren't reflected. For Enderal or Beyond Skyrim, there's no Map Genie equivalent.

**Aggressive subscription model**. Premium pop-ups are frequent. Free tier is functional but limited in features.

**Lack of granularity in some pins**. A large cave can have a single "Daedric quest" pin when there are 5 relevant items inside. For interior detail, UESP complements.

**Limited mobile experience**. The map is zoomable on mobile but the sidebar filters are less comfortable.

**Spoiler-heavy by nature**. If you open the map before playing, you see everything. Hard to "go gradual".

## How to get started

1. Open `mapgenie.io/skyrim`.
2. Without an account, explore: filters and pins work.
3. For tracking, sign up (free).
4. For your first completionist playthrough, recommended filters: Shouts (30) + Stones of Barenziah (24) + Daedric Quests (15). Those alone are 70 hours of gameplay.
5. Every time you find something, mark it. Keeps your progress across sessions.

Map Genie is optional for narrative-focused players, essential for completionists.
