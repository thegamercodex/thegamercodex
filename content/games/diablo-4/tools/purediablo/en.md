## What it is

PureDiablo (purediablo.com/diablo4) is a community-driven Diablo franchise site, active since D2 (2000+) and migrating with each new title. For D4 it offers wiki, per-class guides, interactive map, news + community articles, and a desktop companion via Overwolf. Veteran editorial tone and broad scope.

## What problem it solves

For franchise players (coming from D2/D3) who want a site with historical continuity + current D4 coverage. PureDiablo provides exactly that — broader narrative than D4-only sites.

## How it differs from Fextralife

- **Fextralife**: community-edited MediaWiki, focused on walkthroughs and lore.
- **PureDiablo**: multi-feature community site (wiki + news + guides + map + companion). More veteran-editorial tone.

PureDiablo is more opinionated; Fextralife is more of a neutral encyclopedia.

## What people actually use it for

**Cross-game perspective**: if you played D2/D3, PureDiablo's editorial ties the franchise narrative.

**News + community articles**: unlike Wowhead which is technical, PureDiablo publishes editorial opinions about the game's direction.

**Wiki community lookup**: items, NPCs, lore — alternative to Fextralife.

**Desktop companion**: via Overwolf integration similar to D4Builds Overlay.

**Map utility**: simpler map than MapGenie but accessible.

## Who it's NOT for

- **D4-only players (not franchise)**: veteran editorial doesn't add context.
- **Those wanting technical database queries**: Wowhead is better.
- **Those seeking curated build tier lists**: PureDiablo covers builds but it's not its strength.
- **Mobile-first**: functional UI but not optimized vs Mobalytics/Lexi.gg.

## How it's actually used

1. [purediablo.com/diablo4](https://www.purediablo.com/diablo4/).

2. Top menu: Wiki, Guides, News, Map.

3. For veteran-perspective editorial news, home page.

5. Desktop companion via Overwolf available separately.

## Honest limitations

**Less prioritized coverage vs D4-specific hubs**: big hubs like Maxroll update faster. PureDiablo follows with lag.

**Dated UI**: old site, design reflects D2/D3 era. Functional but not modern.

**Less exhaustive curated builds**: for serious tier list, other sites.

**Editorial bias toward franchise sentiment**: veteran tone may critique D4 vs D2 nostalgically.

**Moderate ads**: monetized via ads.

## How to get started

1. [purediablo.com/diablo4](https://www.purediablo.com/diablo4/).

2. Browse news to understand the editorial voice.

3. If you like veteran-style editorial articles, subscribe to updates.

4. For D4-only use cases (builds, tier lists), Maxroll/Mobalytics first. PureDiablo as community complement.
