## What it is

D4 Planner (d4planner.io) is a web planning tool for D4. Different from Maxroll's planner which lives embedded in their guides, D4 Planner is standalone — its only job is to be a complete visual editor for skill tree, paragon board, gear slots, and aspects.

Features:
- **Visual skill tree**: drag and pick skills exactly like in the game.
- **Interactive paragon board**: rotate boards, place glyphs, see stat totals.
- **Gear input**: stats and aspects per slot.
- **Share via link**: any build is shareable with a permanent URL.
- **Side-by-side comparison**: two builds open at once for exact diff.

Free, no login required to use (login to save permanently).

## What problem it solves

Maxroll and D4builds have embedded planners but limited (no side-by-side comparison, no full paragon board rotation). For users needing maximum precision — serious theorycrafters, hardcore raiders pushing Pit tiers, content creators building guides — D4 Planner provides granular control.

It also solves the "respec cost" problem: in D4, changing paragon allocations costs significant gold at endgame. Free planning in the planner before committing in-game saves a lot.

## How it differs from D4builds.gg

- **D4 Planner**: pure individual planning, no community.
- **D4builds.gg**: community sharing + voting, planner as a medium for sharing.

D4 Planner is the planning tool. D4builds is the discovery platform. Use Planner to create, D4builds to share.

## What people actually use it for

**Paragon optimization**: with high respec costs, plan the entire board in Planner before spending gold.

**Side-by-side comparison**: choosing between 2 Necromancer builds. Open them side-by-side and see differences in skills, paragon, gear.

**Sharing your build with a mentor**: copy the link, send to an experienced player who critiques the planning without Discord image dumping.

**Experimental mocking**: try "what if I swap glyph X for Y" without spending gold. Planner gives instant stat-change feedback.

**Content creator prep**: guide writers use Planner to visualize builds before screenshotting for articles.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Casuals without endgame**: if you play casual campaign, you don't need paragon planning depth.
- **Those seeking community discovery**: this is only a planner. To discover existing builds, D4builds.gg.
- **Those wanting editorial guidance**: D4 Planner doesn't opine. If you want "is it S tier or not", Maxroll/Mobalytics.
- **Mobile-first users**: UI works on mobile but paragon board complexity is hard on small screens.

## How it's actually used

1. [d4planner.io](https://d4planner.io). No login required to create.

2. **New Build** → select class.

3. **Skill Tree** tab: click skills to activate. See total invested.

4. **Paragon Board** tab: start with starter board. Click "Add Board" to add another. Rotation is drag.

5. **Gear & Aspects** tab: input items with stats and rolls.

6. **Save & Share**: get a permalink. Send to your mentor or paste in your Discord.

7. To **compare**: open two builds in separate tabs or use the comparison feature.

## Honest limitations

**No editorial opinion**: the planner doesn't tell you if your build is good. It just shows resulting stats. You need to cross-reference with Maxroll/Mobalytics or your own experience.

**Paragon learning curve**: the paragon board has 5+ boards with rotation, glyphs, rare nodes. For newcomers it's overwhelming. Maxroll guides explain this better.

**Owner-dependent maintenance**: planner maintained by individuals, no professional team. When Blizzard does big patches, there can be lag in updates.

**No account integration**: you can't import your current game gear (D4 doesn't expose API). Manual input only.

**UI can feel rigid**: drag-and-drop workflows in paragon sometimes fight back. Getting used to it takes hours.

## How to get started

1. [d4planner.io](https://d4planner.io). New Build.

2. Select your class. Get familiar with tabs.

3. Build your current build (based on a Maxroll guide). This helps you learn the planner UI.

4. Save and share the link with yourself or a teammate.

5. To mock changes: clone the build, modify, compare stats.

6. Once comfortable, plan paragon fully before spending gold on respec.

7. For complementary depth + editorial opinion, combine with Maxroll/Mobalytics.
