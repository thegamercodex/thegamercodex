## What it is

MapGenie is a cross-game interactive map platform (covers 50+ games including Elden Ring, Hogwarts Legacy, RDR2). Its D4 version (mapgenie.io/diablo-4) covers all of Sanctuary with dungeon entries, waypoints, side quests, Altar of Lilith locations, statues, gathering nodes, and collectibles. Plus a mobile app companion for iOS and Android.

## What problem it solves

D4 in-game has maps but no "what's left to collect" layer. Altars and statues count toward permanent power gains — knowing which you have vs missing, in extensive zones like Hawezar, without external tracking is prohibitive. MapGenie shows exact location + checkable status.

## How it differs from in-game maps + Helltides.com

- **In-game**: limited filters, no progress tracking.
- **Helltides.com**: single-purpose (Helltide timing).
- **MapGenie**: comprehensive map exploration with exhaustive progress tracking and mobile app.

MapGenie dominates when the goal is completionist exploration; Helltides.com dominates when the goal is Helltide farm efficiency.

## What people actually use it for

**Lilith statue hunting**: altars give permanent power. MapGenie marks which you've collected so you don't revisit zones.

**Side quest completion**: for completionist players, see which quests are missing per region.

**Dungeon location lookup**: for aspect codex grinding, MapGenie locates the dungeons that drop specific aspects.

**Mobile reference during gaming**: the mobile app lets you check locations without alt-tab from PC.

**Multi-game players**: if you already use MapGenie on other games (Elden Ring, etc.), the UI is familiar.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Casuals without completion goals**: if you don't farm altars or achievements, MapGenie adds nothing.
- **Those avoiding login**: free tier works without login but persistent progress tracking requires an account.
- **Mobile-only without desktop**: the app works but web is more comfortable for detail.
- **Players who prefer pure in-game maps**: if external tools bother you, it's not for you.

## How it's actually used

1. [mapgenie.io/diablo-4/maps/sanctuary](https://mapgenie.io/diablo-4/maps/sanctuary).

2. Login optional but recommended for persistent tracking.

3. Sidebar filters: mark which POI types you want to see (Lilith statues, altars, dungeons, etc.).

4. Click POIs for detail. Mark them as "Found" when you get them in-game.

5. For mobile: install the official app, sync with your web account.

6. Per-zone progress visible — orients you to which zone has the most missing.

## Honest limitations

**Free tier ads**: there are display ads. Premium tier ($3/mo) removes them plus extra features.

**Manual marking**: doesn't automatically detect what you got in-game (D4 exposes no API). You click "Found" manually.

**Post-patch update lag**: when new zones or secrets land in patches, MapGenie takes days to add them.

**No timer features**: not a replacement for Helltides.com — doesn't track spawn timings.

**Cross-game brand**: the team is split between many games. D4 specifically isn't an absolute priority.

## How to get started

1. [mapgenie.io/diablo-4/maps/sanctuary](https://mapgenie.io/diablo-4/maps/sanctuary), login for tracking.

2. Filters: start with Altar of Lilith filter only — shows you all statues.

3. While playing in-game, when you collect one, click "Found" on the web (or app).

4. Eventually expand filters to Renown, side quests, etc.

5. For mobile: install the iOS/Android app for on-the-go access.

6. If ads bother you, consider Premium tier for a clean experience.
