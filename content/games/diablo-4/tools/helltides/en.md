## What it is

Helltides.com is a single-purpose timer for Diablo IV Helltide events. Helltides are zone events that activate every hour for 55 minutes, rotating between Sanctuary's 5 major zones. During a Helltide, mobs drop Cinders (event-specific currency) and items with higher chance of uniques/legendaries.

The site shows:
- **Current status**: active or not, and in which zone.
- **Time remaining**: countdown to the next rotation.
- **Next zone**: where the next Helltide will be.
- **Rotation schedule**: 24h+ next-rotations table.

Zero login, zero clutter. The home page is the only page that exists.

## What problem it solves

D4 in-game doesn't prominently show you when the next Helltide is or which zone. There's a small indicator but it doesn't contain the rotation schedule. For players who actively farm Helltides (the main source of uniques and mythics post-leveling), knowing exact timing is critical — you don't want to arrive at the zone with 2 minutes remaining.

Helltides.com solves this with maximum simplicity — bookmark, open, see status, close.

## How it differs from Diablo4.life

- **Helltides.com**: single-purpose Helltide only. Hyper-focused.
- **Diablo4.life**: multi-purpose hub with Helltide + world boss + legion events + more utilities.

For Helltides exclusively, Helltides.com is faster and cleaner. For multiple events, Diablo4.life centralizes.

## What people actually use it for

**Pre-session check**: before logging in, open Helltides.com. "Active? Login. 15 min left? Wait next."

**Farm rotation planning**: if you have 2-3 gaming hours, plan when to do Helltide vs when dungeons.

**Avoid wasting commute time**: traveling to a zone takes minutes. If Helltide has 5 min left, not worth it.

**Coordinate with friends**: share the current status with your group to sync up.

**Quick mobile check**: from your phone, check from the bus if it's worth firing up the PS5.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Casuals without endgame**: if you don't farm Helltides, doesn't add anything.
- **Those seeking builds**: not a content tool, it's a timer.
- **Those wanting lots of info**: if you want comprehensive event tracking (Helltide + world boss + Tree of Whispers timings), Diablo4.life is better.
- **Eternal-only players**: Helltides are more relevant in seasonal context. In eternal they're minor utility.

## Honest limitations

**Helltides only**: by design, doesn't cover other events. For world boss timing, another tool.

**Server-time dependency**: rotations are global server-side, but zone availability can have subtle differences between regions. Helltides.com uses your local time but server activity may be timezone-shifted.

**Owner-dependent maintenance**: site by an individual. If the owner leaves, it could disappear. Stable since 2023 so far.

**No native notifications**: tells you time remaining but doesn't alert you when it spawns. For alerts, integrations via Diablo4.life or Discord bots.

**No historical data**: only current + next. For historical patterns, other tools.
