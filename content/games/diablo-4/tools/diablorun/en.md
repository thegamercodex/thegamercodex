## What it is

DiabloRun (diablorun.com) is a community platform for speedrunning tracking and leaderboards for Diablo games, now with primary focus on D4. It allows registering character runs, tracking granular stats, and viewing leaderboards per category.

It covers:
- **Character creation runs**: from-scratch character, tracking XP/hour, kills, deaths.
- **Campaign speedrun**: time to complete main story.
- **Season ladder**: ranking by point/level metrics.
- **Per-class records**: best runs per build/class.
- **Comparative stats**: see community rankings with granular filters.

Free, no login required to read. Login to register runs.

## What problem it solves

D4's in-game competitive leaderboards are limited and don't expose granular detail. For players interested in speedrunning, run optimization, or competing in community leaderboards, DiabloRun centralizes tracking Blizzard doesn't provide directly.

Also useful for self-improvement — see your XP/hour vs your previous run, identify bottlenecks.

## How it differs from Diablo 4 Codex

- **DiabloRun**: focus on performance metrics + leaderboards. How fast / efficient are you?
- **Diablo 4 Codex**: focus on completion metrics. What percentage of codex of power do you have?

DiabloRun is performance racing. Diablo 4 Codex is completion accounting. Different goals.

## What people actually use it for

**Speedrunning community**: tracking community records, world records, personal bests.

**Self-improvement**: see your own run progress, identify where you lose time.

**Comparison with friends**: friends-only leaderboards if your group competes.

**Season ladder**: public ranking per season metrics.

**Deep statistics dive**: granular metrics the game doesn't expose (kills per zone, time per dungeon, etc.).

## Who it's NOT for

- **Casuals without speedrun interest**: if you don't compete, adds nothing.
- **Those seeking builds**: not a content tool.
- **Those avoiding data tracking**: requires continuous run tracking.
- **Players without seasonal endgame**: relevant leaderboards are during active season.

## How it's actually used

1. [diablorun.com](https://diablorun.com).

2. Browse leaderboards without login.

3. For personal tracking: account login + character link.

4. **Submit run** post-completion to register.

5. Filters: by class, season, category (campaign, Pit, etc.).

6. Compare profile vs others.

## Honest limitations

**Niche**: speedrunning community in D4 is smaller vs PoE or D2. Limited coverage.

**Manual submission**: depends on users posting runs. Coverage isn't comprehensive.

**Updates depend on community**: post-season, leaderboards refresh depends on community activity.

**Basic UI**: functional but less polished vs competitive game leaderboards.

**No strict verification**: relies on user honor system for entry validation.

## How to get started

1. [diablorun.com](https://diablorun.com).

2. Browse community leaderboards to see if it interests you.

3. If it appeals: account + character link.

4. Submit your first run post-completion for test.

5. Once set up, future runs are tracked.

6. If you just want casual stats, Diablo 4 Codex may be a better fit. DiabloRun is for speed-competitive specifically.
