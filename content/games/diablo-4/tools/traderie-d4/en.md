## What it is

Traderie (traderie.com/diablo4) is a multi-game P2P trading platform by Akrew Inc. Covers Pokemon, Adopt Me (Roblox), various MMOs and ARPGs including D4. Has auction format (buyer can bid up vs fixed price) plus standard listings.

## What problem it solves

For D4 trading, Diablo.Trade is the dominant marketplace. Traderie serves as alternative — in some cases it has listings Diablo.Trade doesn't, and auctions can surface a better price for patient sellers. For serious flippers, monitor both platforms.

## How it differs from Diablo.Trade

- **Diablo.Trade**: D4-only focus, higher volume, D4-specific optimized UI.
- **Traderie**: multi-game platform, auction feature, smaller D4 community.

For volume and D4-optimized UX: Diablo.Trade. For auctions or cross-game traders: Traderie.

## What people actually use it for

**Alternative listings**: items not appearing on Diablo.Trade may appear here.

**Auctions**: if your listing is high-value specific, auctions surface real market price.

**Cross-game collectors**: if you trade in multiple games, single Traderie account covers all.

**Backup when Diablo.Trade is down**: alternative source.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Those wanting D4-only marketplace**: Diablo.Trade is a better fit.
- **Casuals without trading interest**: irrelevant.
- **Those seeking buyer volume**: D4 community on Traderie is smaller — fewer listings buyers.

## Honest limitations

**Lower D4 volume**: fewer listings and buyers than Diablo.Trade.

**Multi-game brand**: team distributed across many titles. D4 may have less priority.

**Scam risk**: inherent P2P trading. Same precautions as any trading platform.

**Multi-game UI**: optimized for multi-game flexibility, not D4-specific.

**Ad presence**: site monetized with ads.
