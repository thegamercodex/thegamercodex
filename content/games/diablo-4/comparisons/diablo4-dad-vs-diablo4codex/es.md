D4 tiene dos collection trackers prominentes en la community. La elección no es competencia directa porque cubren scopes diferentes.

## Diablo4codex: seasonal-first

Foco principal en seasonal progress y Codex of Power. Trackea: aspects unlocked vs total, Codex of Power completion, achievements del season actual, Seasonal Journey objectives. Strong para players que farmean each season cycle.

## Diablo 4 Dad: world-completion-first

Foco en world collection comprehensive. Trackea: altars of Lilith per region, Renown statues, paragon glyphs collected, mounts, dungeons completed, minor collectibles. Strong para ultra-completionists buscando 100% world coverage.

## Cuándo cada uno

- **Diablo4codex**: enfoque seasonal. Aspect grinding, Codex of Power completion, Journey objectives.
- **Diablo 4 Dad**: enfoque world. Altars, Renown 100%, glyph collection, mounts.

## Flow combinado

Para completionists serios:
1. **Diablo4codex** durante season activa para track aspects y Journey.
2. **Diablo 4 Dad** para world completion gaps que persisten across seasons.

Ambos sync via Blizzard account login. Re-sync periódicamente para keep data fresh.

## Veredicto

No competidores estrictos — scopes complementarios. Diablo4codex para seasonal metrics; Diablo 4 Dad para world collection comprehensive. Si solo te importa una métrica, pická según foco. Si sos completionist serious, ambos.
