D4 has two prominent collection trackers in the community. The choice isn't direct competition because they cover different scopes.

## Diablo4codex: seasonal-first

Main focus on seasonal progress and Codex of Power. Tracks: aspects unlocked vs total, Codex of Power completion, current season achievements, Seasonal Journey objectives. Strong for players farming each season cycle.

## Diablo 4 Dad: world-completion-first

Focus on comprehensive world collection. Tracks: altars of Lilith per region, Renown statues, paragon glyphs collected, mounts, dungeons completed, minor collectibles. Strong for ultra-completionists seeking 100% world coverage.

## When each one

- **Diablo4codex**: seasonal focus. Aspect grinding, Codex of Power completion, Journey objectives.
- **Diablo 4 Dad**: world focus. Altars, Renown 100%, glyph collection, mounts.

## Combined flow

For serious completionists:
1. **Diablo4codex** during active season to track aspects and Journey.
2. **Diablo 4 Dad** for world completion gaps that persist across seasons.

Both sync via Blizzard account login. Re-sync periodically to keep data fresh.

## Verdict

Not strict competitors — complementary scopes. Diablo4codex for seasonal metrics; Diablo 4 Dad for comprehensive world collection. If only one metric matters, pick by focus. If you're a serious completionist, both.
