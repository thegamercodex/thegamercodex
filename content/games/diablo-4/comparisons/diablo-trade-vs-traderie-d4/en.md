For D4 P2P trading there are two serious platforms. The choice isn't really "which is better" but "for what use".

## Diablo.Trade: D4-focused volume

D4-only platform. Listings, search with granular filters (affixes, ilvl, ancients, etc.), buyer pool concentrated in D4. UI optimized for D4-specific decisions. Primary in the space.

## Traderie: multi-game with auctions

Cross-game platform (Pokemon, Adopt Me, MMOs, ARPGs). D4 is one of the titles. Built-in auction format. Lower D4 volume but distinct feature.

## When each one

- **Diablo.Trade**: day-to-day trading. Searching specific items. Selling normal drops. Buyer pool size matters.
- **Traderie**: high-value items where auctions surface real market price. Players who already trade across other games.

## Tradeoffs

**Diablo.Trade**: fixed-price only (no auctions). No cross-game features. But higher volume and D4-optimized.

**Traderie**: lower D4 listings volume. Less D4-specific UI. Auctions add flexibility for unusual items.

## Verdict

Diablo.Trade is the default for D4 trading. Traderie as complementary for specific auctions or if you're already in its ecosystem. For serious flippers, monitor both — listings missing on one may be on the other.
