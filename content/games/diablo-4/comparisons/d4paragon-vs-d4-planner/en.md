For planning in D4 there are options. The choice between D4Paragon and D4 Planner comes down to scope: paragon only, or full build?

## D4 Planner: all-purpose

Covers the full build flow: skill tree, paragon board, gear pieces, aspects. Integrated UI on a single page. Useful when creating a build from scratch or iterating a complete build.

## D4Paragon: paragon specialist

Paragon system only. UI focused exclusively on boards, glyph placement, rare nodes, board rotation. Saves clutter for players who already have skills and gear defined and just want to refine paragon.

## When each one

- **D4 Planner**: build new from scratch, compare full builds, leveling planning.
- **D4Paragon**: paragon optimization of existing build, pre-respec mockup, push-tier paragon refinement.

## Combined flow

1. **D4 Planner** to create or adopt the base build.
2. **D4Paragon** for deep-dive optimization of the paragon section once baseline is set.
3. Implement in-game.

## Verdict

Not strict competitors. Different scope: D4 Planner is general; D4Paragon is specialist. Serious users use both in sequence for different phases of build optimization.

For casuals: D4 Planner is enough. For push-tier players focused on paragon edge cases, D4Paragon adds real value.
