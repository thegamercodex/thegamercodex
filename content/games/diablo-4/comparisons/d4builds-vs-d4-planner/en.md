---
title: "D4builds.gg vs D4 Planner — community sharing or individual planning"
description: "Both are build planners but serve opposite workflows: community discovery vs personal planning."
---

At first glance they look like alternatives — both let you build a D4 setup with skill tree, paragon, and gear. In reality they target different points in the build flow: D4builds.gg is for discovery and sharing, D4 Planner is for planning.

## D4builds.gg: community first

D4builds.gg is a community-sharing platform. Any user publishes a build with full skill tree, paragon, gear, aspects. Others vote. Popular builds rise in ranking. Filters by class, season, popularity, and a Fork system (copy an existing build and modify).

The value is in diversity and validation. Per class you have dozens of builds available, some top tier, others experimental. If you post a build idea, the community votes and comments — immediate feedback on whether it works.

## D4 Planner: individual workflow

D4 Planner is laser-focused on personal planning. A single dense page to configure skill tree, paragon (with full board rotation), gear, and aspects. Side-by-side comparison between two builds. Permanent link sharing is optional but not central — culture is "for me, optionally for others".

The value is precision and comparison. To mock paragon allocation before spending gold on respec, or compare variants of your build without publishing, D4 Planner is optimal.

## How the use case decides

**Looking to discover builds?** D4builds.gg. Browse, find top-voted, evaluate. D4 Planner has no catalog.

**Want to modify an existing build?** D4builds.gg with Fork. Copy + modify.

**Want to compare two builds side-by-side?** D4 Planner. Built-in feature.

**Want iterative private planning?** D4 Planner. No community pressure.

**Want community to vote your idea?** D4builds.gg. Sharing is core.

## The natural stack

Many players use both in sequence:

1. **D4builds.gg**: discover top builds or your inspiration.
2. **Import to D4 Planner**: for private edits before publish (or never publish).
3. **Side-by-side** between your variant and the canonical.
4. **If you perfect it**, optionally post back to D4builds.gg for community feedback.

## Limitations

**D4builds.gg**: variable signal-to-noise (outdated builds, voted up by bots, etc.). No editorial filter — everything is publishable. Voting can be gamed.

**D4 Planner**: no community, no discovery. Denser UI than D4builds.gg. Paragon board rotation takes a learning curve.

## Verdict

Not real competitors — tools covering different points of the flow. **D4builds.gg** is the portal to the community + discovery. **D4 Planner** is the personal editor with precision. If you only want one: D4builds.gg for discovery, D4 Planner to create. But ideally both.

If you play casually, D4builds.gg covers most — Maxroll's planner + D4builds discovery supply what D4 Planner adds. If you craft custom builds seriously, D4 Planner becomes indispensable.
