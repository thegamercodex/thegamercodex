title: "Mobalytics D4 vs Icy Veins D4 — modern visual or encyclopedia prose"
description: "Two guide hubs for D4 with opposite formats. Mobalytics is visual speed; Icy Veins is prose depth."
---

Both sit between Maxroll (depth + planner) and D4builds.gg (community + discovery). The concrete clash between Mobalytics and Icy Veins is **format**: cards vs prose.

## Mobalytics: visual cards

Mobalytics presents build guides as compact visual cards with tier badges, skill icons, and stats summary in a scannable grid. Tier lists are grids with classes ordered by S/A/B/C. Minimum reading time — the design asks you to scan, not read.

Good for players who already know D4 and just need a refresh of meta state. Their brand strength is modern UI inherited from LoL work.

## Icy Veins: encyclopedia prose

Icy Veins is text-first. Each class guide is ~5000 words of prose with structured sections: Skills, Stats Priority, Rotation, Itemization. No embedded videos, no distractions. It's an encyclopedia.

Good for learners who want to understand the why via reading, not via cards. Familiar brand for players migrating from WoW (where Icy Veins is default).

## When Mobalytics

Visual learners. Quick scans. Mobile usage. Players coming to D4 from MOBAs (LoL).

## When Icy Veins

Players who prefer reading. Long-form depth. Familiarity for WoW veteran players. Offline workflows (text is printable).

## Update cadence

Mobalytics post-patch tends to be ready in 24-48h. Icy Veins 3-5 days — their revision process is slower because they have to rewrite prose vs swap card badges.

## Limitations

Mobalytics: simplifies decisions to cards that can hide nuance. Heavy ads. Smaller coverage than Maxroll on advanced builds.

Icy Veins: austere UI. Zero interactive features. Moderate ads. Slower updates.

## Verdict

For casual scan: Mobalytics. For depth learning via reading: Icy Veins. Neither is objectively superior — the choice depends on your format consumption preference.

If you play casually and mobile, Mobalytics alone. If you come from WoW reading culture and want text-based depth, Icy Veins alone. For serious endgame, both are alternatives to the core Maxroll + Wowhead stack.
