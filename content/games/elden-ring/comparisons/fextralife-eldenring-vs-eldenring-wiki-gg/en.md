The community's oldest wiki question: the most complete and ad-heavy, or the clean but incomplete? **Fextralife** has 10+ years of content with community comments. **wiki.gg** has clean ad-free UI but ~80% coverage. Both are valid for different purposes.

## Coverage

Fextralife has ~100% coverage of base game + SOTE: every item, boss, NPC, location with a dedicated page. The community has accumulated 10+ years of content (including legacy from Souls before ER).

wiki.gg was created in 2023 and coverage is catching up: ~80% of base game, notable gaps in SOTE longtail. For common item lookups, it's enough; for obscure items, not yet.

## Ads and UX

Fextralife is ad-supported with aggressive advertising: autoplay video, popups, moving banners. For extended reading it's frustrating. Mobile is worse — ads cover half the screen.

wiki.gg is donation-supported, fully ad-free. Clean UI, legible mobile, reading-focused. For long lore pages, the difference is notable.

## Community comments

Fextralife has a comments section on every page where the community leaves insights, workarounds, undocumented tips. For confusing items or quests with obscure branches, comments hold the "actual" walkthrough.

wiki.gg doesn't implement comments — strictly factual content. Gain: no noise. Loss: no community wisdom.

## Update cycle

Fextralife has paid editors who update post-patch within days. wiki.gg depends on volunteer editors and the cycle is slower (weeks).

## When each wins

| Scenario | Best option |
|---|---|
| Rare item with community insights | Fextralife |
| Extended reading without distractions | wiki.gg |
| Complete DLC coverage | Fextralife |
| Mobile while playing | wiki.gg |
| NPC quest walkthrough with branches | Fextralife |
| Immediate post-patch update | Fextralife |
| Supporting community-driven alternative | wiki.gg |

## Verdict

**Use Fextralife as primary** if you need complete coverage and accept ads. **Use wiki.gg as secondary** for extended reading and mobile. The ideal workflow: Google search → Fextralife appears first → open → if saturated with ads, copy the slug and open it on wiki.gg. Both are valid; complementary, not substitutes.
