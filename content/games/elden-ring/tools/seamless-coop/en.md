## What it is

ELDEN RING Seamless Co-op is a free, open-source mod developed by LukeYui (with contributions from the Seamless team) and distributed via Nexus Mods + GitHub. It enables up to 6 players in a persistent world, with shared progress and without vanilla co-op barriers.

Released in March 2023, it's the most-downloaded Elden Ring mod (~3M downloads) and practically the reason many players started installing mods on the game.

## What it solves

Vanilla Elden Ring co-op has limitations designed to preserve single-player difficulty:
- You summon a Phantom and they leave when the area boss dies.
- Phantoms can't ride Torrent.
- The open world has invisible fog walls that limit distance.
- Invasions can kill your session.
- Quest and map progress isn't shared.

Seamless Co-op removes all of that. You and up to 5 friends share a world: kill bosses together, explore without barriers, ride horses, no invasions (unless you toggle them on), and progress persists between sessions.

## What people use it for

**Full run with a friend**: start Elden Ring from scratch together, without Phantom mechanics cutting sessions.

**4-6 friend co-op**: up to 6 players in the same world, adjustable difficulty scaling via settings.

**Open world exploration**: ride Torrent in a group, discover areas together with no restrictions.

**Boss rushing with friends**: re-fight specific bosses multiple times to farm or practice.

**SOTE in co-op**: the mod supports the DLC; you can run Shadow of the Erdtree fully grouped.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you play on console (PS5/Xbox), the mod doesn't exist — Seamless Co-op is PC-exclusive.

If you want to keep your character compatible with vanilla online, you can't — the mod forces offline mode (anti-cheat).

If your plan is PvP/invasions, Seamless Co-op doesn't apply — the mod disables invasions by default.

If the "Souls intended" co-op idea (ephemeral Phantoms, preserved difficulty) appeals to you, don't touch the mod.

## How it's used in practice

1. Install Mod Engine 2 first (Seamless Co-op uses it as a base).
2. Download Seamless Co-op from `nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/510` or `github.com/LukeYui/EldenRingSeamlessCoopRelease`.
3. Extract files following the README — includes a dedicated launcher.
4. Define a shared password for your group in `seamlesscoopsettings.ini`.
5. Launch the game with `launch_elden_ring_seamlesscoop.exe`.
6. You and your friends use the same password — connect automatically when in the same area.

## Honest limitations

**Version-locked to the game patch**: when Bandai patches ER, Seamless takes 1-7 days to update. During that window, you can't play.

**Offline only**: your Tarnished becomes "tainted" (community terminology) — you can't go back to vanilla online with that character. Workaround: keep a separate save for mod vs online.

**Controversial difficulty scaling**: the mod scales boss HP for groups, but feel is that some fights become trivial and others stay frustrating. Tweakable via settings but requires experimentation.

**Basic netcode**: lag and desyncs happen especially with 5-6 players. For 2-3 people the experience is stable; for 6 it fluctuates.

**Quest-specific bugs**: some NPC quests (Ranni, Millicent) have quirks in co-op that may need workarounds.

## How to start

1. Make sure you have Mod Engine 2 installed first.
2. Visit `nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/510` and download the latest version.
3. Extract files following the README.
4. Edit `seamlesscoopsettings.ini` with a unique password for your group.
5. Coordinate with friends to use the exact same mod version.
6. Launch with the Seamless executable and start playing.
