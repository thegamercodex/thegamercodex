## What it is

Maxroll is a free, ad-supported web hub and the de-facto English-language reference for Lost Ark builds, raids, and systems. It hosts full class build guides covering engravings, gems, tripods, and stat priority; raid mechanics guides for Legion, Abyssal, and Guardian raids as well as the newer Tier 4 "Kazeros" raids; tier lists; and its own interactive planner plus an upgrade (honing) calculator.

The site is maintained by the Maxroll team, the same brand behind well-regarded resources for Diablo 4, Path of Exile, PoE 2, and Last Epoch. Content is updated every patch and currently tracks the Tier 4 / T4.5 state of the game (2026).

## What problem it solves

Lost Ark is a systems-heavy game: engravings, tripods, gems, stat thresholds, gear honing, and a raid calendar that shifts every patch. Scattering that across forum posts and Discord pins makes it hard to play any class correctly, let alone optimally.

Maxroll consolidates the answers into one authoritative, patch-current place. Instead of guessing which engravings to run or how a raid gate works, you get a maintained, opinionated guide written by people who follow the meta closely.

## Differentiation

The obvious comparison is **Lost Ark Nexus**, the other major English guide site. Maxroll is the most comprehensive and highest-authority source, with deeper systems-level coverage and more detailed theorycraft — but that depth makes its pages denser and slower to read.

Nexus trades some of that depth for a cleaner, lighter, more mobile-friendly layout. If you want the most complete answer and don't mind reading, Maxroll wins; if you want a fast skim, Nexus is the lighter option. Many players keep both open.

## What people use it for

- **Class build guides:** Looking up the correct engravings, gems, tripods, and stat priority for a main or an alt.
- **Raid preparation:** Reading mechanics guides before pugging or progging a Legion, Abyssal, Guardian, or Tier 4 Kazeros raid.
- **Tier lists:** Checking where a class sits in the current meta before committing to a roster slot.
- **Planning upgrades:** Using the interactive planner and honing calculator to map a gear progression path.
- **Patch catch-up:** Returning after a break and seeing what changed in the current Tier 4 / T4.5 state.

## Who this tool is NOT for

This is not the tool for someone who wants a single quick answer without reading a thorough guide. Maxroll's pages are detailed by design; if you just want a one-line "what engraving do I press," the density can feel like overkill. In that case a lighter site like Nexus, or a quick Discord question, may serve you better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open the Lost Ark section of Maxroll and find your class or the raid you're prepping.
2. For builds, read the engravings, tripods, gems, and stat priority sections, then mirror them onto your character.
3. For raids, read the mechanics guide for the specific raid and gate before you queue.
4. Cross-check the current tier list if you're deciding which alt to push.
5. Use the interactive planner and honing calculator to budget your next gear upgrade.
6. Re-check the guide after each patch, since Maxroll updates content as the meta shifts.

## Honest limitations

Maxroll is free but ad-supported, so expect ads on the page. Its depth is a double-edged sword: thorough guides can feel overwhelming if you only need a quick pointer, and the volume of information takes time to parse. As an English-first resource it tracks the Western (NA/EU) version of the game, so timing and availability can differ from KR. And like any meta resource, guidance lags slightly right after a patch until the team updates it.

## How to get started

Go to the Maxroll website and open its Lost Ark section — no account or download is required. Pick your class to read its build guide, or jump straight to a raid guide if you're prepping mechanics. From there, explore the tier lists, planner, and honing calculator. Bookmark the pages for the classes you play, and revisit after each patch since content is kept current with the live game.
