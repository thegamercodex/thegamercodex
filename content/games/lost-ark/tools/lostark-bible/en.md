## What it is

lostark.bible is a free, community-built armory and character-profile lookup for Lost Ark, recently rebranded from uwuowo.mathi.moe. You type any NA/EU character name and it pulls up that character's full profile: their entire roster, gear, gems, engravings, cards, stats, and gear history.

It reads the same public character data the official in-game armory exposes, just presented in a faster, more readable web interface. It's maintained by Mathi and is free to use, with no account required.

## What problem it solves

The in-game armory and the official web armory are functional but slow and awkward to navigate, especially when you want to scan a whole roster or compare details quickly. Vetting a potential party member or benchmarking your own progress against a top player means a lot of clicking.

lostark.bible streamlines that into a single search. Type a name, get the complete picture — roster depth, gear, gems, engravings — in a layout built for quick reading. It turns a tedious lookup into a few seconds.

## Differentiation

The comparison is **meta-game.gg's armory**. Both read public character data, but lostark.bible is the beloved, roster-deep community lookup — its strength is fast, thorough character inspection and a roster view players trust.

meta-game.gg differentiates by bundling a built-in honing calculator alongside its armory, so it leans toward "inspect plus plan upgrades in one place." If pure lookup quality and roster depth matter most, lostark.bible is the favorite; if you want an armory with a planning tool attached, meta-game.gg is the alternative.

## What people use it for

- **Benchmarking progress:** Comparing your own gear, gems, and engravings against players at a similar or higher level.
- **Vetting party members:** Checking a potential raid mate's setup and roster before inviting them.
- **Copying setups:** Inspecting a top player's gems, engravings, and stat distribution to mirror onto your own character.
- **Roster overview:** Scanning a player's full roster of alts at a glance.
- **Tracking history:** Reviewing a character's gear history to see how they progressed.

## Who this tool is NOT for

This is not the tool for someone who wants to plan a build from scratch. lostark.bible inspects characters that already exist; it doesn't help you design engraving combinations or theorycraft a new setup. For that, you want a build planner or a guide resource, not a character lookup.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open lostark.bible and type the NA/EU character name you want to inspect.
2. Read the profile: gear, gems, engravings, cards, and stats.
3. Switch to the roster view to see the player's full set of characters.
4. Compare those details against your own to benchmark or copy a setup.
5. Check gear history if you want to see how the character progressed over time.
6. Repeat for any party members you want to vet before a raid.

## Honest limitations

lostark.bible only shows public character data, so it's bound by whatever the game exposes — it can't reveal anything a character has set to private, and data refreshes on the game's schedule, not in real time. Because it reads existing characters, it does nothing for planning your own build. As a community project it depends on Mathi's upkeep, and the recent rebrand from uwuowo means older links and bookmarks may need updating.

## How to get started

Go to lostark.bible — no account or download needed. Type the NA/EU character name you want to look up and open their profile. From there, browse their gear, gems, engravings, and roster, and switch to the roster view for the full picture. Bookmark the new domain, and use it to vet party members or benchmark your own characters whenever you need a quick, thorough lookup.
