## What it is

meta-game.gg is a free, Western character and armory lookup for Lost Ark. You can inspect a character's profile — gear, engravings, roster, and more — much like any other armory tool. Its differentiator is that it also bundles a built-in honing calculator (at /honing-calculator), so inspection and upgrade planning live under one roof.

It reads public character data and is free to use, with no download required.

## What problem it solves

When you're improving a character, the workflow usually splits across two tools: one to inspect the current state of your gear and roster, and a separate honing calculator to plan the next upgrade. Bouncing between tabs and re-entering numbers adds friction.

meta-game.gg collapses that into one site. You can look up a character to see where it stands, then jump straight to the honing calculator to figure out the materials, gold, and odds for the next step — without leaving for a second tool.

## Differentiation

The comparison is **lostark.bible**. meta-game.gg's draw is the combination: armory lookup plus honing calculator in one place, aimed at players who want to inspect and plan together.

lostark.bible, by contrast, is the more beloved, roster-focused pure lookup — the community's go-to when character inspection itself is the priority. So the split is clear: if you want depth and polish on the lookup alone, lostark.bible leads; if the appeal is having a honing calculator attached to the armory, meta-game.gg is the convenient all-in-one.

## What people use it for

- **Character inspection:** Looking up a profile's gear, engravings, and roster.
- **Honing planning:** Using the built-in honing calculator at /honing-calculator to budget an upgrade.
- **One-tab workflow:** Inspecting current state and planning the next step without switching tools.
- **Upgrade math:** Estimating materials, gold, and success odds for a honing attempt.
- **Quick reference:** Pulling a character's stats while planning progression.

## Who this tool is NOT for

This is not the tool for someone who only wants character inspection and already uses lostark.bible. If you don't need the honing calculator and you're happy with a dedicated lookup, the bundling adds little, and lostark.bible's more beloved, roster-deep experience will likely suit you better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open meta-game.gg and search the character you want to inspect.
2. Review the profile: gear, engravings, and roster.
3. When you're ready to plan an upgrade, open the honing calculator at /honing-calculator.
4. Enter your current gear level and target to estimate materials, gold, and odds.
5. Use those numbers to decide your next honing attempt.
6. Return to the lookup to re-check progress after upgrading.

## Honest limitations

meta-game.gg reads public character data, so it's bound by what the game exposes and refreshes on the game's schedule, not in real time. As a pure lookup it's generally seen as less polished and less roster-deep than lostark.bible, so its main argument is the bundled honing calculator rather than best-in-class inspection. And like any unofficial tool, it depends on continued maintenance to stay current with patches.

## How to get started

Go to meta-game.gg — no account or install needed. Search a character to see their gear, engravings, and roster, then head to /honing-calculator when you want to plan an upgrade. Enter your current and target gear levels to get the material, gold, and odds estimates. Bookmark it if the inspect-plus-plan workflow in one tab fits how you play.
