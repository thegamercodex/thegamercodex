## What it is

The Maxroll Upgrade Calculator is a free, multi-item honing cost simulator for Lost Ark. Honing is the game's core gear progression system — and a luck-based one, where each upgrade attempt can succeed or fail. This calculator lets you input your current and target item levels across all gear slots and estimates the materials, gold, and silver required to get there.

It accounts for the details that matter: honing research bonuses, artisan's energy (the pity system that guarantees a success after enough failures), and your fail history. It's maintained by the Maxroll team and is current up to T4.5 (updated April 2026).

## What problem it solves

Honing is Lost Ark's biggest pain point. Because each tap is a gamble, players routinely sink materials and gold into upgrades without knowing the real cost of reaching their target, and either overspend or run dry mid-path.

The calculator converts that uncertainty into a concrete budget. Instead of taping blind and hoping, you see roughly what an upgrade path will cost in materials, gold, and silver — so you can decide what's affordable, what to prioritize, and whether to wait for an event before committing.

## Differentiation

The obvious comparison is **meta-game.gg's honing calculator**. The key difference is scope and integration. Maxroll's tool is a multi-slot, full-roster planner built into the broader Maxroll ecosystem, so it fits alongside the site's build guides and planner. meta-game's calculator is a standalone tool bundled into an armory lookup, oriented around a single character pulled from your account.

If you're planning a complete upgrade path across all your gear slots — or across multiple characters — Maxroll's multi-item approach is the better fit. If you specifically want a calculator tied to an armory import, meta-game's bundling may suit you.

## What people use it for

- **Budgeting an upgrade path:** Mapping the full material, gold, and silver cost of going from current to target item level.
- **Prioritizing slots:** Deciding which gear pieces to hone first when resources are limited.
- **Accounting for pity:** Factoring in artisan's energy and fail history so estimates reflect reality, not best case.
- **Event planning:** Checking whether to hone now or wait for an honing buff or material event.
- **Roster-wide planning:** Estimating costs across alts before committing materials.

## Who this tool is NOT for

This is not the tool for someone who just wants to hone one piece on a whim without planning a whole upgrade path. If you're going to tap a single item and don't care about the broader budget, a quick standalone calculator is enough and the multi-slot planning is overhead you won't use.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open the Upgrade Calculator on Maxroll.
2. Enter your current item level for each relevant gear slot.
3. Set the target item level you're aiming for.
4. Apply your honing research bonuses and your accumulated artisan's energy / fail history so the estimate is accurate.
5. Read the estimated materials, gold, and silver totals.
6. Use the numbers to decide what to hone first, what to defer, and whether to wait for an event.

## Honest limitations

Honing is probabilistic, so the calculator gives an estimate, not a guarantee — your actual run can land above or below the projection depending on luck. The accuracy depends on entering your bonuses, pity, and fail history correctly; garbage in, garbage out. It tracks the Western version up to T4.5 (April 2026), so a future patch can shift costs until it's updated. And like the rest of Maxroll, the surrounding site is ad-supported.

## How to get started

Go to Maxroll and open the Upgrade Calculator — it's free, with no account or download needed. Enter your current and target item levels for each gear slot, then add your honing research bonuses and artisan's energy so the estimate reflects your actual situation. Read off the projected materials, gold, and silver, and use that budget to plan your next upgrade. Re-run it whenever your gear or the patch changes.
