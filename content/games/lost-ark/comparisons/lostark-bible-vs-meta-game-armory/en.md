Lost Ark exposes character data publicly through its armory, and third-party tools make it far nicer to read. You use one to benchmark your own progress, vet a party member before a raid, or copy a top player's setup. The two main options are **lostark.bible** (the tool formerly known as uwuowo, by Mathi) and the **meta-game.gg** armory — and they differ on one main axis: roster depth versus bundled tooling.

## Roster depth vs bundled tooling

lostark.bible is the community favorite for character inspection. Type any NA/EU name and it lays out the full roster, gear, gems, engravings, cards, and stats in a clean, readable form. That clarity is why it's the go-to for vetting party members or studying a reference build: everything you want is legible at a glance. It reads the same public data the official armory does — no special access, just a better presentation.

meta-game.gg makes a different pitch. Its armory is solid, but its real draw is that it bundles a honing calculator alongside the lookup, so you can inspect a character and then plan their next item-level push without leaving the site. If you want both jobs in one tab, that integration is the reason to choose it.

## When each one wins

| Situation | Winner |
|---|---|
| Inspect a character's roster, gear, and gems by name | lostark.bible |
| Review a character and plan honing in one place | meta-game.gg |
| Vet a party member before a raid | lostark.bible |
| Estimate the cost of the next item-level push | meta-game.gg |
| Copy a top player's setup for your class | lostark.bible |

## The verdict

Use **lostark.bible** for the cleanest, deepest roster and character inspection — it's the community favorite for good reason, and the faster read when all you want is to see what someone's running. Use **meta-game.gg** when you value having the armory and a honing calculator in the same tab. Note that lostark.bible recently rebranded from uwuowo.mathi.moe, so older bookmarks may point at the old domain. For pure lookup, bible; for lookup-plus-planning convenience, meta-game.gg.
