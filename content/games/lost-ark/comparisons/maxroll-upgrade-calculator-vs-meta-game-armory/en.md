Honing — Lost Ark's luck-based gear upgrade system — is the game's biggest gold and material sink, and going in blind is how players burn a week's income for nothing. Two tools turn it into a plan: the **Maxroll Upgrade Calculator** and the honing calculator built into **meta-game.gg**. They solve the same math from opposite starting points: a full-gear plan versus a quick calc from your live profile.

## Full-gear planning vs quick calc from your profile

The Maxroll Upgrade Calculator is the multi-slot planner. You set current and target item levels across every gear piece, and it models the materials, gold, and silver you'll need, accounting for honing research bonuses and artisan's energy (the pity system). Because it lives inside the Maxroll ecosystem, you can jump from the calc straight to a build or raid guide without switching sites. It's the tool for budgeting a whole upgrade path, not a single tap.

meta-game.gg's calculator starts from your character's armory. Pull up your profile and it works from your real numbers, which makes it the more direct option when you just want a quick estimate for where you actually are — no manual setup of every slot.

## When each one wins

| Situation | Winner |
|---|---|
| Plan honing cost across every slot to a target ilvl | Maxroll Upgrade Calculator |
| Calculate from your character's real profile | meta-game.gg |
| Estimate materials and gold for a week of honing | Maxroll Upgrade Calculator |
| Run a quick calc without building a full plan | meta-game.gg |
| Integrate the calc with guides and tier lists | Maxroll Upgrade Calculator |

## The verdict

Use the **Maxroll Upgrade Calculator** when you're budgeting a full upgrade path and want the detail and ecosystem integration — it's the planner for thinking a week or a milestone ahead. Use **meta-game.gg** when you'd rather start from your actual character and get a fast number. Many players do both: meta-game.gg for the quick "what's this tap cost" check, Maxroll when they sit down to plan the whole climb.
