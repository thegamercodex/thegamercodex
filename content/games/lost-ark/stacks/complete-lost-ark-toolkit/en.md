## What this stack solves

Lost Ark is an MMO that hides almost all of its information outside the game. Raids have mechanics the client never explains and that you have to know in advance or you cause a wipe. Honing is luck-based progression: every attempt can fail, so pushing forward without budgeting materials and gold is the recipe for getting stuck. On top of that sits a mountain of horizontal content (mokokos, islands, collectibles) that grants permanent power but is scattered across the whole map, plus a roster of six or more characters that shares resources and rotates daily and weekly tasks.

No game demands external tools quite like this one. The stack is built for the player who's already in, picked a class, and wants to stop improvising: a clear flow that runs from deciding which build to play to not forgetting your sixth alt's dailies.

## The flow

It all starts at **Maxroll**. Here you pick your class and build, and — more importantly — you learn each raid's mechanics before you set foot in the instance. It's the brain of the stack: it defines what you want to build and where you're headed.

With the build decided, you take your item level target to **Maxroll Upgrade Calculator**. It turns honing into a concrete number: how many materials, how much gold, and how much silver you need to get there. What was a goal in Maxroll becomes a budget here.

While you fine-tune the setup, **Lost Ark Codex** is your quick reference: exact item stats, the details of each skill, and a skill builder to lock in your configuration without guessing. It's the spot lookup that complements Maxroll's bigger-picture planning.

With your character rolling, you open **Lost Ark Map** to attack the horizontal content. Its interactive map lets you track and mark mokokos, islands, and collectibles, so that permanent power stops being scattered chaos and becomes a list you check off.

To know whether you're on the right track, **lostark.bible** works as a progress mirror. You inspect your own roster in the armory and top players', and compare: what gear you're missing, what engravings the best run, where to aim next.

And so none of this slips through the cracks day to day, **Lost Ark Helper** closes the loop with a daily, weekly, and Una's task checklist for your whole roster. It's what keeps six characters in order without a reset getting away from you.

## Why these six

Each tool covers a distinct layer of the game, and together they leave no gaps:

- **Planning** — **Maxroll**: class, build, and raid mechanics.
- **Honing budget** — **Maxroll Upgrade Calculator**: materials, gold, and silver to your target item level.
- **Reference** — **Lost Ark Codex**: item stats, skill details, and a skill builder.
- **Horizontal content** — **Lost Ark Map**: a map to track mokokos, islands, and collectibles.
- **Benchmarking** — **lostark.bible**: an armory to inspect your roster and compare it against the top.
- **Roster logistics** — **Lost Ark Helper**: dailies, weeklies, and Una's tasks across all your characters.

## The whole set

None of these tools replaces another: one plans, one budgets, one references, one tracks, one compares, and one organizes. Used together they cover the game end to end, from picking a build to closing out the weekly reset without forgetting a thing.

Each one has its full analysis in the codex, with its profile, its strengths, and its limits.
