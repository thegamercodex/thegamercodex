If you play Wuthering Waves and you're looking for where to read up on how to build your Resonators, sooner or later you'll land on these two sites. Prydwen is a multi-gacha fan site that covers WuWa with an almost obsessive level of detail: per-character ratings, Echo sets with their Sonata effects, main stats per slot, substat priority, and reasoned team comps. Mobalytics is a multi-game platform that arrived at WuWa with more of an editorial blog format, built so a new player understands the why behind each decision without feeling like they're reading a spreadsheet. They don't compete for the same reader: they compete for the same moment of doubt, but they resolve it differently.

## Depth and editorial judgment

Prydwen lives on depth. Each Resonator page breaks down which Echoes to equip, which Sonata sets to prioritize, which main stat goes in each slot, and the order in which substats matter. Its ratings aren't a lonely letter grade: they come with reasoning about why a character rises or falls depending on the content. That makes it the most reliable source once you already know the basics and need to make an expensive decision — for example, whether it's worth investing limited resources in a new Resonator.

Mobalytics plays a different game on purpose. Its WuWa coverage explains concepts before throwing numbers at you: what a hypercarry team is, what quick-swap means, why a support Resonator changes the plan. It's less exhaustive and sometimes a step behind on coverage of brand-new characters, but the player still learning the mechanic walks away understanding, not just copying.

## Audience and entry curve

The most honest difference between the two is who they serve best. Prydwen assumes you already handle endgame vocabulary: Tower of Adversity, Sonata effects, crit value, Resonance Chains. If you show up without that context, its density can overwhelm. In exchange, once you know how to read it, there's no more complete source per Resonator.

Mobalytics is built for the entrance. Its reroll and basic team-building guides are ideal for an account's first days, when you still don't know which Convene to chase or how to put together a decent team. It's the page you hand to someone who just started, not the one you open to optimize the last 5% of damage.

## Coverage speed and format

Prydwen tends to update its tier lists and build pages shortly after each patch, and it keeps the segmentation by endgame content instead of a single flat list. That matters: a Resonator can be mediocre in exploration and excellent in Tower of Adversity, and Prydwen reflects that nuance. The format is a navigable database, with a clean but dense UI.

Mobalytics prioritizes the article format: fewer tables, more explanatory prose. That makes it slower for pinpoint lookups ("which substats go on this character?") but more comfortable for a zero-to-one read. If what you want is to quickly scan an exact data point, Prydwen wins; if you want to understand the whole framework, Mobalytics flows better.

## When each one wins

| Case | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Decide whether a new Resonator is worth investing in | Prydwen | Detailed per-content ratings with the reasoning behind each grade. |
| Understand basic team building (hypercarry vs quick-swap) from scratch | Mobalytics | Explanatory editorial format aimed at players just learning the mechanic. |
| See exact Echo sets, main stats, and target substats | Prydwen | Pages detail Sonata effects, main stats per slot, and substat priority. |
| Quick reroll guide when starting an account | Mobalytics | Curated, direct reroll guide for your first pulls. |
| Compare the ceiling of several Resonators for Tower of Adversity | Prydwen | Tier list segmented by endgame content, not a single flat list. |

## Verdict

This isn't a case of picking one and discarding the other: it's a question of order. Start with Mobalytics if the mechanics still feel new to you — its reroll and team-building guides give you the framework without drowning you — and graduate to Prydwen when you need the fine detail: per-content ratings, exact Echo sets, and substat priority to squeeze every Resonator. For a player who already has hours in, Prydwen is the daily tool; Mobalytics is the best on-ramp so a new friend doesn't get lost in week one.
