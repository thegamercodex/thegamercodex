Wuthering Waves is a huge open world packed with collectibles, and clearing a region to 100% without an interactive map is a thankless job. Qiqi's Notebook and Appsample solve the same base problem —knowing where things are and what you've already grabbed— but they target different moments in the process. One is obsessed with tidy tracking and a clean interface; the other bets on community intelligence when a marker isn't enough. Knowing which to open depends on whether your friction of the day is "don't lose count" or "I can't find this specific collectible."

## Clean tracking vs social layer

Qiqi's Notebook is built for the methodical completionist. Its strength is the persistent checklist: you mark what you've already collected, region by region, and progress is saved so you know exactly what's left. The interface is clean and fast, no noise, ideal for daily use where you open the map, clear an area and close. When your goal is taking a new region to 100% in an orderly way, that notebook-style format is exactly what you need.

Appsample tackles the other problem: the marker that isn't enough. Some collectibles are hidden behind puzzles, inside caves or in positions that a dot on the map doesn't fully explain. Appsample adds a social layer —comments and photos uploaded by other players— that gives you the visual hint to crack that confusing case. In exchange, it's ad-supported and the interface is heavier than Qiqi's Notebook's, which makes it less comfortable for the daily sweep but invaluable when you're stuck on a specific marker.

## When each one wins

| Case | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tracking collectible progress with a persistent checklist | Qiqi's Notebook | Per-region checklist with saved progress, built for completion |
| Cracking a hidden collectible with photos from other players | Appsample | Social layer with community photos and comments |
| Clearing a new region to 100% in an orderly way | Qiqi's Notebook | Region-by-region notebook format, tidy and persistent |
| Confirming the exact location of an ambiguous marker | Appsample | User visual hints that clarify where it actually is |
| Fast, distraction-free interface for daily use | Qiqi's Notebook | Clean UI, no ads, fast to open and close |

The trade-off is clarity against context: Qiqi's Notebook wins when speed and tidy tracking are what matter, and Appsample wins when a single collectible has you going in circles and you need the community's knowledge. Many players use Qiqi's Notebook as their main day-to-day map and open Appsample only when a marker resists.
