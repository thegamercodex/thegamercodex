## What it is

WuWaFlex (wuwaflex.com) is a build showcase platform for Wuthering Waves. Its core purpose is generating flex cards: you enter or import a Resonator's build and the tool produces a visual card ready to share. Beyond creating your own, you can browse builds that other players have published.

Unlike a full optimization workbench, WuWaFlex is lighter and focused on sharing and showing off builds, not on deep OCR scanning or detailed per-Echo scoring. It's the tool you reach for once you have a finished build and just want to present it well.

## What problem it solves

When you finish building a Resonator in Wuthering Waves, communicating that build to someone else is awkward: describing 5 Echoes with their main stats, substats, and Sonata effects in plain text is tedious and hard to read. In-game screenshots aren't ideal either, since they show one screen at a time and don't summarize the whole build at a glance.

WuWaFlex solves exactly that: it turns your build into a tidy, shareable visual card. Instead of pasting a wall of text or several screenshots, you send a single image showing the Resonator, its weapon, its Echoes, and its key stats. It also solves the discovery side: if you want quick inspiration, you browse other players' builds without having to reconstruct them yourself.

## Differentiation

The directory's three Echo tools (WuWaBuilds, WuWaFlex, WaveMate) all touch builds and Echoes, but you don't use them the same way:

- **WuWaFlex** is the showcase option: you enter or import a build and generate a clean flex card, plus browse other people's. Its focus is sharing and showing off, not analysis.
- **WuWaBuilds** is the full workbench: OCR scanning, scoring, editing, and leaderboards. That's where you build and optimize seriously.
- **WaveMate** is the fast scorer: it evaluates how much an Echo is worth directly, without building a presentation card.

Use WuWaFlex when you already have the build done and just want a nice card for Discord or Reddit, or when you're after inspiration by flipping through other people's builds. To genuinely optimize, go to WuWaBuilds.

## What people use it for

**Generating shareable flex cards**: the core use. You enter your build and get a visual image ready to post on Discord, Reddit, or social media.

**Importing an existing build**: instead of building it by hand, you import your Resonator's data so the card generates faster.

**Showing off a finished build**: when you've completed a Resonator and want to display the result, the card summarizes everything legibly.

**Looking for inspiration**: you browse builds other players have published to get ideas on Echoes, weapons, or stat distribution.

**Comparing community approaches**: by viewing several builds of the same Resonator, you pick up which configurations dominate without doing an exhaustive analysis.

## Who this tool is NOT for

It's not for someone who needs to scan many Echoes with OCR or who wants detailed substat-quality scoring: that work is done by WuWaBuilds or WaveMate. It's also not for early game, where there are no finished builds worth showing. And it's not a theorycraft tool: it helps you present and discover builds, not calculate which one is optimal.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open wuwaflex.com in any browser, with no install.
2. Pick the Resonator and build or import its build (weapon, Echoes, and stats).
3. Adjust whatever data the tool needs to complete the card.
4. Generate the visual flex card.
5. Download it or copy the link to share on Discord, Reddit, or social media.
6. If you want ideas, browse the builds published by other players.

## Honest limitations

- **It doesn't scan Echoes via OCR**: unlike WuWaBuilds, you depend on entering or importing the data rather than reading it from a screenshot.
- **It doesn't score your Echoes' quality**: the card shows the build, but it won't tell you how good your substat rolls are or whether an Echo is worth keeping.
- **Focus on presentation, not analysis**: it's a showcase tool; it doesn't substitute for an optimization workbench.
- **Other people's builds are reference, not gospel**: what others publish reflects their choices, not necessarily the optimal build for each endgame mode.

## How to get started

No install or account required. Go to wuwaflex.com, pick a Resonator you've already built, and enter or import its build to generate your first flex card. Once you see the format, use it as the final step of the flow: optimize in WuWaBuilds, verify your Echoes' value, and when the build is ready, generate the card in WuWaFlex to share it. If you're just after ideas, spend some time browsing published builds before assembling your own.
