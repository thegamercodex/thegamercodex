---
title: Last Epoch Fandom vs Last Epoch Wiki — which wiki to use
description: LE's two active community wikis. Fandom has broader coverage and better SEO; lastepoch.wiki has clean UI and modern editorial.
---

LE has two active community wikis — lastepoch.fandom.com (legacy, Fandom-hosted) and lastepoch.wiki (modern, independent). Both stay current post-Season 4 but with opposing propositions.

## Coverage history

Fandom exists since Early Access (2020) and accumulated four years of editor work pre-launch. Has the most extensive library: hundreds of articles covering items, mechanics, lore, quests, bestiary, patch history.

Lastepoch.wiki started February 2024 (same month as 1.0 launch). Smaller library but curated coverage and consistent editorial team. Grows patch after patch.

For exhaustive lookup, Fandom is more likely to have the article you need.

## UX: ads vs cleanliness

Fandom is notorious for invasive ads — banner ads, video popups, sticky ads on mobile. The platform's corporate UI is the same across all Fandom wikis; not optimized for gaming.

Lastepoch.wiki runs on its own platform without invasive ads. Modern UI, clear navigation, mobile-friendly. Reading experience is noticeably better.

## Google SEO

Here Fandom wins. When searching "Last Epoch [unique name]" or "Last Epoch [mechanic]", Fandom usually appears first. Fandom's network size as a global platform helps it in ranking.

Lastepoch.wiki has lower SEO — direct bookmark is the fastest access.

## Editorial quality

Lastepoch.wiki has a consistent editorial team that curates articles. Quality is predictable.

Fandom has dispersed editorial — some articles are excellent maintained by active editors, others incomplete or outdated for months. No clear signal which is which.

## When each wins

| Use case | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Clean reading UX | lastepoch.wiki | No ads, modern UI |
| Coverage breadth | Fandom | 4 more years of articles |
| Google search hit | Fandom | Dominant SEO |
| Mobile reading | lastepoch.wiki | Mobile-friendly vs Fandom mobile horrible |
| Editorial consistency | lastepoch.wiki | Curated team vs dispersed editors |
| Lore deep dive | Fandom (tie) | More extensive coverage |

## Recommendation

Ideal workflow: **bookmark lastepoch.wiki as default**. For specific queries it lacks, fallback to Fandom (typically via Google search already filtered by your query).

If you arrive at Fandom from Google search, perfect — read and leave. No need to navigate within the site.

Strong adblock recommended for Fandom. Without it, the experience is heavily degraded.

For mobile: always go to lastepoch.wiki. Fandom mobile is painful.
