title: Last Epoch Discord vs official forum — real-time chat vs structured archive

---

Both are official from Eleventh Hour Games, but their role in the community workflow differs. Discord is the casual community of daily traffic; the forum is the structured archive for formal discussion.

## Volume and velocity

Discord (200k+ members) is high-volume, high-velocity. Channels live in the current moment, with casual chat, voice channels, immediate reactions. For newcomers can be overwhelming — setting notification preferences is essential.

Forum (forum.lastepoch.com) is low-volume, low-velocity. Posts cooked over hours-days. More serious tone, less meme. For players who prefer async discussion, forum is better.

## Dev presence and depth

Here the forum wins on a specific case. When devs want to explain design decisions extensively, they do it via **Developer Blogs** on the forum. Long-form posts with context, philosophy, future plans. Discord doesn't get that kind of content — devs simplify messages for chat format.

To understand the depth of why EHG made X decision, the forum is the only source.

But for day-to-day Q&A, Discord has more interactive dev and CM presence.

## Bug reports

Forum wins clearly. The bug-report template forces structured fields (repro steps, version, severity, screenshots). Useful for devs ingesting reports and tracking.

Discord bug reports in #help get buried in scroll. Without proper tracking, they can be ignored unless high visibility.

For bugs affecting your account progress, the forum is the correct formal option.

## Build Compendium

The forum has a pinned thread called "Last Epoch Build Compendium" that aggregates community builds per cycle. Curated by the community with links to guides, planners, videos. Single source for build discovery beyond Maxroll/LE Tools.

Discord has no curated equivalent — build discussion flows in class channels but without archive.

## Searchable archive

Forum archives discussions since Early Access (2018+). Google indexes threads and they're searchable forever. If you want to know what was discussed 3 years ago about X mechanic, the forum has it.

Discord is ephemeral. Hard to search historically.

## When each wins

| Use case | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Real-time announcement reactions | Discord | Velocity, channels live |
| Long-form dev blog | Forum | Devs publish here, not on Discord |
| Formal bug report | Forum | Structured template + tracking |
| Curated Build Compendium | Forum | Unique pinned thread of its kind |
| Searchable archive | Forum | 8+ years of indexed threads |
| Fast Q&A | Discord | Class channels respond in minutes |
| LFG / coordination | Discord | Velocity for party making |

## Recommendation

Use both in different flows:

- **Discord**: daily check for real-time updates, LFG, Q&A, community pulse.
- **Forum**: weekly/bi-weekly check of Developer Blogs and formal bug report when needed.

For newcomers: Discord first. Forum when you start having formal opinions or need archive search.

For hardcore: both active. Forum for depth, Discord for velocity.
