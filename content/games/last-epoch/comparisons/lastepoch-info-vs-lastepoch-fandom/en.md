---
title: Last Epoch Info (TunkLab) vs Last Epoch Fandom — DB-style vs wiki-style
description: TunkLab is a technical database with calculators; Fandom is a narrative wiki with broad coverage. They cover different but complementary reference needs.
---

Both serve as "reference" but with totally different propositions. TunkLab (lastepoch.tunklab.com) is a technical database with dedicated calculators; Fandom (lastepoch.fandom.com) is a narrative wiki with historical coverage.

## Each one's proposition

TunkLab provides browseable tables of items, affixes, uniques, skills, monolith timelines, factions, plus defense/ward/damage calculators no other hub offers. The info is dense, technical, no storytelling. For theorycrafters who need precise stat ranges or want to model ward decay, TunkLab is the choice.

Fandom provides a traditional wiki with narrative articles. Items have their stats but also narrative descriptions, history, related items, lore connections. Covers quests, characters, temporal eras, complete bestiary. For players who want to understand the world or look up a quest walkthrough, Fandom is better.

## Technical lookup

TunkLab wins clearly. The Equipment section lists items by type with all possible affixes, tier ranges, level requirements in table format — exactly what you need when crafting.

Fandom has the info but embedded in narrative articles. Lookup requires more reading effort.

## Calculators

TunkLab has dedicated calculators for defense (HP, armor, dodge, glancing blow, resistances → effective HP), ward (decay rate, generation, max → per-second dynamics), damage (base, multipliers, conditional → final output). No other site in the ecosystem provides this.

Fandom has no calculators. It's read-only reference.

## Lore and narrative

Fandom wins. Has extensive articles on Eterra's history, main characters, narrative connections between temporal eras. For players who want to understand the game's world, Fandom is the only decent option (lastepoch.wiki is catching up but Fandom has more legacy).

TunkLab doesn't cover lore at all.

## Quest walkthroughs

Fandom has per-chapter quest walkthroughs. Useful for players stuck on a specific quest.

TunkLab doesn't cover quests.

## When each wins

| Use case | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Technical affix lookup | TunkLab | Precise browseable tables |
| Defense/ward/damage calculators | TunkLab | Only site with these |
| Quest walkthroughs | Fandom | Per-chapter coverage |
| Lore and narrative | Fandom | Only one with depth here |
| Patch history archive | Fandom | Chronological compile |
| Modern UI without ads | TunkLab | Spartan but functional |
| Mobile reading | TunkLab | Fandom mobile horrible due to ads |

## Recommendation

Don't pick one — use both for different reference needs.

- **TunkLab** for theorycraft, crafting, technical stat lookup, calculators.
- **Fandom** for lore, quest walkthroughs, history.

For players only interested in mechanics (not lore), TunkLab alone is enough. For players who want to appreciate the game's world, Fandom is a good complement.

Mobile: TunkLab wins easily. Fandom mobile is painful due to ads.
