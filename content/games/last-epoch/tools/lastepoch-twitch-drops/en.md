## What it is

Twitch.lastepoch.com is the official Last Epoch Twitch drops page, maintained directly by Eleventh Hour Games. Every season EHG runs Twitch drops campaigns where watching streams of the game for X hours grants in-game cosmetic rewards.

The page provides:
- **Current campaign**: which drops are active now with artwork and required hours.
- **Participating partners**: list of streamers/channels that count toward drops.
- **How to claim**: step-by-step instructions to link Twitch account to the game.
- **History**: past season drops (mostly retired, but documented).
- **FAQ**: claim issue troubleshooting.

Free, no signup on the page (Twitch signup required to participate).

## What problem it solves

**Reliable source of active drops**: many sites claim to have drops info but are outdated. The official page is ground truth.

**Partner discovery**: drops only count watching specific channels (not any LE stream). The official list tells you which streams qualify.

**Claim troubleshooting**: when drops don't appear in your account, the FAQ has resolution paths.

## What people use it for

**Pre-season check**: when a cycle starts, see which drops are available and plan watch time.

**Verify partners**: want to stream in background → confirm the channel you'll watch counts for drops.

**Resolve claim issues**: drop doesn't appear → go to FAQ → follow troubleshooting steps.

**Free cosmetics**: hardcore players collect historic drops. Exclusive cosmetics are only obtained during their window.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Players without a Twitch account**: drops require a Twitch account linked to the game. Without that, doesn't apply.
- **Players who don't watch Twitch or play**: if you don't consume streams or care about cosmetics, skip.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to [twitch.lastepoch.com](https://twitch.lastepoch.com/).

2. **See current campaign**: highlighted card with this season's active drops.

3. **To participate**:
   a. Twitch account → settings → connections → connect your Last Epoch account.
   b. On lastepoch.com → your account → connect Twitch.
   c. Start watching a participating partner's stream.
   d. Drops accumulate on your Twitch account → claim → appear in-game next login.

4. **To see partners**: scroll the page, there's a list.

5. **For history**: the page shows retired cosmetics from past seasons (no longer claimable).

## Honest limitations

**Active season only**: drops are time-limited. Once the cycle ends, you can't get them.

**Specific channels only**: not any LE streamer counts. Only partners on the official list.

**Requires real watch time**: not an instant claim. Required hours can be 1-8 depending on the drop.

**Twitch outages affect**: if Twitch tracking fails, drops can stop counting. Typical bug that takes hours-days to resolve.

**No push notifications**: if a new campaign starts and you don't check the page, you can miss it.

**English only**.

## How to start

1. [twitch.lastepoch.com](https://twitch.lastepoch.com/). No signup on the page.

2. **First-time setup**:
   - Twitch account → connect to Last Epoch (via lastepoch.com settings).
   - Verify the connection is working.

3. **When to open it**: pre-cycle launch to see which drops are coming and plan watch time.

4. **Recommended combo**: drop info on lastepoch-twitch-drops + your favorite LE streamer as background watch.

5. **Mobile-friendly**: the page works on mobile but account linking setup is better on desktop.

6. **Recommended bookmark**: the home, because each new season campaign appears there highlighted.
