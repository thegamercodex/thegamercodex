## What it is

Maxroll (maxroll.gg/last-epoch) is the LE section of the Maxroll editorial hub, a professional team that combines known creators with dedicated staff publishing curated guides. Covers cross-game ARPGs: PoE 1, PoE 2, D4, LE, D2R, and Lost Ark, with shared editorial team across all of them.

For LE specifically it provides:
- **Build guides**: extensive per-mastery, with leveling + endgame + push variants sections.
- **Build planner**: integrated web planner where published builds load automatically.
- **Tier lists**: class tier list and build tier list, updated patch after patch.
- **Getting Started**: guides for newcomers covering campaign, class choice, first endgame.
- **Monolith & Dungeons**: specific Monolith of Fate guides (beginner + advanced), each dungeon (Lightless Arbor, Soulfire Bastion, Temporal Sanctum), and boss strategies.
- **Leaderboards**: community-maintained leaderboard updated every few minutes, alternative to the official ladder.
- **News**: editorial analysis of patches and season previews.

Free to read. Optional login for favorites and comments.

## What problem it solves

LE has intermediate complexity between D4 (easy) and PoE (extreme). For a newcomer, the 15-mastery system with independent skill passive trees can paralyze — you don't know where to start.

Maxroll solves this with strong editorial curation: you go in, pick a class, see the cycle's top builds with tier ratings, open the build, and have leveling path + endgame + paragon-equivalent (blessings/passive points) on a single page. Saves days vs assembling info from Reddit.

For intermediate/advanced players, the **Monolith of Fate** and **Empowered Monolith** guides are required reading: they explain blessing targeting, corruption push strategy, and boss strategies with depth no other hub provides.

## How it differs from Last Epoch Tools

The two are LE's large hubs, but with opposing focus:

- **Maxroll**: editorial depth. Builds published by staff with long prose and per-slot justification. Tier lists with analysis. If you're new or want to understand the build's why, Maxroll.
- **Last Epoch Tools**: community breadth. Auto-crawled builds, exhaustive DB, planner without curation. For quick meta scans or seeing many build variants, LE Tools.

Most players use both: Last Epoch Tools for quick inspection and DB lookups; Maxroll for the deep guide of the chosen build.

## What people use it for

**Picking mastery pre-cycle**: 2 weeks before season start, you check Class Tier List and Build Tier List on Maxroll to see which mastery is strong and plan your fresh character.

**Leveling build**: the guide gives optimal leveling path (skills in order, gear baseline, blessing target) from level 1 to endgame transition (~lvl 70).

**Endgame optimization**: once at cap, you read build variants (push variant, farm variant, boss-kill variant) and pick by objective.

**Monolith planning**: the Empowered Monolith Guide explains timeline completion order, blessing priority per timeline, and corruption push strategy. Critical for optimizing the character.

**Patch reaction**: post balance change, Maxroll updates tier lists within hours. Whether your build survived or it's time to reroll, you find out there.

**Crafting deep dives**: specific guides on legendary potential, weaver's will, sealed affixes — crafting mechanics the game teaches poorly.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Extreme theorycrafters**: the integrated planner is good but isn't Path of Building. For deep DPS simulation, complement with PoB for LE.
- **Spanish speakers who want Spanish content**: Maxroll is English only. No localization.
- **Mobile-first players**: the UI is desktop-first. Long guides are hard to read on a small screen.
- **Lore enthusiasts**: this is technical content. For deep lore, go to wikis (lastepoch.wiki, fandom).
- **Off-meta theorycrafting**: published builds are the "canonical" ones for the patch. For creative non-meta builds, Last Epoch Tools has more variety.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to [maxroll.gg/last-epoch](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch).

2. **Browse by class**: top menu → **Classes** → pick your class. See all masteries with published builds.

3. **Click a build**: the page has sections — Overview, Skills, Passives, Idols, Gear, Mechanics, Leveling, Endgame.

4. **Open the build's planner**: link in each guide. You can clone it to your account to modify.

5. **For tier lists**: top menu → **Tier Lists** → Class or Build tier list. Each entry has a link to its guide.

6. **For Monolith**: top menu → **Monolith** → Beginner Guide → Empowered Guide. Read both before starting Empowered.

7. **For Dungeons**: top menu → **Dungeons** → guide per dungeon with boss strategies.

8. **For news/patches**: home → see latest articles including patch analysis and season previews.

## Honest limitations

**Endgame focus**: although there's leveling content, the most detailed material is endgame. For the first 50 levels any Maxroll build works — curation there is lighter.

**Build variety limited by curation**: they only publish builds that pass the editorial filter. For creative off-meta builds, Last Epoch Tools has more variety.

**Fast updates but not immediate**: post-patch it typically takes 24-72h to have tier lists fully revised. For immediate reaction, follow Maxroll team's Discord/Twitter.

**Display ads**: there are banner ads and newsletter prompts. Manageable with adblock; nowhere near the intrusive level of some competitors.

**No filter editor system**: Maxroll gives you published filters as downloadable files, but no integrated visual editor. To create/edit your own filter, go to Last Epoch Tools.

## How to start

1. [maxroll.gg/last-epoch](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch). No signup to read.

2. **If you're new to LE**: go to **Getting Started** → Beginners Guide. Then **Class Tier List** to pick a mastery.

3. **Once you've chosen mastery**: open the top-tier build for that mastery. Read the **Leveling** section first, not the endgame.

4. **Import the planner** if you want to modify the build to your situation.

5. **At endgame**: reread the **Endgame** and **Idols/Blessings** sections to refine.

6. **Empowered prep**: read the Empowered Monolith Guide before pushing corruption.

7. **Bookmark Maxroll** and check it weekly during active season for tier list shifts.

8. **For Q&A**: Maxroll team's Discord is active and guide authors respond there.
