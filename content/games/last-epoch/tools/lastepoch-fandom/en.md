## What it is

Last Epoch Fandom Wiki (lastepoch.fandom.com) is the LE wiki hosted on the Fandom platform (formerly Wikia). It started during the game's Early Access in 2020 and accumulated four years of articles before the official launch.

It covers everything you'd expect from a AAA wiki:
- **Equipment**: items, uniques, sets with stats.
- **Classes**: classes and their mastery trees.
- **Skills**: active skills with their passives.
- **Mechanics**: crafting, blessings, factions.
- **Quests**: campaign walkthroughs.
- **Lore**: history of Eterra, main characters, eras.
- **Bestiary**: enemies and bosses.
- **Patch History**: list of patches with main changes.

Free, no signup to read.

## What problem it solves

**Dominant SEO**: when you Google "Last Epoch Bleeding Heart" or "Last Epoch Lightless Arbor", Fandom usually appears first. For fast access via search engine, it's the default.

**Accumulated coverage**: four years of editor work means almost everything has an article. While lastepoch.wiki is new, Fandom has the most extensive legacy.

**Quest walkthroughs**: campaign coverage is more complete than on lastepoch.wiki — useful for players stuck on a specific quest.

## How it differs from lastepoch.wiki

- **Fandom**: broader coverage, better SEO, invasive ads, cluttered UI. Dispersed editorial team.
- **lastepoch.wiki**: more curated coverage, no ads, clean UI. Consistent editorial team.

Use case: if you land on Fandom from Google search, perfect — read and leave. If you'll navigate the wiki for pleasure or to learn mechanics, lastepoch.wiki gives a better experience.

## What people use it for

**Quest walkthrough**: stuck on a specific campaign quest → Google "Last Epoch quest X" → Fandom has the walkthrough.

**Quick unique lookup**: search the exact item name → Fandom appears in search → see stats and notes.

**Lore curiosity**: history of Eterra, narrative connections between eras, obscure characters.

**Patch history**: chronological list of patches with changes — useful context when an old build stops working.

**Bestiary**: enemies, bosses, their specific drops.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Players with strict adblock**: Fandom loads many ads and trackers that can break adblock or trigger warnings.
- **Those who want a clean UI**: Fandom's corporate theme with banner ads and video popups is invasive.
- **Theorycrafters**: the wiki isn't technical enough. For that, lastepoch.tunklab.com.
- **Mobile-first**: Fandom mobile is one of the worst gaming wiki experiences. Lastepoch.wiki is much better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to [lastepoch.fandom.com](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Last_Epoch_Wiki) — or more realistically, you arrive via Google search.

2. **To search**: the search bar works but is noisy (auto-suggests with ads). Better to use Google with "site:lastepoch.fandom.com query".

3. **For navigation**: sidebar with categories. But Fandom's navigation is notoriously bad — search is faster.

4. **For edits**: signup with a Fandom account → edit. Editorial moderation is loose.

5. **For mobile**: the experience is poor — ads take half the screen. Consider going to lastepoch.wiki instead.

## Honest limitations

**Invasive ads**: banner ads, video popups, sticky ads on mobile. Adblock helps but some features break.

**Dated UI**: the Fandom theme is the same across wikis, not optimized for gaming.

**Variable quality**: some articles are excellent, others incomplete or outdated. No clear signal which is which.

**Inconsistent edit cadence**: editor activity varies throughout the year. Some articles go months without updates.

**Tracking cookies**: Fandom is owned by a corporate parent (not Wikimedia Foundation) with aggressive tracking.

**Bad mobile experience**: ads take up half the viewport on mobile.

## How to start

1. If you arrived via Google, you're already here. Read and leave.

2. **To explore wikipedia-style**: go to the home and use the sidebar.

3. **Strong adblock recommended**: without it, the experience is heavily degraded.

4. **For mobile**: consider lastepoch.wiki as an alternative.

5. **For edits**: signup → contributions. But if you're going to contribute, it's probably more useful to do it on lastepoch.wiki where editorial focus is better.
