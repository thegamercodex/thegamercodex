## What it is

The Sanctum Market is a community Discord server dedicated to the Last Epoch trading ecosystem, specifically the Merchant's Guild Bazaar. It's not official from Eleventh Hour Games but has consolidated presence — it's where ArreatSummit sources its prices.

Main channels:
- **#price-check**: active traders give live price estimates for items you post.
- **#big-sales-gallery**: screenshots of valuable drops (T7 affixes, multi-LP uniques).
- **#meta-trading**: discussion of which builds are buying which items → demand meta.
- **#creator-spotlight**: streams and videos related to LE trading.
- **#announcements**: price guide updates and community events.

To join, click the invite link and accept rules. No barrier to access.

## What problem it solves

**Pricing precision**: the in-game bazaar has no auction history. Knowing what your T6 crit chance belt is worth requires knowing the meta — this lives in Sanctum Market.

**Real-time vs ArreatSummit lag**: ArreatSummit's Price Guide updates hours after changes; Sanctum Market discusses prices live, useful when there are sudden shifts (post-balance change, post-event).

**Big-drop discovery**: seeing which rare items are dropping (and selling for how much) calibrates expectations for your own farm.

**Community trader network**: if you trade seriously, you get to know other traders by name and that facilitates direct deals outside the bazaar.

## What people use it for

**Price check before listing**: post a screenshot of the item in #price-check, traders respond with a range.

**Verify price before buying**: ask if X price is fair before spending gold.

**Stay updated on meta shifts**: if a new build becomes meta and its items rise in price, you find out before it's reflected in guides.

**Sell specific items**: directly via DM or by posting in selling channels.

**Trader networking**: building relationships with high-volume traders facilitates future deals.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Circle of Fortune players**: CoF doesn't trade. The entire server doesn't apply.
- **Casuals who just level a character per season**: if you won't trade, the ArreatSummit guide covers the basics.
- **Players with Discord overload**: if you're already in 20 servers, adding one more for trading may not be worth the noise.
- **Those who prefer non-Discord tools**: if Discord isn't your thing, ArreatSummit covers 80% of the use case.

## How it's used in practice

1. Click invite [discord.gg/sanctummarket](https://discord.gg/sanctummarket).

2. Accept rules → see channels.

3. **For price check**: go to #price-check → post a screenshot of the item → traders respond with estimate.

4. **To watch big drops**: scroll #big-sales-gallery when you're bored.

5. **To list an item**: most selling happens via the in-game Bazaar after price-check. Discord is for validation, not fulfillment.

6. **For community**: voice channels are occasionally active during NA/EU prime time.

## Honest limitations

**Discord-only**: no web alternative. If Discord goes down, you temporarily lose access.

**English only**: active traders speak almost exclusively English.

**Variable quality price checks**: some traders give conservative estimates, others optimistic. Cross-check with 2-3 responses ideal.

**Activity bias toward NA prime time**: if you play in Asian hours, response time degraded.

**Loose moderation**: occasional spam or scams. Verify trader reputation before large DM trades.

**Cycle-dependent**: prices only apply to the active cycle. Eternal Realm has a different economy (more stable but less liquid).

## How to start

1. [discord.gg/sanctummarket](https://discord.gg/sanctummarket). Without a Discord account, you don't get in.

2. **Recommended setup**: mute notifications on all channels except #announcements (high volume).

3. **First price check**: post a screenshot of the item with context (class, target build). Wait 5-10 minutes for multiple responses.

4. **Learn the lingo**: T6/T7 affix, LP value, WW value, idol, and exalted are basic terms.

5. **Recommended combo**: ArreatSummit Price Guide for overview + Sanctum Market for validation before listing.

6. **Don't trade without a Discord-previewed price**: the difference between selling well vs cheap is just 5 minutes of price check.
