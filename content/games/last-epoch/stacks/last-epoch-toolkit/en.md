Last Epoch has the build depth of a serious ARPG —passive tree, skill specialization, targeted crafting— but the client barely helps you plan it. As in other ARPGs, players who make the most of each cycle lean on external tools to design, simulate, and reference their builds. This five-tool stack covers that cycle end to end.

## A cycle's workflow

Build selection starts at **Maxroll (Last Epoch)**: editorial guides with leveling and endgame that give you a clear plan from day one. To design or modify your own, **Last Epoch Tools** is the central planner and database —passive tree, skills, and items in a single tool. And to validate that the build holds up, **Path of Building (Last Epoch)**, the fork of the PoE classic, simulates your DPS and defenses in detail.

When you're hunting specific pieces, **Last Epoch Info** is the database of items, uniques, and affixes, so you know exactly what to chase. And so you don't miss new content, **ARPG Timeline** gives you cycle and event dates, useful for planning when to return to the game.

## Why these five

Each covers a stage of the cycle:

- **Build guides** → Maxroll
- **Planner and database** → Last Epoch Tools
- **DPS simulation** → Path of Building (LE)
- **Item reference** → Last Epoch Info
- **Cycle calendar** → ARPG Timeline

You don't need all five to play, but together they turn character building into something planned instead of trial and error. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
