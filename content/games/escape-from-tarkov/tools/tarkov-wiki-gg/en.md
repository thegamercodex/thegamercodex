## What it is

Escape from Tarkov Wiki on wiki.gg is the official migration of the historic Tarkov wiki from Fandom to wiki.gg, a platform that offers wiki communities ad-free migration while keeping editorial control. It covers lore, mechanics, items, maps, tasks, and traders, with content written and maintained by volunteer editors. Runs on MediaWiki with the standard wiki.gg theme.

## What problem it solves

Fandom monetizes with invasive ads, autoplay videos, and heavy tracking that degrade the experience of reading wiki articles. The migration to wiki.gg solves that by offering the same content on a platform without that commercial overhead. For players who still prefer wiki-style structure over modern databases, wiki.gg is the readable option.

## Differentiation

Versus the Fandom wiki (same historic content but heavy platform) and versus tarkov.dev (jugable data updatable via API), wiki.gg occupies the traditional wiki space focused on narrative coverage, lore, and historic context of mechanics. If you want to know why a trader sells certain items or the background of a task, wiki.gg gives you context that tarkov.dev doesn't include.

## What people use it for

- **Reading lore and background** of traders, locations, and in-game events to understand the world.
- **Looking up task walkthroughs** with full narrative, not just the objective list.
- **Consulting historic articles** on removed mechanics or between-wipe changes documented editorially.
- **Browsing community-embedded galleries and videos** to visualize locations without leaving for YouTube.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you want jugable data updated to the current wipe (prices, numeric stats, recipes), tarkov.dev updates much faster. And if you want active tracking of your progress, this doesn't manage state — use TarkovTracker.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open escapefromtarkov.wiki.gg in your browser.
2. Use the search box top-right to look up items, locations, traders, or tasks.
3. Location pages include maps, common loot lists, extracts, and trivia.
4. Task pages have full objectives, prerequisites, and rewards with narrative.
5. No login required to read; you only need a wiki.gg account to edit.

## Honest limitations

- **Update lag**: after a big patch, numeric data takes days or weeks to reflect changes. For current prices and stats, tarkov.dev is the faster source.
- **Uneven coverage**: some pages are extremely complete and others stay thin after the migration.
- **English only**: although MediaWiki supports multilingual, the active wiki.gg editor community is EN-only.
- **Still a wiki**: for players who prefer databases with search and filters over reading long articles, the traditional UX can be frustrating.

## How to get started

Visit escapefromtarkov.wiki.gg. To edit, create a free wiki.gg account. The editor community coordinates on a Discord linked from the wiki footer.
