## What it is

Tarkov Advisor is a Tarkov companion web app combining task tracking, hideout upgrade planner, stash management system to physically inventory items, and a value-per-slot tier list that helps decide what to keep in raid. Reports 80,000+ active users. Google login. Data comes from tarkov.dev API, with hourly updates.

## What problem it solves

Tracking progress in Tarkov requires several simultaneous dimensions: tasks, FIR items, hideout, stash, and meta items. Tarkov Advisor groups everything in a single UI with cross-component linking. The stash management is unique — you can inventory what you have and see what's needed vs what's extra.

## Differentiation

Versus TarkovTracker (open-source, minimalist, better team sync), Tarkov Advisor adds layers Tracker doesn't have: item tier list by value-per-slot (ROI per stash space), graphical hideout planner with dependency tree (what unlocks what), and stash inventory tracking. The trade-off is closed-source and less refined team sync.

## What people use it for

- **Visual task and hideout tracker** with a friendlier UI than Tracker's minimalism.
- **Value-per-slot tier list** to decide what loot to keep when the stash is full.
- **Stash management** to inventory what's stored and plan uses.
- **Visual hideout planner** with a dependency tree (what unlocks what).
- **Integrated item price comparison** with tarkov.dev data.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you value open-source and flawless team sync, TarkovTracker is the choice. If you just want raw fast data without a friendly layout, tarkov.dev directly. And if your wipe is solo and casual without obsessing over value-per-slot, Tarkov Advisor is overkill.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit tarkovadvisor.com and log in with Google.
2. The dashboard offers quest tracking, hideout planner, and stash inventory as separate modules.
3. For tier list, the Items module shows ranking by value-per-slot with category filters.
4. For stash, you can mark items you own and the system tells you which hideout upgrades you can do now.
5. Advanced features (sync, advanced hideout planner) require login; item search and prices work without login.

## Honest limitations

- **Closed-source**: if you value transparency or want to contribute, TarkovTracker is preferable.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Less mature team sync**: it exists but TarkovTracker has the better implementation.
- **Depends on tarkov.dev**: if the source API changes or fails, Tarkov Advisor is affected.

## How to get started

Visit tarkovadvisor.com. Log in with Google for advanced features. Searching items and prices works without login if you just want to try it.
