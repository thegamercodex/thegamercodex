## What it is

Tarkov Market is a web app specialized in Tarkov economic data: real-time flea market prices, per-item evolution charts, alerts when a price crosses a threshold, and barter-trade-vs-trader-sale comparison. It has a mobile app (iOS and Android) for push alerts and price notifications when you're outside the game.

## What problem it solves

Tarkov's flea market is volatile. An item worth 50k roubles today may be 80k tomorrow if a wipe task demands it. Selling at the right moment or buying before a spike can multiply your raid profit. Tarkov Market solves the missing price history and alerts that the in-game flea doesn't provide — it only shows active offers.

## Differentiation

Versus tarkov.dev (current price data but no alerts or deep history) and the prices embedded in TarkovTracker (reference only), Tarkov Market is the only tool with pure economic focus: per-item charts, configurable alerts, and barter margin comparisons. The trade-off is that if you only want to know "what's this worth now", tarkov.dev is faster and ad-free.

## What people use it for

- **Set price alerts** for items you want to buy cheap (e.g. GPU, LEDX) when they cross a threshold.
- **Decide to sell or wait** by looking at the 7-day chart before listing.
- **Compare barter vs trader sell** for items with barter trades — sometimes the barter is 30% more profit.
- **Track wipe meta items** that rise/fall with balance changes or demanding tasks.
- **Mobile alerts** while not playing — useful for active flippers.

## Who this tool isn't for

If your rolling rouble loop is modest and you're not flipping seriously, tarkov.dev's static prices are enough. If your priority is quest tracking, this is orthogonal — use TarkovTracker.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit tarkov-market.com and search the item you want to monitor.
2. The item page shows current flea price, trader sells, 7/30-day chart, and active barter trades.
3. For alerts, register with email and set a threshold (e.g. "alert when GPU < 350k").
4. Install the mobile app for push notifications when the price meets your condition.
5. The Barter section compares which barter trade is more profitable than buying the item directly on the flea.

## Honest limitations

- **Community-driven data**: prices come from flea scraping — there can be minutes of gap on rarely listed items.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Free tier sufficient, but ads**: the web has display ads. The mobile app has an optional premium tier without ads.
- **Doesn't replace the game**: prices change fast; always check the in-game flea before treating an alert as final.

## How to get started

Visit tarkov-market.com with no registration to view prices. Alerts require a free email account. The apps are on App Store and Google Play as "Tarkov Market".
