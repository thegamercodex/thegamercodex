## What it is

The Escape from Tarkov wiki on Fandom is the game's historic wiki, built since the early closed beta years by the same community that migrated their work to wiki.gg in 2023. Fandom (formerly Wikia) keeps the active copy of the content, monetized with display ads, autoplay videos, and cross-promotion of other wikis in the network. It's still the top Google result for almost any Tarkov search.

## What problem it solves

It's the most accessible historic Tarkov archive. For someone landing on it from a topic-specific search without knowing wiki.gg exists, this is the first hit. Covers tasks, items, traders, locations, lore, and trivia with the depth of years of community edits.

## Differentiation

Versus the wiki.gg migration (same base content but clean platform) and tarkov.dev (jugable data without lore), Fandom keeps the role of a massive archive with strong SEO ranking. The editorial difference with wiki.gg after the migration is minimal in content — the choice between them is about platform and update speed.

## What people use it for

- **Quick Google searches** when you land on a page without knowing which wiki it is.
- **Deep lore** and long mechanic articles with full editorial history.
- **Screenshot galleries** and visual references maintained by years of contributions.
- **Trivia and easter eggs** the community documents on this wiki by historic habit.

## Who this tool isn't for

If ads, video popups, or heavy tracking annoy you, jump straight to wiki.gg for the same content without the load. For current jugable data (prices, post-patch stats), tarkov.dev is much faster. And for progress tracking, this doesn't manage state.

## How it's used in practice

1. Google the topic you need — the top result is usually this wiki.
2. Use the internal search or category navigation to dig deeper.
3. Ignore the popups and banners; the actual content is in the center of the page.
4. Consider installing an ad blocker to make the experience tolerable.

## Honest limitations

- **Invasive ads**: autoplay videos, popups, and banners that break reading flow. The main reason for the wiki.gg migration.
- **Outdated data**: after a patch, numeric data takes weeks to adjust — sometimes never updated because active editors moved to wiki.gg.
- **Heavy load times**: large pages take time to load due to Fandom's tracking JS.
- **English only**: other-language versions exist but are abandoned.

## How to get started

Visit escapefromtarkov.fandom.com. No account needed to read. If you want to edit, consider doing it on wiki.gg instead — that's where the community's active editorial effort lives.
