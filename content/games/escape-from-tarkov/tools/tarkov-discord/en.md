## What it is

The official Escape from Tarkov Discord is the server maintained by Battlestate Games with channels for LFG (looking for group), technical support, official patch announcements, bug reports, and occasional AMAs with community managers. It's where official event, patch, and maintenance info appears first.

## What problem it solves

Tarkov requires social coordination — team raids are more viable and safer than solo PMC, and the flea market depends on information that often circulates on Discord before hitting sites. The official Discord centralizes that info and connects the community to BSG directly.

## What people use it for

- **LFG by map and level** in channels segmented by difficulty and region.
- **Bug reports** and checking if others have the same problem.
- **Maintenance announcements** and anticipated wipes.
- **Meta discussion** and community reaction to patches.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you want sustained team coordination with progress sync, TarkovTracker is the specific tool. If you want async discussion in threading, the subreddit works better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Accept the invite from discord.com/invite/escapefromtarkov.
2. Read the rules channel and accept the agreement.
3. Access channels via roles — grab the ones matching your platform and language.
4. LFG in its corresponding channel with the format `map | level | gear`.

## Honest limitations

- **Massive size**: hundreds of thousands of members — noise is high.
- **Limited BSG support**: for real support, the web form is more reliable.
- **Variable moderation**: sensitive topics or critique of the game can get moderated aggressively.
