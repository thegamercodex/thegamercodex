## What it is

Map Genie is a multi-game platform that produces premium interactive maps for shooters, RPGs, and survival games. Its Escape from Tarkov section has all twelve maps with filterable layers for PMC and Scav spawns, extracts (separated by timing and requirements), key spawns, high-value loot containers, weapon crates, marker plays, and quest points. The free tier shows basic layers; the Pro tier (~$3-5 USD/month) unlocks full filters and removes ads.

## What problem it solves

Learning Tarkov's twelve maps takes hundreds of hours if you do it only in raid. Map Genie compresses that curve by showing probable spawns, optimal loot routes, extract locations, and where valuable keys appear. With granular filters you can look at only what matters for your run (e.g. only extracts feasible from the north Customs spawn).

## Differentiation

Versus tarkov.dev's embedded maps (functional, free, but less polished) and TarkovHead's (free, coherent layout with quests), Map Genie wins on pure map UX: smooth zoom, responsive filters, precise markers, and a dedicated mobile app. The trade-off is the paywall — the most useful layers require Pro.

## What people use it for

- **Plan extract routes** by filtering only the extracts your spawn can reach without losing the timer.
- **Locate specific key spawns** before a raid where the key determines the route.
- **Study new maps** after each wipe or when BSG adds a new zone.
- **Coordinate with team** by sharing a direct link to the map with the filter applied.
- **Mobile app** to view the map on a second screen while playing on your main monitor.

## Who this tool isn't for

If your budget is zero and basic maps are enough, tarkov.dev has free maps sufficient to start. And if you want editorial guides or narrative context for the zone, KappaGuide and the wikis cover that better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit mapgenie.io/tarkov and select the map.
2. The left sidebar has checkboxes per marker category (loot, extract, key, spawn, quest).
3. Zoom in with the mouse wheel or pinch on mobile.
4. For Pro, log in with email and the subscription activates instantly — unlocks advanced filters and hides ads.
5. The mobile app (iOS / Android) syncs with your account — personal markers saved on web appear on mobile.

## Honest limitations

- **Paywall on useful features**: the free version shows maps but the granular filters and real time savings are behind Pro.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Tarkov isn't their priority**: Map Genie has 30+ games. Coverage is solid but per-patch updates can take longer than specialized tools.
- **Community-driven data**: like other maps, markers come from the community. Small patch changes can take days.

## How to get started

Visit mapgenie.io/tarkov with no registration to view the free version. If you're going to invest in Pro, try the free version for 1-2 raids to confirm the UX works for you. The mobile app is on App Store and Google Play as "Map Genie".
