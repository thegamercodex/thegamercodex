## What it is

SPT Forge (forge.sp-tarkov.com) is the official Single Player Tarkov mod platform, maintained by the SPT team under open-source on GitHub. It hosts every mod compatible with SPT with downloads, comments, version compatibility tracking, and community wiki. Replaced the legacy hub in 2024-2025. Top mods have millions of downloads (SAIN: 1.18M, Skipper: 271K).

## What problem it solves

Base SPT is good but the mod ecosystem transforms it — SAIN rewrites the AI, Fika adds private multiplayer, Realism completely changes gunplay. Without a centralized hub, finding mods compatible with your SPT version was a chaos of Discord servers and forums. Forge unifies distribution with automatic compatibility tracking.

## Differentiation

No competitor — it's the official SPT platform. The trade-off is that some experimental or author-distributed mods live only in private Discords; Forge covers most but not all of the long tail.

## What people use it for

- **Download SAIN** (AI mod), practically mandatory to make SPT feel alive.
- **Install Fika** for offline co-op multiplayer with friends.
- **Explore Realism** and other gameplay overhauls for alternate experiences.
- **Keep mods updated** by checking compatibility with your current SPT version.
- **Read reviews and comments** before installing to avoid buggy or abandoned mods.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you only play online without SPT, Forge isn't relevant. If you want mods for BSG's official client, that doesn't exist — BSG doesn't support modding the online game.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit forge.sp-tarkov.com.
2. Filter mods by category (AI, gameplay, QoL, items, etc.) or search by name.
3. Each mod shows compatible SPT version, downloads, rating, and comments.
4. Download the zip and follow install instructions (typically extract to /BepInEx/plugins/ or /user/mods/).
5. Mods with dependencies (e.g. SAIN requires SAIN Edition) are clearly documented.

## Honest limitations

- **SPT only**: irrelevant if you only play the online client.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Experimental mods live outside**: some modders distribute via private Discord by personal preference or restrictions.
- **Compatibility tracking depends on modder**: if the modder doesn't update the version flag, you might install something incompatible with your SPT.

## How to get started

Visit forge.sp-tarkov.com. No login required to browse and download. To comment or rate you need a free account.
