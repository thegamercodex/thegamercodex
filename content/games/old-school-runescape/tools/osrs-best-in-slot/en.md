## What it is

OSRS Best in Slot is a free DPS and best-in-slot calculator for Old School RuneScape. Its core trait is how little it asks of you: you can load your RSN to pull your combat stats straight from the hiscores, auto-equip best-in-slot gear, pick a target boss or monster, and instantly see your max hit, hit chance, DPS, and time-to-kill.

The most useful part is that it ranks all three combat styles —melee, ranged, and magic— against each other for any boss or slayer monster, so you know which one actually wins without building three loadouts by hand.

## What problem it solves

Powerful calculators require entering everything manually: stats, item by item, prayers. For a new or rushed player, that initial setup is already a barrier. And comparing melee against ranged against magic means repeating the work three times.

OSRS Best in Slot solves the friction. The RSN auto-fills your stats, auto-equip puts the BiS in place for you, and the three-style ranking gives you the answer —"this boss dies faster with ranged"— without you having to build anything.

## Differentiation

It's the **lowest-friction, most beginner-friendly** option of the trio. RSN auto-fill and BiS auto-equip make it the fastest to reach a useful answer.

Unlike the [OSRS Wiki DPS Calculator](/en/old-school-runescape/tools/osrs-wiki-dps-calc), which is canonical and open source but asks you to build the loadout manually, here the default path is automatic. And unlike [GearScape](/en/old-school-runescape/tools/gearscape), which searches for the best setup under constraints like budget, OSRS Best in Slot aims straight at the absolute BiS and the style ranking, prioritizing speed over fine control.

## What people use it for

- **Seeing at a glance which style wins against a boss:** the melee/ranged/magic ranking answers without building three setups.
- **Auto-filling stats with your RSN:** loading the account and skipping manual combat-stat entry.
- **Auto-equipping best-in-slot:** letting the tool put the best gear in place without knowing each item.
- **Estimating time-to-kill quickly:** deciding whether a boss is viable for your account before heading there.

## Who this tool is NOT for

It's not for someone who needs full, precise control of the loadout, slot by slot, with every exact switch and buff; for that level of detail the wiki calculator is better. It's also not for someone optimizing under a budget: auto-equip aims at the absolute BiS, not the best gear you can afford —GearScape fits better there.

And anyone who wants the community's canonical, auditable math should cross-check with the wiki's open-source calculator.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open osrsbestinslot.com in your browser.
2. Enter your RSN to load your combat stats from the hiscores.
3. Let the tool auto-equip the best-in-slot gear.
4. Pick the target boss or slayer monster.
5. Read the max hit, hit chance, DPS, and time-to-kill across all three styles.
6. Check the ranking to know which style kills fastest, and adjust if you want.

## Honest limitations

- **Less fine control:** the automatic path is convenient, but it doesn't replace precise manual tuning of each switch.
- **Doesn't optimize by budget:** it auto-equips the absolute BiS, not the best gear within what you can afford.
- **Not the canonical source:** it's wise to cross-check results with the wiki's open-source calculator for the most trustworthy math.
- **Smaller tool:** it may include ads and has less community backing than the official wiki calculator.

## How to get started

Head to [osrsbestinslot.com](https://www.osrsbestinslot.com), enter your RSN, and pick a boss you visit often. Let it auto-equip the BiS and check the three-style ranking to know which one wins. It's the fastest starting point; when you want to fine-tune each switch or audit the math, move to the wiki calculator.
