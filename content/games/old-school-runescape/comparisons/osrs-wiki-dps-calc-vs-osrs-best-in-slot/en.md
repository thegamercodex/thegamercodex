Both calculate DPS, max hit, accuracy and time-to-kill, and both live in combat-gear. But they target different profiles. The **OSRS Wiki DPS Calc** is the community's canonical tool: precise, manual and authoritative. **OSRS Best in Slot** prioritizes convenience: load your RSN, it auto-equips the best-in-slot, and you compare all three styles without needing to know anything upfront. One rewards control; the other, speed.

## Trust and authority

The Wiki DPS Calc is maintained by the OSRS Wiki team (Weirdgloop) and is **open-source**. That makes it the source the community cites when settling a gear decision: the formulas stay current and anyone can audit the calculation. When two options are nearly tied and you need certainty, it's the yardstick.

OSRS Best in Slot also produces useful numbers, but it isn't open-source and doesn't carry the same provenance authority. It also leans more on advertising, worth keeping in mind if ads bother you while planning.

## Friction and the beginner experience

This is where OSRS Best in Slot shines. Load your **RSN** and it auto-fills your combat stats from the hiscores; then it auto-equips the BiS and compares melee, ranged and magic against the boss you pick, all in seconds. For someone who doesn't know their own levels or the meta gear, it's the path of least resistance: you see max hit, hit chance and DPS instantly, without building anything by hand.

The Wiki DPS Calc is more manual by design: you pick every piece, prayer, potion and style. That asks for more upfront knowledge, but in exchange it gives you full control over every loadout variable.

## Control and precision

If you want to model exact combinations (a specific switch, a particular prayer, a certain potion), the Wiki DPS Calc exposes every variable for you to tune. OSRS Best in Slot's auto-equip is fast but doesn't give you that same fine-grained detail: it optimizes for you, which is great to get going but limits manual tweaking.

Another practical difference: the Wiki DPS Calc needs no account and no RSN, whereas OSRS Best in Slot gives its best experience when you load your name from the hiscores.

## When each one wins

| Scenario | Better pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New player who doesn't know their stats | OSRS Best in Slot | Auto-fills from hiscores and auto-equips the BiS. |
| A tight gear decision | OSRS Wiki DPS Calc | Open-source, wiki math: the authoritative source. |
| Quickly seeing which style hits hardest | OSRS Best in Slot | Compares all three styles at once, no loadout building. |
| Fine-tuning prayers, potions, switches | OSRS Wiki DPS Calc | Full manual control over every variable. |
| Calculating with no account and no ads | OSRS Wiki DPS Calc | No RSN required and not cluttered with advertising. |

## Recommendation

If you're just starting out or only want a quick answer on what to wear, **OSRS Best in Slot** is the easy path: your RSN does almost all the work. If you need precision, fine control and an authoritative number for a tight decision, use the **OSRS Wiki DPS Calc**. A good flow: start with OSRS Best in Slot to orient yourself, then confirm the fine details in the Wiki DPS Calc.
