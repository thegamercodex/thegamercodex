---
title: Wise Old Man vs Crystal Math Labs — the modern one vs the original
description: A short comparison of OSRS's modern XP tracker and the original that invented EHP. When integrated UI wins and when deep history wins.
---

These two XP trackers represent two eras of OSRS. **Crystal Math Labs** (CML) is the original: the tracker that pioneered EHP (Efficient Hours Played) and holds the longest historical record in the game. **Wise Old Man** (WOM) is the modern generation: open source, clean UI, RuneLite plugin, Discord bot, and clan competitions. Knowing which to use depends less on which is "better" and more on what you need: modern integration or historical depth.

## UI and integration

Here WOM dominates without question. Its site is clean, fast, and readable, with a near-zero learning curve. But the real differentiator is the integrations:

- **RuneLite plugin** that updates your profile automatically on login, with no pasting usernames or opening the site.
- **Discord bot** that lives in the clan server and posts progress, results, and stats by command.
- **Native competitions** (Skill of the Week, Boss of the Week) with a live leaderboard, plus achievements and name change tracking.

CML has a clearly older UI, inherited from another era of the web. It works and the data is there, but the design and usage friction show. It doesn't have the plugin-plus-bot ecosystem that makes WOM update on its own.

## Historical depth

And here CML has something WOM can't replicate: **time**. CML has been tracking OSRS since long before WOM existed. That means for old accounts, CML holds XP records reaching back years beyond what any other tracker has. If you want to see an account's progress curve across a decade, CML is the only source that has it complete.

On top of that, CML is the **origin of EHP**. The concept the whole ecosystem uses today — including WOM — was born there. For anyone who values the historical efficiency reference and the lineage of the concept, CML carries a weight no modern UI grants.

## Competitions and clans

WOM is built for modern clans: recurring competitions automated via Discord, a roster that stays current on its own, minutes-long onboarding. It's the default for any clan that lives in Discord and runs events. CML doesn't target this use case with the same depth; its strength is the long-term individual record, not real-time clan event coordination.

## When each one wins

| Scenario | Better pick |
|---|---|
| New player onboarding | Wise Old Man |
| Auto-update from RuneLite | Wise Old Man |
| Clan competition with Discord bot | Wise Old Man |
| Self-maintaining clan roster | Wise Old Man |
| 10-year XP history | Crystal Math Labs |
| EHP origin reference | Crystal Math Labs |
| Longest-reaching efficiency record | Crystal Math Labs |

## Recommendation

If you're new or run a clan, **Wise Old Man** is the clear answer: the integrations and modern UI make it today's default tracker. If your account has years of history and you want the complete record, or you're an EHP purist who values the original source, **Crystal Math Labs** still has something WOM can't give: accumulated time. For most people, WOM daily and CML as an occasional historical lookup is the natural balance.
