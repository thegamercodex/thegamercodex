## What it is

Valorbuff is a minimalist Valorant stats lookup site. Free, no required login, light ads. Active since 2021. No large team behind it — a small project run by one person or small group, which shows in its simplicity.

Covers the basics: paste Riot ID, get aggregate stats, win rate by agent, win rate by map, recent match history. No overlay, no editorial tier list, no coaching modules. Single-purpose, single-screen.

## What problem it solves

Tracker.gg and Blitz are powerful but have overhead — ads, premium popups, navigation between tabs. Sometimes you just want "what's this opponent's K/D" and don't need 15 UI sections.

Valorbuff solves this: one page, essential data, fast load. The pitch is speed and simplicity, not depth.

## How it differs

Versus Tracker.gg, Valorbuff has **fewer features but also less friction**. Tracker.gg wins on analysis depth and global leaderboards; Valorbuff wins on "open tab → see info" speed.

Versus Blitz, no real competition — Blitz is overlay + site, Valorbuff is just an express site. Different use cases.

If your workflow is fast lookup between matches, Valorbuff. If you want to analyze your career deeply, Tracker.gg.

## What people use it for

**Quick lobby lookup**: opponent shows on the team list, paste their ID, see K/D in 5 seconds. Faster than Tracker.gg.

**Smurf checks**: Riot ID with 50% win rate and 1.8 K/D in Iron is probably a smurf. Valorbuff shows it fast.

**Personal tracking without friction**: paste your Riot ID and see recent match history. For casuals who don't want more complication.

**Quick comparison**: two players side-by-side by opening tabs. Simple UI helps.

## Who it's not for

If you want depth — per-map analysis, percentiles, per-act trends, global leaderboards — Valorbuff doesn't have it. Tracker.gg is the option.

If you want an in-match overlay, Blitz is the only one.

If you need editorial content (agent guides, tier lists), Mobalytics.

Valorbuff is deliberately simple. If "more is better" for you, the alternatives fit better.

## How to use it in practice

1. Go to `valorbuff.com`. No login.
2. Paste Riot ID in the search bar (format `Name#TAG`).
3. Profile page loads in 1-2 seconds: stats, agents, maps, recent history.
4. Click a match for detail.
5. Done.

## Honest limitations

**Limited functionality by design**. Don't expect advanced features — they aren't here and won't be.

**Data can lag vs Tracker.gg**. As a small project, syncs with Riot's API are less frequent. Your latest match can take extra minutes to appear.

**Very basic UI**. No frills, no themes, no charts. Plain data table.

**No premium tier**. It's free forever, which is good, but means no economic incentive to grow features.

**English only**. No localization.

**Discontinuation risk**. Small projects can close. Not the safest bet for critical use cases.

## How to get started

Go to `valorbuff.com`. Paste your Riot ID. Done. If it suits you for quick lookups, bookmark it. If you want more, head to Tracker.gg.
