World of Warcraft's endgame —mythic raids and high Mythic+ keys— is where optimization stops being optional. The difference between wiping and killing a boss often comes down to decisions the game never explains: which talents to run, which piece to equip, where you're losing DPS. This stack fills exactly that gap with six tools most serious players keep on hand at all times.

## The optimization workflow

It all starts with the theory: **Icy Veins** gives you your spec's guide —rotation, talents, stat priority— updated for the current patch. It's your starting point before tuning anything.

From there, you stop guessing. **Raidbots** simulates your character and, with Droptimizer, tells you exactly how much DPS each available loot piece adds: no more equipping on instinct. After each raid, **Warcraft Logs** turns your fight into data: percentile parse, buff uptime, avoidable deaths, compared against millions of other players. **WoWAnalyzer** complements that with automated analysis flagging concrete leaks —a cooldown you didn't use, a cast you dropped.

For Mythic+, **Raider.IO** is the standard: your score, your key history, and the tool the rest of the world uses to build groups. And underneath it all, **Wowhead** is the encyclopedia: every boss's mechanics, drops, quests, and the guides that hold the rest together.

## Why these six

They cover both sides of endgame without redundancy:

- **Theory and prep** → Icy Veins + Wowhead
- **Optimization by numbers** → Raidbots
- **Performance analysis** → Warcraft Logs + WoWAnalyzer
- **Mythic+ and groups** → Raider.IO

You don't need all six to step into a raid, but you do to improve consistently. Each one has its full analysis in the codex.
