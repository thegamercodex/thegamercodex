## What it is

AtlasForge covers two WoW Classic versions at `atlasforge.gg/wow-classic` (Season of Discovery) and `atlasforge.gg/wow-cataclysm` (Cataclysm Classic). Combines interactive map, character armory, weapon/item database, and editorial guides.

Free tier without paywall. Moderate ads. Maintained by a dedicated team.

## What it solves

Classic players with multiple characters across Season of Discovery and Cataclysm need an armory to check gear, a map to locate quests/elite mobs/rare spawns, and guides for optimization. Keeping separate Wowhead, Icy Veins, and a map tool open is friction.

AtlasForge consolidates these in a single UI per version, with a unified account for cross-character tracking.

## Differentiation

Against Wowhead, it wins on modern UX and better visual map. It loses on database depth — Wowhead is the exhaustive reference with 20 years of community contributions.

Against Icy Veins: AtlasForge has map + armory that Icy Veins doesn't implement. Icy Veins wins on long-form written class guides.

## What people use it for

**SoD playthrough tracking**: armory and map for your Hardcore/PvP Season of Discovery toon.

**Cataclysm Classic completionist**: rare spawn tracking, achievement mapping.

**Cross-version user**: same platform for SoD and Cataclysm.

**Casual lookup**: weapon stats, quest locations.

**Cross-game**: if you also play Elden Ring, Cyberpunk, etc., same account.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you play Retail WoW exclusively, doesn't apply — AtlasForge only covers Classic versions.

If you want exhaustive database, Wowhead is still more complete.

If your priority is written class guides, Icy Veins or Wowhead Guides win.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open `atlasforge.gg/wow-classic` (SoD) or `wow-cataclysm` (Cata).
2. Create a free account for sync.
3. Enable map filters by type (quests, elite mobs, rare spawns).
4. For armory, enter character name.
5. Cross-reference with Wowhead for exhaustive data.

## Honest limitations

**Only covers 2 Classic versions**: if you play Wrath Classic or any other version, AtlasForge doesn't apply.

**Database shallower than Wowhead**: for obscure items you'll need to cross-reference.

**No per-spec character builder**: armory shows but doesn't allow spec planning.

**Updates tied to Blizzard patches**: each SoD phase and Cataclysm patch requires map updates.

## How to start

1. Visit `atlasforge.gg/wow-classic` or `wow-cataclysm` based on your version.
2. Create a free account.
3. Start with the armory of your current character to validate it detects your gear.
4. Enable map filters for your current quest hub.
5. Cross-reference with Wowhead when you need depth on specific items.
