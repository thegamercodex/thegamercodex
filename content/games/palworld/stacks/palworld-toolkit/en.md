Palworld mixes creature capture, base automation, and survival, and each of those layers has hidden depth: which Pal is good for which task, how to lay out an efficient base, and which breeding combinations yield the perfect Pal. The game explains almost none of it. This five-tool stack turns trial and error into informed decisions.

## How you use it

The base reference is **Palworld.gg**: the complete Paldex with stats, locations, drops, and skills for each Pal, ideal for deciding who to capture and for what. When you need more technical data —exact recipes, internal values— **paldb.cc** is the deep database that complements it.

For the base, **Palworld Base Planner** lets you design the structure layout and Pal assignments before building, avoiding redoing everything when you discover something doesn't flow. And to get specific Pals, the **xGaming Breeding Calculator** works out which breeding combinations yield the Pal with the passives you want, instead of crossing at random.

Finally, **Nexus Mods** is the mod hub: quality of life, balance tweaks, and content that extends what the base game offers, especially useful in long playthroughs or on your own servers.

## Why these five

Each covers a layer of the game:

- **Pal reference** → Palworld.gg + paldb.cc
- **Base planning** → Palworld Base Planner
- **Breeding** → xGaming Breeding Calculator
- **Mods and QoL** → Nexus Mods

You don't need all five to start, but together they remove almost all the trial and error of an optimized playthrough. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
