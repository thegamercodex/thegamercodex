## What it is

AtlasForge covers Palworld at `atlasforge.gg/palworld` with an interactive Palpagos map (the game's archipelago). Markers for Pal spawn locations, dungeons, fast travel points, resource nodes (minerals, plants), and syndicate tower bosses.

Free tier without paywall. Moderate ads. Covers base game plus Pocketpair's live-service updates.

## What it solves

Palworld has 140+ Pals at specific spawn locations, 13 syndicate tower bosses, dungeons that rotate daily, and resource nodes you need for base building. Without an interactive map, finding specific Pals for your breeding setup is trial-and-error.

AtlasForge solves this with per-Pal, per-element, per-location filters, plus checkboxes for captured-Pal tracking.

## What people use it for

**Specific Pal hunting**: filter by Pal and see exact spawn locations.

**Resource gathering**: locate minerals and plants for crafting.

**Boss tracking**: see which Syndicate towers you already cleared.

**Dungeon farming**: identify active dungeons of the day.

**Cross-game user**: same account for Palworld + 4 other AtlasForge-covered games.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you expect a breeding calculator (which combos produce which Pal), AtlasForge doesn't have that — look for dedicated Palworld breeding tools.

If you play heavily on private servers with modded content, the vanilla map may not reflect your content.

If you play casual without caring about min-maxing, the map is overkill.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open `atlasforge.gg/palworld`.
2. Create a free account for sync.
3. For Pal hunting, search the target Pal name in the filter.
4. Mark catches and resources gathered.
5. For syndicate towers, follow the priority order the map suggests.

## Honest limitations

**No breeding mechanics**: AtlasForge covers the map, not breeding combos.

**Variable DLC coverage**: Pocketpair keeps adding content and AtlasForge lags on updates.

**No native mobile app**: web responsive only.

**Small community**: comments and community feedback are less than in older games.

## How to start

1. Visit `atlasforge.gg/palworld`.
2. Create a free account.
3. Start with filters active only for the Pal type you want to farm.
4. Mark catches and come back when you need another hunting round.
5. For breeding mechanics, use a dedicated Palworld breeding tool.
