## What it is

PalBreed.AI is a web breeding calculator that goes beyond the classic "father + mother = child". Its standout feature is a **pathfinder** that calculates the shortest path between any starting Pal and any target Pal — useful when you're several steps away from the goal. It also has a conversational AI assistant that explains breeding decisions and suggests optimizations for passive and IV chains. Free, no login.

## What problem it solves

Basic breeding calculators tell you "if you cross A and B, you get C". That solves the simple case. The real scenario is: you have a Pal in your inventory and want to reach a target that's 3-4 hops away. Which intermediate combo saves you hours? PalBreed.AI calculates the optimal route. On passive skill chains (where you want to maintain 4 specific passives across multiple generations), it tells you which parents to use at each step to maximize odds of keeping the set.

## How it differs

Compared to Palworld.gg's breeding calculator (the basic, most-used one), PalBreed.AI wins on:
- **Shortest-path pathfinding**: indispensable when the target is far.
- **AI assistant**: explains the why of each decision, useful for newcomers who don't grok the mechanic.
- **Passive optimization**: shows probabilities of inheriting each passive combo.

Palworld.gg wins on:
- **Speed and simplicity**: if you just want the child of A+B, it's one click.
- **No AI, no "personalized experience"**: some users find the chat annoying.
- **Integrated with the general database**: while planning, you can jump to the Pal entry.

## What people use it for

- **Plan multi-generation chains**: 3-5 steps to reach a specific endgame Pal.
- **Optimize farming S-tier passives**: like Lucky or Burly Body that need controlled chains.
- **Build the "perfect" Pal**: target with element, work suitability, and 4 specific passives.
- **Ask the AI**: "I have an Anubis with Burly Body, how do I pass that passive to a Jormuntide?".

## Who this isn't for

If you're just starting with breeding, it's overkill — use Palworld.gg's calculator first. PalBreed.AI shines when you already grok the system and want to optimize long chains.

## How it's used in practice

1. Hit palbreedai.com.
2. Pick a starting Pal (what you have) and a target Pal (what you want).
3. The tool shows the shortest route with all intermediate steps.
4. For passive chains, add target passives and the tool computes probabilities per step.
5. If you need explanation, open the AI chat and ask in natural language.

## Honest limitations

- **AI can hallucinate mechanics**: like any LLM-based feature, it occasionally claims things that aren't true. Verify critical decisions against Palworld.gg or paldb.cc.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Closed source**: the pathfinder algorithm isn't public, you can't audit how it decides.
- **Performance**: very long chains (6+ steps) can take several seconds.

## How to get started

Open palbreedai.com, no signup. First setup: pick any Pal as a starting point and another as a target — the route is instant. If your real Pal inventory is complex, you can input it and the tool considers what you already own.
