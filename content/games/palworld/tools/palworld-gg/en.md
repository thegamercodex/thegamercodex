## What it is

Palworld.gg is a community-maintained web database that centralizes the game's information in one place: Paldex, breeding calculator, interactive map, items, weapons, structures, and stats. It's free, no login required, and updates within 48-72 hours of every major patch. Run by an independent team that also operates similar databases for other games in the genre.

## What problem it solves

Palworld throws information at firehose speed: 250+ Pals, ~90 passive skills, 9 elements, dozens of items and structures, breeding combos counted in the thousands. Without a decent database you end up with 8 tabs open jumping between Game8, half-finished wikis, and Reddit posts. Palworld.gg gives you one tab where everything lives.

## How it differs

Compared to paldb.cc (the other strong community database), Palworld.gg wins on presentation: the interactive map is faster, the Paldex has better filtering by work suitability and element, and individual Pal pages show reverse breeding (who produces this one) without bouncing to another section. PalDB compensates with rawer data for min-maxers (raw stats, full drop tables), but for daily use Palworld.gg is the more comfortable entry point.

## What people use it for

- **Quick Pal lookup**: base stats, work suitability, drops, where it spawns on the map.
- **Breeding calculator**: punch in two parents and see the child, or set the target and see every pair that produces it.
- **Interactive map**: mark rare Pal spawns, dungeons, Fast Travel, Lifmunk Effigies.
- **Item and crafting reference**: what you need to craft each weapon or structure, in which workbench.
- **Community tier lists**: passive skills and Pals ranked by use case (combat, base work, mount).

## Who this isn't for

If you want a save editor, a mod manager, or a Pal-by-Pal base planner, Palworld.gg isn't that — it's read-only. It's also not ideal if you need Spanish-language data; everything is in English.

## How it's used in practice

1. Hit palworld.gg, no signup.
2. Search a Pal by name, or browse the Paldex with filters.
3. For breeding, open "Breeding Calculator", pick parents or target.
4. To locate something, open "Map" and toggle the layers you care about (Pals, dungeons, fast travel, etc.).

## Honest limitations

- **English only**: no localization to other languages.
- **Patch lag**: new items typically take 24-72 hours to show up after each major update.
- **Subjective tier lists**: come from site staff, not open voting — useful but not gospel.
- **No accounts / no persistence**: you can't save favorite Pals or plan across sessions.

## How to get started

Open palworld.gg, no account needed. Bookmark `palworld.gg/map` if you'll mostly use the map, or `palworld.gg/breeding-calculator` if you'll plan breeding. The rest is one click from the top nav.
