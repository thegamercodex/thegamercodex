## What it is

RivalsDB is a third-party, unofficial web tracker for Marvel Rivals. Its emphasis is on leaderboards and hero statistics broken down by rank, alongside player profiles with their stats. It's a community tool, free and accessible from the browser, with no desktop client. It overlaps in part with Tracker.gg and RivalsTracker, but its own character comes from giving more weight to the standings and to how each hero's stats shift across ranks.

## What problem it solves

Marvel Rivals exposes no detailed leaderboards and doesn't tell you how each hero performs at different skill levels. What works in low ranks may not work at high level, and vice versa. RivalsDB tackles that: its per-rank stats help you understand which heroes stand out in your specific league, not in a blurry average of the whole base. And for those who like following the top of the ladder, its leaderboards give a clear view of who sits up high.

## How it's different

Within the tracker cluster, Tracker.gg is the big, established one with an overlay, and RivalsTracker is the lean, single-game option. RivalsDB sets itself apart by leaning into leaderboards and per-rank statistics as a central axis, rather than treating them as a secondary tab. If you're specifically interested in watching the ladder and understanding how hero performance varies by level, that orientation works in its favor. If instead you want general coverage, an in-match overlay, or a big brand's infrastructure, Tracker.gg covers more; and if you just want a simple, clean profile, RivalsTracker also fits. It's respectable as a second source, or as your main tracker if its focus matches what you're after.

## What people use it for

- **Following leaderboards**: seeing who sits at the top of the ladder globally and checking the standings regularly.
- **Per-rank hero stats**: understanding which heroes stand out in your specific league rather than a general average.
- **Looking up player profiles**: reviewing a given account's stats when you want to check their performance.
- **Cross-checking data**: using it as a second source to compare against another tracker.
- **Informed pick decisions**: backing your hero choices with how they perform at your skill level.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you want an in-match overlay with live stats, RivalsDB doesn't offer one, that's where Tracker.gg with Overwolf is the pick. If you're after the tracker with the broadest coverage and historical catalog, the bigger brands cover more. And if leaderboards and per-rank stats don't interest you, much of its differentiating value is lost and a more general-purpose tracker suits you better.

## How to use it in practice

1. Go to [rivalsdb.gg](https://www.rivalsdb.gg).
2. Explore the leaderboards to see the top of the ladder.
3. Review hero stats filtered by rank for your skill level.
4. Search a player name to look up their profile and stats.
5. Bookmark the site if you'll come back to check leaderboards or per-rank stats often.

## Honest limitations

- **Third-party, unofficial data**: NetEase does not endorse the tool. Accuracy depends on data collected from public accounts and can have gaps or delays after patches.
- **Overlap with other trackers**: some of what it offers is already covered by Tracker.gg and RivalsTracker; its real value lies in its leaderboard and per-rank focus.
- **No overlay**: it's web-only, with no desktop client or live in-match stats.
- **No official API**: since there's no public Marvel Rivals API, all data is reconstructed from whatever the site manages to collect.
- **Possible ads or private accounts**: the site may show ads to sustain itself, and players with hidden profiles won't appear.

## How to get started

Open [rivalsdb.gg](https://www.rivalsdb.gg) and start with the leaderboards and per-rank stats, which are its strong suit. Search your name to see your profile, and if its focus proves useful, bookmark it as your main tracker or as a second source alongside another.
