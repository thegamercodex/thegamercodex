## What it is

Marvel Rivals Wiki (wiki.gg) is an independent community wiki, third-party and unofficial, hosted on the wiki.gg platform. It documents NetEase's game with pages for heroes, their abilities, maps, Team-Up combos, and patch notes, maintained collaboratively by the community under a Creative Commons license. Its distinguishing trait is the reading experience: clean, orderly, and far lighter on advertising than Fandom wikis. It's a growing project, so its coverage keeps expanding, but it already covers the essentials for reference while you play or plan.

## What problem it solves

Marvel Rivals doesn't ship an in-game encyclopedia with full detail on every hero, every ability, every map, and every Team-Up. For that you turn to a community wiki. The problem with many wikis is the experience: intrusive ads, pop-ups, video players that start on their own, and a page that takes a while to load. wiki.gg solves exactly that, offering the same kind of reference information in a clean, fast environment, which matters when you're checking something mid-match or between rounds and need the answer without friction.

## How it's different

Against the Fandom wiki, the split is clear. wiki.gg wins on usability: light advertising, an orderly interface, and snappy loading, ideal for daily reference. Fandom wins on breadth and depth, especially in lore, cosmetics, and search-engine ubiquity, thanks to its size and years of accumulated content. As an everyday resource we recommend wiki.gg for its cleanliness; when you're after a very specific lore detail or something not yet covered on wiki.gg, Fandom likely has it. For most practical lookups of heroes, abilities, maps, and Team-Ups, wiki.gg delivers without the noise.

## What people use it for

- **Looking up heroes and abilities**: reading what each hero does, their cooldowns, and how their skills work.
- **Reviewing Team-Up combos**: seeing which heroes enable which Team-Up Skills and how they fit a composition.
- **Studying maps**: getting familiar with the layout, objectives, and key points of each map.
- **Following patch notes**: keeping up with what changed in each seasonal update.
- **Quick in-match reference**: checking a specific detail without the page filling up with ads.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you want the most exhaustive lore, the most complete cosmetics coverage, or the niche article that only exists on the largest wiki, Fandom probably covers more. If you want live data from your account or match stats, a wiki isn't that, that's what trackers are for. And if you need information about a freshly released patch, keep in mind a community wiki can take time to update: confirm against the official notes if the detail is critical.

## How to use it in practice

1. Go to [marvelrivals.wiki.gg](https://marvelrivals.wiki.gg).
2. Use the search or the hero index to reach the page you need.
3. Open the hero page to see abilities, cooldowns, and synergies.
4. Check the Team-Up and map pages to plan compositions and rotations.
5. Bookmark the site as your default reference for quick lookups.

## Honest limitations

- **Third-party and unofficial**: NetEase does not endorse the wiki. It's community content and can contain errors or go stale after a patch.
- **Growing coverage**: being a younger project than Fandom, some niche or lore articles may still be missing or incomplete.
- **Depends on volunteers**: how fast it updates after each season depends on the editing community, not an official team.
- **No substitute for official notes**: for critical, freshly patched balance data, verify against the game's official source.
- **Ad-light, not ad-free**: it's much cleaner than Fandom, but the platform may show some advertising to sustain itself.

## How to get started

Open [marvelrivals.wiki.gg](https://marvelrivals.wiki.gg) and look up a hero you're learning to see how the page is organized. If the clean experience wins you over, bookmark it as your default daily wiki and keep Fandom as a second stop for deep lore or articles not yet here.
