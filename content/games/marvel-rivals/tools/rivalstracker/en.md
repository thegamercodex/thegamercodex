## What it is

RivalsTracker is a third-party, unofficial web tracker dedicated solely to Marvel Rivals. Unlike the big multi-game brands, the entire site is built around a single title. It offers player profiles, match history, hero analytics, and rank distribution charts that show how the competitive population is spread. It's a community tool, free and accessible from the browser, with no downloads or desktop client.

## What problem it solves

Marvel Rivals keeps no queryable history or aggregate stats inside the game. If you want to review your per-hero win rate, look back at your recent matches, or understand which rank most players cluster around, you need an external source. RivalsTracker handles that with a lightweight site: you search your name and get your data with no extra steps. The rank distribution charts add context too, helping you see whether your current rank sits above or below the community average.

## How it's different

Against Tracker.gg, the biggest and most established tracker, RivalsTracker bets on the opposite: instead of being one game in a huge catalog, it lives and breathes Marvel Rivals. That usually translates into a cleaner, more focused interface, without the noise of a site covering dozens of titles. The trade-off is clear: Tracker.gg has more scale, a single account across all your games, and above all an in-match overlay that RivalsTracker doesn't offer. If you want the overlay and a big brand's infrastructure, Tracker.gg wins; if you prefer a simple, dedicated site to check stats quickly, RivalsTracker does the job well.

## What people use it for

- **Quick browser lookups**: checking stats without installing anything, handy on any device.
- **Tracking per-hero win rate**: seeing which Vanguard, Duelist, or Strategist you perform best on across the season.
- **Understanding rank distribution**: using the charts to locate where most of the population sits and where you stand.
- **Reviewing recent history**: looking at your last matches to spot streaks or shifts in performance.
- **A second data source**: cross-checking numbers when you want to compare against what another tracker shows.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you want an in-match overlay that gives you live teammate and opponent stats, RivalsTracker doesn't have one, that's where Tracker.gg with Overwolf is the pick. If you follow several games and prefer a single account and site for all of them, a multi-game brand will serve you better. And if you're after tier lists or meta guides more than your personal stats, this isn't the focus.

## How to use it in practice

1. Go to [rivalstracker.com](https://rivalstracker.com).
2. Search your exact player name in the search bar.
3. Review your profile: match history, hero analytics, and overall stats.
4. Check the rank distribution charts to place yourself within the community.
5. Bookmark your profile in your browser to return with one click.

## Honest limitations

- **Third-party, unofficial data**: NetEase does not endorse the tool. Accuracy depends on data collected from public accounts and can have gaps or delays after patches.
- **No overlay**: it's web-only, with no desktop client or live in-match stats.
- **Smaller brand**: as a community project focused on a single game, it lacks the infrastructure and historical catalog of the big brands.
- **No official API**: since there's no public Marvel Rivals API, all data is reconstructed from whatever the site manages to collect.
- **Possible ads or private accounts**: the site may show ads to sustain itself, and players with hidden profiles won't appear.

## How to get started

Open [rivalstracker.com](https://rivalstracker.com), search your name, and bookmark your profile. There's nothing to install, so within a couple of minutes you'll have your stats on hand whenever you want to check them.
