## What it is

Blitz.gg is a third-party platform, not affiliated with NetEase or Marvel, known for providing tools and stats across several competitive games (League of Legends, Valorant, TFT, among others). Its Marvel Rivals section offers what you'd expect from the brand: tier lists, per-hero stat breakdowns, and build suggestions, all from an interface consistent with the rest of the site.

Beyond the web, Blitz offers a desktop app that acts as an overlay/companion for several of its supported games, and it runs a paid premium tier. Marvel Rivals coverage within Blitz is one section of a broad ecosystem, not the product's central focus.

## What problem it solves

If you play several competitive titles and don't want to hop between a different site for each one, Blitz centralizes meta reads under a single brand and a consistent interface pattern. For Marvel Rivals specifically, it answers the basic "what's strong right now" question with an immediately accessible tier list and per-hero stats.

The value is convenience and familiarity: you learn to read Blitz once and apply that knowledge across all its games, including Marvel Rivals.

## How it's different

The reference competitor here is RivalsMeta, a site dedicated entirely to Marvel Rivals.

The difference is scope. RivalsMeta lives and breathes a single game, so it goes deeper on Marvel Rivals-specific data: more breakdowns by rank and map, leaderboards, team comps, and a skin database. Blitz covers Marvel Rivals as one of many sections, with a lighter tier-list read but integrated into a multi-game ecosystem and a desktop app. You pick Blitz for cross-game convenience; you pick RivalsMeta for depth in a single title.

## What people use it for

- **Quick tier-list read**: see which heroes are well-positioned without opening a Marvel Rivals-only tool.
- **Leveraging an existing account**: if you already use Blitz for LoL or Valorant, adding Marvel Rivals without changing your habit.
- **Checking per-hero stats and builds**: keeping a baseline reference for performance and suggested setup.
- **Using the desktop app**: for those already running the Blitz companion in other games who want info on hand.

## Who this tool isn't for

If Marvel Rivals is the only game you care about and you want the most granular data possible, a dedicated tool will serve you better than a section of a multi-game brand. It's also not ideal if the premium-tier model bothers you or you don't want to install a desktop app to get the most out of the ecosystem. And if you just need a one-off tier list and nothing else, more focused options exist.

## How to use it in practice

1. Go to blitz.gg/marvel-rivals in your browser.
2. Open the tier list to see the current season's hero landscape.
3. Enter a specific hero to review its stats and the suggested build.
4. If you already use the Blitz desktop app, check whether Marvel Rivals is integrated in your companion.
5. Cross-check the tier list against another source before committing: any site's aggregate data lags the latest patch.

## Honest limitations

- **It's unofficial and multi-game**: Marvel Rivals is one section among many, so the game-specific depth is shallower than a dedicated site's.
- **Aggregate data that lags balance**: like any meta tool, its numbers reflect accumulated matches and take time to absorb a new patch.
- **Premium model**: part of the experience (especially the desktop app) may sit behind a paid tier.
- **Optional but heavy desktop app for the use case**: installing a companion just for the Marvel Rivals tier list can be overkill.

## How to get started

Open blitz.gg/marvel-rivals in your browser and check the tier list and per-hero stats, no install required. If you already have the Blitz desktop app for other games, see whether Marvel Rivals shows up in your companion. Use it as a quick read and complement it with a dedicated source when you need finer data.
