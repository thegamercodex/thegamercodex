When you search "best PoE 2 build" on Google, two sites usually show up first: **Maxroll** and **Mobalytics**. Both are editorial hubs with build guides, but they approach the space from different angles. Knowing which to use saves you hours of research.

## Key difference

**Maxroll** has spent years as an editorial reference across PoE 1, Diablo, and other ARPGs. Its model: top community players write complete guides — leveling tree per level, gear for each phase, exact gem links, farming strategy, tier list. Each guide has a signed author and is maintained during the league when changes happen. The tone is premium editorial, written so you follow it step by step without having to think.

**Mobalytics** comes from competitive games (League of Legends, Apex, etc.) and applied that model to PoE 2. Its differentiation is that **it has its own integrated web planner** inside the platform, plus a build tracker that lets you follow your progress against a reference build. The intent is for all your interaction with builds — researching, planning, following — to happen in one platform without jumping to PoB 2 or PoEPlanner.

## Which one?

- **You want a complete, well-written guide to start the league** → Maxroll. It's the project's historical strength and where editorial quality matters most.
- **You want to explore popular builds with your own integrated planner in the same place** → Mobalytics. Integration is its differentiation.
- **You care about editorial authority (signed author, community-reviewed)** → Maxroll. Guides are signed by recognized names.
- **You're new to PoE 2 and want an all-in-one workflow without installing PoB 2 or navigating between tools** → Mobalytics. Low initial friction.

## Considerations

Both are free web with clean UX and ad-supported monetization. Maxroll also has optional creator Patreons. Neither is available in Spanish (English only), like most of the PoE ecosystem.

Most reasonable path: if you're starting PoE 2 and want "tell me what build to play and how to level it", **Maxroll**. If you want to explore many builds and plan on the same site without jumping to other tools, **Mobalytics**. For serious players, both work in parallel: Maxroll for reference guides, Mobalytics for fast planning.
