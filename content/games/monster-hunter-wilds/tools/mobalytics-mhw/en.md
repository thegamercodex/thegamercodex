## What it is

Mobalytics is a multi-game platform (LoL, Diablo IV, Teamfight Tactics, among others) that added Monster Hunter Wilds coverage. It offers a build planner to create and share sets, plus per-weapon guides and tier lists, all with the consistent, polished UI the site is known for. It's free with optional premium sections.

## The problem it solves

For many players the problem isn't just solving a set, but understanding what to build and why. Mobalytics tackles both: it lets you construct the set and, alongside, gives you the editorial context—which weapon is strong, which build is recommended—without going to another source. It's planner and guide in one polished place.

## Differentiation

Against WildsBuilder or Rayleon, which are lightweight, powerful but dry solvers, Mobalytics prioritizes experience: clear presentation, integrated guides, consistent navigation. Against Maxroll, its direct platform competitor, the difference is editorial style and depth: Maxroll tends to go deeper in theorycrafting; Mobalytics bets on accessibility and design.

## What people use it for

- **Building with context**: creating a set while seeing the guide that explains the key skills.
- **Reading tier lists**: seeing which weapons are strong in the current patch.
- **Sharing builds**: publishing sets with readable presentation.
- **Getting in without friction**: new players who prefer a friendly interface.

## Who it isn't for

If you want the most exhaustive solver or the fastest assembly, the independent tools win. If premium sections bother you and you prefer something fully free and minimalist, WildsBuilder fits better. And hardcore optimizers tend to prefer Maxroll's depth or a dedicated calculator.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to mobalytics.gg/mhw.
2. Pick a weapon and open the build planner.
3. Build the set leaning on the integrated guides.
4. Check the tier list to validate your weapon choice.
5. Save or share the build.

## Honest limitations

- **Premium sections**: some content pushes toward the subscription.
- **Less solver power**: the planner is comfortable but not as exhaustive as Rayleon.
- **Variable coverage**: as a multi-game platform, MH Wilds depth depends on how much they prioritize the title.
- **English only**: no Spanish localization.

## How to get started

Open mobalytics.gg/mhw, pick your weapon, and look at the tier list and guide first. Then use the planner to reproduce or tweak a recommended set.
