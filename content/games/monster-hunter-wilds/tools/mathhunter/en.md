## What it is

Math Hunter is a web resource for Monster Hunter Wilds focused on damage, but with a pedagogical angle: beyond calculating, it explains how the formula works, offers per-weapon guides, and publishes meta summaries. It's free and community-maintained. Where other calculators give you the result, Math Hunter teaches you to read it.

## The problem it solves

Many players use calculators without understanding what they're measuring, and end up drawing wrong conclusions. Math Hunter tackles the root: it documents the damage formula, what effective affinity is, how element and raw interact, and why certain skills perform better on certain weapons. It turns theorycrafting into something you understand, not just copy.

## Differentiation

MH Wilds Calculator is the pure interactive, open-source tool; Math Hunter is stronger in the explanation layer and the per-weapon meta summaries. They're complementary: many theorycrafters read the theory on Math Hunter and run the numbers in the open-source calculator. Against a hub like Maxroll, Math Hunter goes deeper into the math and lighter on ready-made builds.

## What people use it for

- **Understanding the formula**: actually learning how damage is calculated in Wilds.
- **Per-weapon guides**: reading how to optimize a specific weapon's damage.
- **Meta summaries**: seeing which weapon or approach performs best in the current patch.
- **Settling theory questions**: confirming how two skills interact before investing in them.

## Who it isn't for

If you just want a quick number without reading, a pure interactive calculator is more direct. If you're just starting and don't yet grasp the basic mechanics, the theory material can be dense. And if you want the set built for you, this isn't a builder.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to mathhunter.ca.
2. Read the damage formula section to get the framework.
3. Open your weapon's guide to see how it applies.
4. Use the calculator to measure your setup.
5. Cross-check the result with the meta summary for context.

## Honest limitations

- **Dense for beginners**: the theory-first approach demands some baseline.
- **Community maintenance**: post-patch updates depend on the author.
- **Fewer ready builds**: it prioritizes theory over copy-paste sets.
- **English only**: no localization.

## How to get started

Open mathhunter.ca and start with the damage formula explanation. With that framework, the calculator and meta summaries make far more sense than using them blind.
