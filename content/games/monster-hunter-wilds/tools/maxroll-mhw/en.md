## What it is

Maxroll is a guides platform that started in Diablo and Path of Exile and expanded into Monster Hunter Wilds. It combines an interactive planner with per-weapon meta builds, endgame guides, decoration farming routes, and patch analysis, all written and maintained by an editorial team with a reputation in the ARPG community. It's free, ad-funded.

## The problem it solves

Solving a set is half the work; the other half is knowing which set is worth it and how to get the pieces. Maxroll fills that gap with curated, explained builds, plus farming context: which monster drops what, which decorations to prioritize, how to progress from early to endgame. It's the difference between "a set" and "a plan."

## Differentiation

Against WildsBuilder or Rayleon, which solve but don't opine, Maxroll is editorial: it tells you what's meta and why. Against Mobalytics, its platform competitor, Maxroll tends to go deeper in theorycrafting and per-patch maintenance, inheriting the optimization culture of its ARPG community; Mobalytics leans more on UI and accessibility.

## What people use it for

- **Explained meta builds**: copying a top-tier set and understanding the logic behind it.
- **Endgame guides**: tempered monsters, Arch-tempered, and decoration optimization.
- **Farming routes**: knowing what to hunt to get each piece or jewel.
- **Patch analysis**: reading what changed and which builds rise or fall after a Title Update.

## Who it isn't for

If you just want to build your own set fast without reading, an independent solver is more direct. If ads bother you, the experience can feel heavy. And if you want raw data (exact drop rates, hitzones), a database like Kiranico is the primary source.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to maxroll.gg/monster-hunter-wilds.
2. Pick your weapon and open its builds guide.
3. Read the recommended build and open it in the interactive planner.
4. Follow the farming route to get the pieces.
5. Come back after each Title Update to review what changed.

## Honest limitations

- **Ads**: the free model is ad-funded.
- **Depth curve**: the volume of information can be a lot for an absolute beginner.
- **Update cadence**: covering every weapon and patch thoroughly takes time; some things may trail the freshest meta.
- **English only**: no Spanish version.

## How to get started

Open maxroll.gg/monster-hunter-wilds, pick your weapon, and start with its builds guide. Use the planner to open the recommended set and follow the farming guide to assemble it in-game.
