## What it is

WildsBuilder is a web set builder for Monster Hunter Wilds, free and maintained by an independent developer. You pick a weapon and target skills, and the tool finds the combination of armor pieces, talisman, and decorations that meets them. Builds are stored locally in your browser, no signup required, and shared via a link. The r/MonsterHunterMeta community frequently cites it as the default option.

## The problem it solves

Monster Hunter Wilds won't tell you which combination of pieces maximizes your skills. There are dozens of armor sets, each with different decoration slots, and solving "what gives me Attack Boost 5 + Weakness Exploit 5 + Focus 3 with the least wasted slots" by hand is tedious and error-prone. WildsBuilder turns that puzzle into a seconds-long search.

## Differentiation

Against platform planners like Mobalytics or Maxroll, WildsBuilder is lighter and more direct: no guides, no accounts, no paywall. It does one thing—solve and share sets—and does it fast. Against MH Wilds Hub, which pairs a builder with a damage calculator, WildsBuilder focuses on the assembly side and shares without friction.

## What people use it for

- **Building a meta set**: copying a set from a guide and reproducing it piece by piece with the exact decorations.
- **Theorycrafting**: testing which skills fit when you swap a weapon or talisman.
- **Sharing builds**: handing a friend or posting in Discord a link that opens the full set.
- **Optimizing slots**: finding the armor that leaves the most slots free for quality-of-life skills.

## Who it isn't for

If you also want written guides, tier lists, and an explanation of why a set is good, a hub like Maxroll or Game8 gives editorial context WildsBuilder doesn't try to. And if your priority is modeling exact damage and comparing motion values, you need a dedicated calculator.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open wildsbuilder.com and pick your weapon.
2. Mark the skills you want and their target level.
3. Let the solver propose armor and decoration combinations.
4. Adjust manually if you want to prioritize a specific piece.
5. Copy the link to save or share the set.

## Honest limitations

- **No editorial context**: it gives you the set, not whether it's meta or why.
- **Depends on updated data**: after a big Title Update it may lag in reflecting new weapons or decorations.
- **English only**: the interface isn't localized.
- **Local storage**: clearing your browser storage loses your builds unless you kept the links.

## How to get started

Head to wildsbuilder.com, no account needed. Pick a weapon, mark a couple of skills, and watch it assemble the set. Save the link of any build you want to keep.
