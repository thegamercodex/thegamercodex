## What it is

Fextralife hosts community wikis for many games (the Souls series, Elden Ring, etc.) and has one dedicated to Monster Hunter Wilds. It's collaboratively edited, with per-monster pages that include weakness charts (the per-part and per-element stars), locations, gear, materials, and lore notes. Fextralife's hallmark is the community comments at the bottom of each page. It's free, ad-funded.

## The problem it solves

Sometimes you don't want raw datamine or an ultra-optimized guide, but the classic reference page for a monster: its weaknesses at a glance, where it appears, what it drops, and other players' experience in the comments. Fextralife fills that general-reference wiki role in a format familiar to anyone who's used its Souls wikis.

## Differentiation

Game8 is more tabular, concise, and fast to scan; Fextralife has a more forum-like style, with community prose and comment sections where tips and corrections show up. Kiranico is the exact-data source; Fextralife presents a more readable, approximate version (stars instead of the numeric hitzone). It works very well as a second opinion.

## What people use it for

- **Weakness at a glance**: quickly seeing a monster's per-element weaknesses.
- **Reference pages**: checking locations, drops, and gear in a familiar format.
- **Community comments**: reading other players' tips and corrections at the bottom.
- **Lore**: narrative context for monsters and zones.

## Who it isn't for

If you want the most exact data, Kiranico gives the number instead of the star. If you prefer more tabular coverage and ready-made builds, Game8 is usually more direct. And collaborative editing means quality varies between pages depending on how much the community has worked them.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to monsterhunterwilds.wiki.fextralife.com.
2. Search for the monster or item you care about.
3. Check its weakness chart and locations.
4. Read the comments at the bottom for community tips.
5. Cross-check sensitive data with Kiranico if you need precision.

## Honest limitations

- **Uneven quality**: being community-edited, some pages are far more complete than others.
- **Heavy ads**: the site loads quite a bit of advertising.
- **Approximate data**: the stars are readable but less exact than the numeric hitzone.
- **English only**: no localization.

## How to get started

Open monsterhunterwilds.wiki.fextralife.com and search for the monster you're hunting. Its star weakness chart gives you the quick read; scroll to the comments if you want tips from other players.
