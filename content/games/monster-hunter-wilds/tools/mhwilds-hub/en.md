## What it is

MH Wilds Hub is a free web tool that merges two things that usually live apart: a set builder and a damage calculator. You build your armor, skills, and decorations, and see a damage estimate for the set on the same page. It's community-maintained and requires no account.

## The problem it solves

The typical optimization flow forces you to jump between a builder (to assemble the set) and a calculator (to compare damage). Each jump means re-entering data by hand. MH Wilds Hub removes that round trip: you change a decoration and the estimated damage updates instantly, so you can iterate on "is this swap worth it?" without friction.

## Differentiation

WildsBuilder is faster for pure assembly and sharing, but doesn't calculate damage. A dedicated calculator like MH Wilds Calculator models the formula in more detail, but won't build the set for you. MH Wilds Hub sits in the middle: enough builder and enough calculator for most decisions, in one place.

## What people use it for

- **Comparing two sets**: building two variants and seeing which hits harder without opening another tool.
- **Validating a decoration swap**: seeing the damage impact of trading Attack for Affinity.
- **Theorycrafting with instant feedback**: iterating skills while watching the number move.
- **Choosing between weapons**: estimating which weapon performs better with a similar set.

## Who it isn't for

If you just want to assemble and share a set as fast as possible, WildsBuilder is nimbler. If you need damage precision down to each motion value and matchup against a specific monster, a specialized calculator is more faithful. And if you want written guides, this doesn't bring them.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open mhwildshub.com and pick a weapon.
2. Build the set with the builder section.
3. Mark target skills and decorations.
4. Check the estimated damage shown next to the set.
5. Iterate by swapping pieces and watching the number move.

## Honest limitations

- **Estimated, not exact damage**: useful for comparison, not for predicting real damage against a specific monster with all its hitzones.
- **Learning curve**: doing two things makes the interface denser than a pure builder.
- **English only**: no localization.
- **Community maintenance**: post-patch updates depend on the author.

## How to get started

Go to mhwildshub.com with no account, pick a weapon, and build a basic set. Watch the estimated damage and start swapping decorations to see the effect.
