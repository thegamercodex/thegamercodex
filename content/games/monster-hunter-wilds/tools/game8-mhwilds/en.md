## What it is

Game8 is a guides-and-wiki site that covers Monster Hunter Wilds with enormous breadth: meta builds for each of the 14 weapons, its own armor set search, a campaign walkthrough, item maps, per-monster weakness tables, and lists of every kind. It's free, ad-funded, and updated with each patch. It's among the first results when you search almost anything about Wilds.

## The problem it solves

When you have a concrete question—"which weapon is good to start?", "what's this monster's weakness?", "which Greatsword build is used?"—you don't want raw datamine or a solver, you want a clear written answer. Game8 is optimized for exactly that: direct articles that answer the player's question with tables and ready-made builds.

## Differentiation

Kiranico gives you the raw figure; Game8 gives it explained and contextualized in a guide. Fextralife, its most direct wiki competitor, has a community/forum style and sometimes more lore; Game8 tends to be more tabular, concise, and fast to scan. Against Maxroll, Game8 is broader in general coverage and less focused on deep theorycrafting.

## What people use it for

- **Quick answers**: resolving a specific doubt with a clear article.
- **Per-weapon builds**: copying a meta set with its explanation.
- **Armor set search**: using its own searcher to build by skills.
- **Walkthrough**: following the campaign or unlocking content step by step.

## Who it isn't for

If you need the most exact data (decimal-precise hitzones), Kiranico is the source. If you want the most powerful set solver, the independent tools win. And if ads and a heavy layout bother you, a cleaner wiki may be preferable.

## How it's used in practice

1. Search your question on Google with "game8 mh wilds ..." or go to game8.co/games/Monster-Hunter-Wilds.
2. Open the article or build that answers your question.
3. For builds, copy the set and follow the explanation.
4. For monsters, check the weakness table.
5. Use the armor set search if you want to build by skills.

## Honest limitations

- **Ads and dense layout**: the free experience loads ads and a lot of content per page.
- **Variable depth**: coverage is broad but not always the deepest in theorycrafting.
- **Translation**: the site originates in Japanese and the English version sometimes trails by a step.
- **No Spanish**: there's no localized version.

## How to get started

Go to game8.co/games/Monster-Hunter-Wilds and open your weapon's builds guide, or search directly for the monster you're hunting to see its weakness table.
