## What Elden Ring Nightreign is

Elden Ring Nightreign is FromSoftware's standalone spin-off released in May 2025, directed by Junya Ishizaki. It's not an Elden Ring DLC — it's a completely separate game that reuses the engine, assets, and aesthetic of Elden Ring to build a different experience: compact co-op, 40-minute runs, with a relic system and class-based gameplay.

The central premise is Expedition: you and up to 2 other players (you can play solo too, but the design is meant for groups) load into a procedural map derived from Limveld, a condensed version of the Lands Between. You have 3 in-game days to level up, find weapons, farm relics, and face the final Nightlord. Each night, a "dark rain" begins closing the map toward a central circle — a gameplay loop borrowing from battle royale but executed Souls-style.

The 8 base Nightfarers (Wylder, Guardian, Ironeye, Recluse, Duchess, Raider, Revenant, Executor) have unique kits: exclusive skills, weapon proficiencies, ultimates with cooldowns, and class synergies. There's no character creation — you pick a Nightfarer when you start the Expedition.

## Forsaken Hollows (DLC, 2026)

In 2026 FromSoftware released Forsaken Hollows, the first paid expansion (~$25 USD). It adds 2 new Nightfarers (DLC details are under selective NDA still), an additional Nightlord, new relics, weapons, and an alternate map. For the community it's a significant content boost — the base game was feeling limited in variety post-launch.

## Why it matters

Nightreign changes the Souls genre's rules:

**Co-op designed from the ground up**. Where vanilla Elden Ring has co-op as an afterthought with fog walls and ephemeral phantoms, Nightreign is built for groups. The 3 hours an Expedition takes with friends are dense — no walking simulator between boss fights.

**Roguelike + Souls fusion**. The Expedition structure (procedural run, death = restart, relics that persist between runs) is genuinely novel in FromSoftware's catalog. It's Souls combat with Hades loops.

**Short session time**. 40 minutes per run makes the game viable for players with fragmented schedules. You don't need to block out 3 hours for "a serious session."

## Codex ecosystem

The tools and resources ecosystem is much smaller than base Elden Ring's — the game is only 12 months old + DLC. There are 2 build planners (Mobalytics, Relics.pro) and a desktop calculator, 2 main wikis (Fextralife, Game8), map routing tools, and multiplayer mods.

Curated tools cover build-planning (Mobalytics, Relics.pro, calculator), reference (Fextralife, Game8), maps (route planner, seed overlay), modding (Seamless Co-op, Fun Is Allowed), and community (official Discord shared with Elden Ring + r/Nightreign).

If you're coming from base Elden Ring, the main shock is the speed: builds form in 30 minutes, you die, you start another Nightfarer. There's no character permanence except through farmed relics. Comparisons between tools matter less than in base ER — the ecosystem is small and editorial differentiation between tools is lower.
