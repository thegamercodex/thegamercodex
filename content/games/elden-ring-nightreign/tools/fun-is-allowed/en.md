## What it is

Fun is Allowed is a Nightreign overhaul mod distributed on Nexus (`nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/49`). Specifically designed to make solo and duo runs playable — vanilla Nightreign is balanced for trios and the other two configurations have real disadvantages.

Covers stats, starting gear, damage scaling, and survivability. Compatible with Seamless Co-op to extend while balancing.

## What it solves

Vanilla Nightreign for solo: very hard. Nightlords scale assuming trio, and solo means less DPS, no healing share, no aggro distribution. Most solo players abandon at the first challenging Nightlord.

For duos: better than solo but still tough — missing the third player who can dedicate to aggro management or healing.

Fun is Allowed adjusts this: you start with Wending Grace (auto-revive), improved base stats, adjusted damage output. The experience becomes "challenging but fair" rather than "impossible without trio."

## What people use it for

**Viable solo runs**: complete Nightreign single-player without extreme frustration.

**Balanced duo runs**: play with a friend without needing to find a third.

**Practice runs**: learn specific encounters without team pressure.

**Solo achievement hunting**: for users who need certain achievements solo.

**Combination with Seamless Co-op**: balancing for custom groups of 4-5 that aren't vanilla 3 nor full 6.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you always play in trio and your group is stable, doesn't add anything.

If you value vanilla's "intended difficulty," the mod adjusts it — doesn't fit purists.

If you play vanilla online multiplayer, the mod forces offline and you can't mix.

## How it's used in practice

1. Download Fun is Allowed from `nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/49`.
2. Follow the README to extract and install (requires offline mode).
3. Configure settings to preference — the mod has "aggressive rebalance" vs "minimal tweaks" options.
4. Launch Nightreign with the matching setup.
5. Start a solo or duo Expedition and validate balance works.

## Honest limitations

**Offline only**: taints your save for vanilla online.

**Controversial difficulty**: some community members think it "trivializes"; others "fixes" broken balance. Subjective.

**More complex setup**: requires Mod Engine 2 + Seamless Co-op for optimal use.

**Slow post-patch updates**: the mod's solo dev takes time to adjust after official balance changes.

**Limited compatibility with other mods**: changes many params; conflicts with mods that touch the same files.

## How to start

1. Visit `nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/49`.
2. Read the full README — includes dependencies.
3. Install Mod Engine 2 if you don't have it.
4. Download Fun is Allowed and extract following the structure.
5. Configure settings and launch with offline launcher.
6. Test in a simple Expedition before attempting hard Nightlords.
