## What it is

Game8 is a Japanese gaming guide network with English localization, covering dozens of popular games. The Nightreign section (`game8.co/games/Elden-Ring-Nightreign`) launched around release and updates with every significant patch.

Format is more guide-style than wiki-style: ordered priorities, tier lists, step-by-step. No paywall, ads more moderate than Fextralife.

## What it solves

When you ask "what priorities does this Nightfarer have" or "what tier is this build in the current meta," Fextralife gives data but not recommendation. Game8 gives direct recommendation: "level this first," "avoid this combo," "for this Nightlord use this."

For new players who want someone to say "do X, then Y," Game8 fits better than Fextralife.

## Differentiation

Against Fextralife it wins on UX (fewer ads, better mobile) and guide format. It loses on complete factual coverage (no page per item; grouped by priority).

Against Mobalytics: similar editorial style, but Game8 has a more rigid format (priority orders, charts) while Mobalytics has more integrated build planner.

## What people use it for

**Per-Nightfarer build orders**: which skills to level first.

**Tier lists**: Nightfarer and relic ranking in the current meta.

**Nightlord weakness charts**: which status effects work vs each boss.

**Quick reference**: for fast info without long scrolling.

**Mobile reading**: the wiki reads well on phones.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you want deep lore dives or community discussion, Game8 is very dry — format is functional but no discussion.

If you want raw datamined data, doesn't apply.

If you expect in-depth editorial coverage (multi-paragraph analysis), Mobalytics has more prose.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit `game8.co/games/Elden-Ring-Nightreign`.
2. Browse the sidebar to find your topic (Builds, Nightlords, Tier Lists, etc.).
3. Read the page: ranking + bullet points of priority.
4. For Nightfarer specific: nav to its page and follow the proposed build order.
5. For boss: weakness chart + recommended approach.

## Honest limitations

**Rigid format**: tables and bullet points can feel dry. If you value elaborate prose, Mobalytics wins.

**Update cycle**: Game8 updates but sometimes takes 1-2 weeks to reflect balance changes in every page.

**Opinionated tier list**: ranking reflects staff perspective; community sometimes disagrees.

**Incremental DLC coverage**: Forsaken Hollows is covered but some specific items lag.

**SEO-heavy titles**: some titles have notable keyword stuffing.

## How to start

1. Visit `game8.co/games/Elden-Ring-Nightreign`.
2. Find the "Beginner Guide" for general context.
3. Pick your Nightfarer and read its build guide.
4. For your next Nightlord run, read the matching weakness chart.
5. Cross-check tier lists with Mobalytics and the subreddit for validation.
