## What it is

Fextralife Nightreign Wiki is the Nightreign version of the wiki Fextralife maintains for Souls games. Launched alongside the game's release (May 2025) and updated actively. Covers all 8 base Nightfarers + the 2 from Forsaken Hollows DLC, every Nightlord, relics, weapons, and consumables.

Ad-supported like all of the Fextralife network. No paywall.

## What it solves

Nightreign has many new mechanics that don't exist in base Elden Ring: relic stat rolls, Nightfarer ultimates, map seed mechanics, Nightlord weakness charts. Fextralife consolidates this info into dedicated pages with community comments where players leave insights and workarounds.

It also solves Nightreign quest tracking: NPC quests (shorter than base ER but present) have branching and specific rewards.

## Differentiation

Against Game8 it wins on community comment coverage and per-item pages. Game8 is more "guide-y" (priority order, recommendations); Fextralife is more "wiki-y" (each item on its own page).

Against Mobalytics: Fextralife has more facts; Mobalytics has more editorial.

## What people use it for

**Relic lookup by name**: each relic has its page with possible stat rolls and location notes.

**Nightlord moveset breakdown**: each Nightlord has a page with attacks, openings, and community tips.

**Per-Nightfarer guides**: skill orders, ultimate timing, build recommendations.

**Map node identification**: per-landmark pages with what loot drops.

**Community comments**: undocumented tips, glitches, workarounds.

## Who shouldn't use it

If ads bother you, Fextralife is ad-heavy. Game8 has fewer ads.

If you want opinionated tier lists, Mobalytics has better editorial.

If you want raw datamined data, Nightreign Calculator is more direct.

## How it's used in practice

1. Visit `eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com`.
2. Use the search bar for specific item/Nightlord/Nightfarer.
3. Read the page with stats, location, drops.
4. Scroll to community comments — important tips are there.
5. For lists (all relics, all Nightfarers), use the sidebar.
6. Consider an ad blocker for clean reading.

## Honest limitations

**Aggressive ads**: autoplay video, popups, banners. Frustrating for long reading.

**Variable page quality**: items recently added with the DLC have less detail than base-game items.

**Poor mobile reading**: ads cover too much screen.

**No tier lists**: not the wiki's strength — for that, Mobalytics or community Reddit posts.

## How to start

1. Visit `eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com`.
2. Read the pages of the 8 Nightfarers to understand the roster.
3. For your favorite Nightfarer, read its page + community comments.
4. Install an ad blocker.
5. Cross-reference with Mobalytics when you need editorial recommendations.
