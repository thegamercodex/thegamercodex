## What it is

It's the official ELDEN RING Discord extended in 2025 to include Nightreign channels. Run by Bandai Namco and FromSoftware, with community managers posting official announcements. There's a Nightreign-specific cluster with channels for LFG, per-Nightfarer builds, Nightlord discussion, and meta updates.

## What it solves

Nightreign has automatic matchmaking but wait times fluctuate by hour and region. For Expeditions with specific compositions (e.g., "looking for Recluse and Duchess for Caligo NG+3"), LFG is faster and lets you coordinate.

It also centralizes official info: balance patches, Forsaken Hollows DLC announcements, community events.

## What people use it for

**LFG by Nightlord**: post in the target Nightlord's thread to assemble a party fast.

**Per-Nightfarer builds**: separate channels per character with meta discussion and screenshots.

**Coordination of specific runs**: NG+3, no-relic challenges, speedrun attempts.

**Official announcements**: Nightreign patches and DLC news.

## Who shouldn't use it

If you play solo only (no party), Discord doesn't add much — solo Nightreign is viable but the Discord ecosystem is centered on co-op.

If you play offline on console, LFG channels don't apply.

## How it's used in practice

1. Click the invite (`discord.com/invite/eldenring`).
2. Accept rules and pick "Nightreign" role in the role-picker.
3. Find the Nightreign cluster.
4. For LFG, post in your platform's channel with: target Nightlord, NG level, preferred Nightfarers.
5. Other players respond with matchmaking codes.

## Honest limitations

**Shared with base ER**: most of the Discord is Elden Ring; the Nightreign channels are a section, not the focus.

**High noise**: with 500K+ total members, general channels scroll fast.

**Post-DLC spoiler rules**: with Forsaken Hollows, there's a strict spoiler policy until the grace period passes.

## How to start

1. Click `discord.com/invite/eldenring`.
2. Accept rules, complete role-picker (including Nightreign role).
3. Go to Nightreign cluster channels.
4. Post in LFG with clear info to assemble a party quickly.
