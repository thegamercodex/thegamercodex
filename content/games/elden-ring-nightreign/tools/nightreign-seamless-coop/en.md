## What it is

Seamless Co-op (Nightreign) is LukeYui's mod adapted to Elden Ring Nightreign. Distributed on Nexus Mods at `nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/3`. Open-source, free, requires offline mode.

Released shortly after Nightreign's launch in 2025 and maintained by the Seamless team that also maintains the base ER version.

## What it solves

Vanilla Nightreign caps parties at 3. For larger friend groups (4-6 regulars), this means rotating who plays. Seamless Co-op extends party size and improves in-game coordination.

It also allows unrestricted duos — vanilla matchmaking favors trios and duos have mechanical disadvantages. The mod allows balanced duo runs.

## What people use it for

**4-6 friend parties**: extending past the vanilla limit.

**Balanced duo runs**: playing with a friend without matchmaking restrictions.

**Improved coordination**: the mod includes voice chat helpers and position sharing.

**SOTE in large groups**: the DLC feels more manageable with more players (configurable difficulty).

**Community events**: speedruns and challenges with larger groups.

## Who shouldn't use it

Console doesn't support mods — PC only.

If you want vanilla multiplayer with randoms, the mod forces closed groups (passwords).

If you value Nightreign's "intended difficulty" with 3, the extra party size can trivialize fights.

## How it's used in practice

1. Make sure Nightreign is installed on PC.
2. Download Seamless Co-op from `nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/3`.
3. Follow the README to extract files and configure.
4. Define a shared password for your group in the config.
5. Launch with the dedicated launcher.
6. Your group uses the same password and connects automatically.

## Honest limitations

**Version-locked**: when Bandai patches Nightreign, the mod takes 1-7 days to update.

**Offline only**: your progress stays separate from vanilla online.

**Debated difficulty scaling**: with 6 players, some Nightlords trivialize; with balanced duos, others are brutal.

**More complex setup than vanilla**: requires reading the README.

**Occasional quest bugs**: NPC interactions in large groups can glitch.

## How to start

1. Visit `nexusmods.com/eldenringnightreign/mods/3`.
2. Download the latest version (verify compatibility with your patch).
3. Follow the detailed README — includes a dedicated launcher.
4. Coordinate with friends to use the exact same version.
5. Join the Seamless Discord (link on the mod page) for troubleshooting.
