Nightreign compresses the Elden Ring formula into cooperative, timed expeditions, where every decision —which character to play, which relics to equip, which route to take— weighs heavily in just a few minutes. The margin for error is small and the game explains almost nothing. This five-tool stack covers the preparation and execution of a run.

## Before and during the expedition

Preparation starts with **Mobalytics Nightreign**: Nightfarer guides and tier lists that tell you which character is best, what build to run, and how to approach each boss. To squeeze your character, **Relics Pro** helps you plan and optimize relics, which are the heart of your passives. And when you're torn between two setups, **Nightreign Calculator** compares them with real stat and damage numbers.

During the run, time rules. **Nightreign Map Router** gives you optimal routes per expedition to maximize runes and reach the final boss as prepared as possible. And if you need to look something up —an item, a mechanic, a boss— **Fextralife** is the reference wiki.

## Why these five

Each covers a part of the expedition:

- **Guides and characters** → Mobalytics Nightreign
- **Relic optimization** → Relics Pro
- **Damage calculation** → Nightreign Calculator
- **Map routes** → Nightreign Map Router
- **Reference** → Fextralife

You don't need all five to play, but together they turn an improvised expedition into a run with a plan. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
