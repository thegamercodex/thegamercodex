title: Seamless Co-op vs Fun is Allowed — which Nightreign multiplayer mod to install
description: Comparison between the mod that extends party size (Seamless Co-op) and the rebalance for solo/duo (Fun is Allowed). Different audiences.
---

These two mods tackle opposite Nightreign multiplayer problems. **Seamless Co-op** extends the party limit past 3, ideal for larger groups. **Fun is Allowed** rebalances for solo/duo viability — ideal when you don't have a 3-player party.

## Different problems

Seamless: "My group has 5 friends but vanilla caps at 3 — I want to play together."

Fun is Allowed: "I play solo or duo and Nightlords are brutal — I want balance that respects but doesn't frustrate."

Disjoint audiences. If you always play in a trio, neither applies.

## Compatibility

Both are offline-only (anti-cheat). Both can be combined — Fun is Allowed works on top of Seamless Co-op for custom group sizes (4-5) with adjusted balance.

## When each wins

| Scenario | Best option |
|---|---|
| Party of 4-6 friends | Seamless Co-op |
| Viable solo runs | Fun is Allowed |
| Balanced duo | Fun is Allowed |
| Closed groups via password | Seamless Co-op |
| Custom 4-5 group with balance | Both combined |

## Verdict

**They're not alternatives — they're complementary or exclusive based on your setup**. Decide based on your usual group size. If you play solo: Fun is Allowed. If you play in large groups: Seamless. If you play duo: Fun is Allowed (better balance) or Seamless (more casual).
