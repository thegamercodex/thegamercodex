title: HoYoLAB Map vs AppSample — which Genshin interactive map to use
description: Short comparison between HoYoverse's official map and the alternative community map. Update speed, login requirement, UI, and when each one fits.
---

Both maps mark collectibles, oculi, chests, materials, and NPCs on the Teyvat map, but they come from different teams. **HoYoLAB Map** is the official map maintained by HoYoverse — updated each patch directly from the devs' data. **AppSample** is an alternative community map focused on a minimalist UI and persistent tracking without login.

## Update speed and day-one coverage

Here HoYoLAB wins outright. When a new region releases (Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan), HoYoverse publishes the map with every node marked on release day. It's the only product with this level of day-one freshness — because it comes from the studio that built the game.

AppSample and other community maps typically take **days to weeks** to fully map new regions. Volunteers have to play the region, find every collectible, and report it to the map. Delay is unavoidable.

If you're exploring a freshly released region, HoYoLAB Map is the obvious pick.

## Progress tracking

Here the situation flips. HoYoLAB requires a HoYoverse account login to save progress, and tracking syncs with your UID — useful but adds friction.

AppSample stores your progress in **browser local storage**: mark a chest as collected, it stays marked, no account or sync. Trade-off: if you switch browsers or clear cache, you lose progress. But for a typical exploration session or weekly farming run, no login required and it's faster.

## UI and performance

AppSample has a lighter UI. Clean layout, few elements, focus on the map. Loads fast on slow connections or on mobile.

HoYoLAB Map lives integrated in the HoYoLAB platform, which also hosts daily check-in, forums, events, and battle chronicle. This makes it heavier and sometimes slower. The UI has more elements competing for attention.

For someone who just wants to "open the map, look for oculus, close it", AppSample is more direct.

## Lore and official context

HoYoLAB Map has an editorial edge: as an official product, it can include narrative context, related lore, and links to official wikis that community products don't have. For players who enjoy lore as part of exploration, this detail matters.

AppSample marks locations but doesn't dive into narrative context.

## Languages

HoYoLAB Map is available in Spanish. AppSample is primarily English with some partial translations.

For Spanish speakers this can be decisive, especially in new regions where item, NPC, and location names haven't been standardized by the community yet.

## Which to use

- **Exploring a new region on release day** → HoYoLAB Map. Unbeatable day-one coverage.
- **Persistent tracking without login** → AppSample. No account, no sync.
- **Fast minimalist UI** → AppSample. More direct.
- **You want lore and official context** → HoYoLAB Map.
- **Spanish speaker exploring new regions** → HoYoLAB Map. More complete localization.
- **Fast weekly-boss farming session** → AppSample. Less friction.

Both fulfill the basic role of marking nodes on the map. The choice depends on what you prioritize: day-one updates (HoYoLAB) or speed of access (AppSample).
