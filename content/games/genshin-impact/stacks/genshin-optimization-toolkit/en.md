Genshin Impact is generous with casual players, but the endgame —Abyss, Theater, max damage— rewards optimization. And optimizing by eye is impossible: with five artifact slots, dozens of stats, and four-character teams, the combinations are astronomical. This six-tool stack solves that end to end, without making you trust your gut.

## The optimization workflow

Everything revolves around **Genshin Optimizer**: you import your artifacts and it tells you, mathematically, which combination maximizes each character's damage. It's the core. But before optimizing a character it helps to know who they'll play with: **KQM Team Compendium** gives you the theorycrafted teams —which pieces combine and why— so you don't optimize in a vacuum.

Once you have a team in mind, **gcsim** simulates the full rotation and gives you a realistic DPS number, ideal for comparing two teams before spending resin on either. To know if your result is good, **Akasha System** shows damage leaderboards: how much that character hits with builds similar to yours across the community.

Two tools close the loop: **Enka.Network** reads your in-game profile and shows your builds without you having to take screenshots (handy for sharing and for Genshin Optimizer to import), and **Seelie.me** builds your farming plan —materials and ascension— to reach the builds you planned without wasting resources.

## Why these six

Each covers a distinct stage of the process:

- **Build optimization** → Genshin Optimizer
- **Team composition** → KQM Team Compendium
- **DPS simulation** → gcsim
- **Reference and comparison** → Akasha System
- **Showcase and import** → Enka.Network
- **Farming planning** → Seelie.me

You don't need all six to enjoy the game, but together they're the standard set for the player who wants to squeeze their account. Each one has its full analysis in the codex.
