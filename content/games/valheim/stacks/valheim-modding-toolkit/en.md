Valheim shines in co-op and building, but the base game falls short on quality of life, and its large bases are tedious to raise block by block. The community solved both with mods and external tools. This six-tool stack covers the base of a modded setup plus the building and exploration utilities most groups end up using.

## The modded base

The entry point is **r2modman**: a profile-based manager that installs, updates, and isolates your mods without dirtying the game folder, ideal because you can keep a separate profile per server. It downloads everything from **Thunderstore**, Valheim's main mod repository. And underneath, **BepInEx** is the framework nearly every mod requires: without it, nothing loads.

## Building and exploration

With the base ready, two tools elevate the game. **PlanBuild** lets you design complex builds and paste them into the world, turning an hours-long base into something manageable. And **Valheim World Generator** previews your seed before you start: where the biomes, bosses, and points of interest are, so you can plan where to sail.

Rounding it out, the **Valheim Wiki** is the encyclopedia: recipes, materials, bosses, and mechanics, so you don't farm blindly or waste time guessing what you need.

## Why these six

They cover both sides of "improved" Valheim:

- **Modded base** → r2modman + Thunderstore + BepInEx
- **Building** → PlanBuild
- **Exploration** → Valheim World Generator
- **Reference** → Valheim Wiki

You don't need mods to enjoy Valheim, but this set is what most long-term groups use. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
