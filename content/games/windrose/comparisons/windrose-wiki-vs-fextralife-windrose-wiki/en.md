Windrose's two main wikis have distinct editorial purposes. **Windrose Wiki (windrose.wiki)** is community-editable, optimized for textual lookup with filters. **Fextralife Windrose Wiki** follows the same publisher's souls-like wiki pattern, focused on combat depth, boss strategies, and documented archetypes.

## How each is organized

Windrose Wiki structures info by type (weapons, armor, items, NPCs, quests, recipes) with side filters that cross attributes (source, level requirement, drop chance). The expected flow: open the search bar, enter a name, land on a page with structured info and cross-links. Ideal for concrete questions like "what does this item do" or "where does X drop".

Fextralife uses the format honed over a decade of souls-like wikis. Each boss has its own page with detailed strategy (attack patterns, weak windows, recommended gear). Each weapon has scaling tables per stat. Each build archetype has its narrative prose with target stats. Ideal for tactical questions like "how to defeat Israel Hands" or "what scaling does Rapier of Devastation have".

## Track record vs freshness

Fextralife has accumulated authority — players who trust their Elden Ring/DS3/Lords of the Fallen wikis come to Windrose already knowing how to navigate the layout. That familiarity is real value for the souls-like reader.

Windrose Wiki is born fresh with Windrose. No positive or negative legacy. Its advantage is that the editorial team is focused on Windrose specifically, not spread thin across 50 simultaneous wikis.

## Update cadence and data quality

Both depend on community contributions and both lag Windrose's patch cycle. Difference: Windrose Wiki has a lower barrier to contribute (free account + click Edit), which accelerates fixes of outdated info. Fextralife has a more controlled editorial flow — more formal consistency, less update speed.

In the first month of EA practice, both have gaps. Windrose Wiki on obscure pages (rare endgame items). Fextralife on technical pages that require an active assigned editor.

## The ads factor

Fextralife always had high ad density. Without an adblocker, the experience degrades — pop-ups, video ads, anchored banners taking screen space. It's a known publisher tradeoff but worth mentioning for players who mind it.

Windrose Wiki has ads but notably less intrusive. Entering without an adblocker, the experience is tolerable.

## When to use each

| Question | Recommended wiki |
|---|---|
| What does this item do? | Windrose Wiki |
| Where is this NPC / quest? | Windrose Wiki |
| How do I defeat this boss? | Fextralife |
| What scaling does this weapon have? | Fextralife |
| How do I craft X? | Windrose Wiki |
| What build archetype to follow? | Fextralife |
| Want to contribute info | Windrose Wiki |
| Want the souls-like wiki style I know | Fextralife |

Not mutually exclusive. Many players bookmark both and pick based on the question at hand.
