Windrose combines character progression with an active mod ecosystem, and as in any game with that mix, external tools make the difference between playing blind and playing with a plan. This five-tool stack gathers the essentials for planning your build, looking up data, and keeping a tidy mod setup.

## How you use it

To build your character wisely, **Windrose Tools** offers the planning and build tools that let you design your setup before investing in the game. When you need concrete data —which item, which stats, what to chase— **Windrose DB** is the database, and the **Windrose Wiki** is the reference with mechanics, content, and guides for specific questions.

On the mod side, **Windrose Mod Manager** is the dedicated manager: it installs, updates, and organizes your mods without you touching files by hand. And **Nexus Mods** is the community hub, where the quality-of-life content and extra tweaks come from.

## Why these five

Each covers a layer of the game:

- **Mod management** → Windrose Mod Manager
- **Build planning** → Windrose Tools
- **Database** → Windrose DB
- **Reference** → Windrose Wiki
- **Mod hub** → Nexus Mods

Being a niche game, its ecosystem keeps growing, but these five already cover the essentials to organize and customize your experience. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
