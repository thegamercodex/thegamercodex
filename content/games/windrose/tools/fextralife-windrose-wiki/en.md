## What it is

Fextralife is a wiki publisher specialized in souls-likes, active for over a decade. When Windrose launched with soulslike combat, Fextralife opened its dedicated wiki immediately. The structure follows their known pattern: bosses have a page with strategy, weak points, drops, and fight scripts; weapons have scaling tables and movesets; builds have documented archetypes with stats and suggested gear.

## What problem it solves

If Windrose Wiki covers "what is this item", Fextralife covers "how to defeat this boss". The real gap it fills is the strategic prose of combat: instead of listing stats, it tells you which boss attacks are punishable, what timing it uses, what armor types resist what damage type. For players from the souls-like school, this format is directly useful.

## Differentiation

- **Windrose Wiki (wiki.org)** is lookup-first, neutral. Fextralife is combat-first and opinionated.
- **Windrose.tools** has no strategic prose, only interactive data.
- Fextralife brings the track record: players who trust their Elden Ring/DS3 wikis already know what to expect.

## What people use it for

- **Prepare fights against specific bosses**: read the boss page before the attempt.
- **Compare weapons via scaling tables**: see what stat scaling a weapon has and predict its damage curve.
- **Look up complete builds with stats and gear**: documented archetypes (Dexterity Fencer, Strength Crusher, etc.)
- **Moveset reference**: when a new enemy resists you, read their moveset breakdown.
- **Lore digestion**: Fextralife usually adds lore notes to NPCs and items, useful for narrative-driven players.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you only want fast factual lookup (what X drops), Windrose Wiki is more efficient — less prose, more data. If you want interactive features (planner, map), this doesn't have them. If you're bothered by ad density (Fextralife always had aggressive ads), bookmark with adblocker or switch to Windrose Wiki.

## How it's used in practice

1. Open [windrose.wiki.fextralife.com](https://windrose.wiki.fextralife.com).
2. For bosses: side nav → "Bosses", pick the boss, read the "Strategies" section.
3. For weapons: side nav → "Weapons", filter by type, open the page to see scaling.
4. For builds: side nav → "Builds", pick an archetype to see target stats and gear progression.
5. For quick reference: use Ctrl+F on a long page to find specific terms.

## Honest limitations

- **Aggressive ads**: Fextralife always had high ad density. Without an adblocker, the experience degrades significantly.
- **Update cadence depends on the assigned editor**: some pages update fast, others go stale for weeks. Large Fextralife wikis suffer from this pattern.
- **English only**: no localizations.
- **Opinionated build archetypes**: recommendations reflect the editor's bias. May not match the community-wide meta.

## How to get started

Open [windrose.wiki.fextralife.com](https://windrose.wiki.fextralife.com). If you're coming with no context, start with "Combat" (basic mechanics) and "Beginner Guide" (on the home). For sessions where you're stuck on a boss, search the boss name on Google + "fextralife" — SEO takes you directly to the relevant page.
