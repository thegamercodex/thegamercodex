In Apex Legends, much of your improvement happens outside the match: having the right sensitivity, picking the right loadout, and understanding your own stats. The game gives you none of those layers. This five-tool stack adds them and covers both preparation and tracking your progress.

## Setup and preparation

Before competing seriously, it pays to nail the basics. **Prosettings** shows you pros' configuration, sensitivity, and setup, so you start from a proven baseline instead of guessing your own. And if you come from another shooter, **Sens Converter** translates your sensitivity so you keep the same muscle memory in Apex instead of starting from scratch.

For combat decisions, the **DryBear TTK Calculator** computes each weapon's time-to-kill, which helps you build the most efficient loadout for the range you fight at. And **Apex Legends Status** gives you the live game state: ranked and pubs map rotation and a prediction of which map is next, useful for deciding when to play.

## Tracking

After playing, **Apex Tracker** gives you the picture: match history, stats by legend and weapon, and how your rank evolves across the season, so you can see whether your adjustments are working.

## Why these five

Each covers a part of the cycle:

- **Pro setup** → Prosettings
- **Sensitivity** → Sens Converter
- **Loadout decisions** → DryBear TTK Calculator
- **Status and maps** → Apex Legends Status
- **Stat tracking** → Apex Tracker

This isn't a stack for relaxed play; it's for the player who wants to climb with method. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
