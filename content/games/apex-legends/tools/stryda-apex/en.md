## What it is

Stryda.gg is a multi-game tracker with a different angle: instead of just showing stats, it assigns concrete challenges (daily, weekly, monthly) targeting measurable improvement habits. "Land 3 headshots with a Wingman in your next match", "Win 2 ranked games this week", "Hit 1500 damage in a single match". Each completed challenge gives XP and badges. Core stats also available. Free with optional Premium for more custom challenges.

## What problem it solves

Traditional stats trackers assume the player is disciplined: checks stats, identifies weakness, practices deliberately. Reality is most players check stats once, scroll, and go back to playing the same. Stryda inserts a concrete loop: challenges turn "I have low accuracy" into "this week, 50 headshots with weapon X". The goal framing over the data framing sustains engagement.

## Differentiation

- **vs apex-tracker-gg / Blitz**: both are stats-data-first. Stryda is goals-first with stats as secondary.
- **vs in-game achievement systems**: in-game achievements are fixed and rarely actionable. Stryda's are dynamic, adapted to your skill level and current meta.
- **Esports tournament integration**: Stryda runs community-organized tournaments with leaderboards based on challenge completion. It's a differentiator but also a niche.

## What people use it for

- **Maintain motivation during rank grinding**: challenges give visible progress even if you don't tier up that week.
- **Identify focused weakness**: if you repeatedly fail accuracy challenges, you know what to practice.
- **Compete with friends on challenges**: compare who completed more weekly challenges among the crew.
- **Tournament participation**: Stryda runs community tournaments where placements depend on challenges.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you want raw data without gamification, tracker.gg or ALS are more efficient. If badges/XP/level systems in gaming bother you, you'll find Stryda excessively "gamified".

## How it's used in practice

1. Free account at [stryda.gg](https://stryda.gg).
2. Link your Apex account (Origin/Steam/etc.) for auto-tracking.
3. Each login, the dashboard shows your active challenges with progress bars.
4. Play Apex normally — Stryda detects automatically and tracks progress.
5. Complete challenges to earn XP, level up, and unlock badges. Some weekly challenges give in-platform rewards.

## Honest limitations

- **Challenges sometimes feel arbitrary**: "Get 5 wins with Bangalore" when you don't play Bangalore is awkward. Stryda tries to personalize but sometimes misses.
- **Less data depth than tracker.gg**: leaderboard depth and session analysis is notably weaker.
- **Aggressive Premium upsell**: free tier has 3 active challenges; Premium adds more. Frequent upgrade notifications.
- **Smaller community vs competitors**: fewer players → less signal in leaderboards and tournaments.
- **Match detection occasionally fails**: if you play non-ranked custom games, they sometimes don't count.

## How to get started

Free account at [stryda.gg](https://stryda.gg), link Apex, leave auto-tracking on. For the first 3-4 days, ignore challenges and let Stryda calibrate your skill level — its first-week challenges are usually too easy to accommodate the algorithm. After that, challenges become adaptive and start being a real push.
