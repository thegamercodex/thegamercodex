## What it is

Blitz.gg is a multi-game platform focused on overlay tools and post-match analysis, supporting Apex since 2020. The Windows app runs in the background, detects your Apex launch, and overlays live stats during the match — kills, damage, accuracy in real-time plus teammate and enemy comparisons at the start of each game. Web companion (blitz.gg/apex) for post-match breakdowns and history. Free with optional Premium tier for advanced features.

## What problem it solves

Apex has a brief, limited end-of-match summary. Blitz gives you the deep dive: damage by weapon, accuracy per gun, time-to-kill per engagement, K/D fight by fight. For players who want post-session coaching, the data is all there. More importantly: the live overlay lets you see enemy and teammate stats at match start — useful for spotting smurfs or pros in your lobby before committing to an engage.

## Differentiation

- **vs apex-tracker-gg + Overwolf**: Tracker.gg requires Overwolf as wrapper. Blitz has its own standalone app. Blitz integrates more features in one app (overlay + post-match + sensitivity converter). Tracker.gg has better historical leaderboards.
- **vs Stryda.gg**: Stryda has challenge gamification. Blitz is pure overlay + analytics, more professional and less game-like.
- **vs apex-legends-status**: ALS has no overlay, only web. Blitz covers overlay where ALS doesn't.

## What people use it for

- **Live overlay during match**: see enemy stats at spawn, accuracy and kill counter in HUD during combat.
- **Post-match coaching**: per-weapon breakdown, accuracy heatmap, identify exact mistake moments.
- **Compare with trio teammates**: at-a-glance see who is performing best on KD and damage per session.
- **Track sensitivity and aim across patches**: Blitz has a built-in sens converter useful when changing monitor or mouse.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you play casually and don't want a background app running, don't install it — overlays distract casual players. If you only need meta-level data (not your-stats data), apexlegendsstatus is lighter.

## How it's used in practice

1. Download Blitz from [blitz.gg](https://blitz.gg) — Windows-only installer.
2. Install and launch the app. Log in with email or Steam/Google.
3. In settings, link your Apex account (Origin/Steam) for auto-detect.
4. Launch Apex normally — Blitz detects it automatically and activates the overlay.
5. Post-match: the app opens a browser tab automatically with a detailed summary.

## Honest limitations

- **Windows-only**: no Mac/Linux builds. If you play Apex via Steam Play / Proton, it doesn't work.
- **Background resource usage**: the app consumes ~200MB RAM constantly. Players with modest setups notice it.
- **Privacy concerns**: it reads player names in your lobby, which some players consider invasive. Riot/EA servers don't tolerate tools scraping in-game data, although Blitz has historically been OK with ToS.
- **Free tier with upsells**: periodic nag screens asking to upgrade to Premium ($8/mo).
- **App update cadence depends on Blitz's team**: when Apex patches, the overlay can break temporarily.

## How to get started

Download from [blitz.gg](https://blitz.gg), install. First time takes 5 minutes to set up (login, link account, configure overlay positions). After that it's transparent — you open Apex and Blitz does its work in the background. If after 2 weeks you don't use it, uninstall — there's no cost to keeping it but also no value if you don't use it.
