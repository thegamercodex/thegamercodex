title: ProSettings vs SensConverter — full DB vs cross-game converter
description: Short comparison between Apex's two main pro settings tools. Large DB with detailed gear vs cross-game converter with role filters. Apex-only focus vs multi-FPS workflow.
---

Apex's two serious pro settings tools overlap but with different focus. **ProSettings.com** is the established DB with 100+ pros granularly cataloged, full gear list included. **SensConverter.app** is calculator + smaller but role-searchable DB, with unique value in converting sens between Apex and other FPS.

## DB depth

ProSettings catalogs 100+ pros with granular detail: in-game sens, DPI, calculated eDPI, per-scope ADS multiplier, FOV, custom keybinds, crosshair color, mouse model, mousepad, monitor specs, headset, chair. To verify a specific pro's setup in particular, ProSettings is first stop.

SensConverter covers 50+ pros — smaller quantity, but with filters by role (IGL, entry, support) and region (NA/EU/APAC/Latam) that ProSettings lacks. Useful if you want to analyze patterns within roles.

## SensConverter's unique value

The cross-game converter is the killer feature. If you play Apex and also Valorant/CS2, maintaining mechanical consistency across games is critical. SensConverter takes sens and DPI from a game and outputs the exact Apex equivalent maintaining cm/360°.

ProSettings doesn't replicate this — it only catalogs static configs, no cross-game conversion math.

## ProSettings's unique value

Extensive gear list. For players planning to buy gaming mouse, monitor, or pad, ProSettings is valuable research — you see what hardware each pro uses and common patterns (800 DPI is standard, certain mousepads predominate, typical monitor refresh rates).

SensConverter covers some gear but with less detail.

## When each wins

| Case | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Copy a pro's full config | ProSettings |
| Convert sens Apex↔other FPS | SensConverter |
| Pre-purchase gear research | ProSettings |
| Filter pros by role | SensConverter |
| Quick eDPI calc | SensConverter |
| Verify a specific config | ProSettings |

## Recommendation

Both bookmarked. ProSettings as the main reference DB, SensConverter for sens math and quick role filtering. Having both costs zero — both are free without account requirement for basic use.

For a player who only plays Apex without other FPS, ProSettings is enough. For a multi-FPS player, SensConverter is nearly indispensable due to the cross-game converter.

Shared limitations: both English-only, both require manual application of settings (no auto-import to Apex), both depend on pro track records — emerging or newcomer pros take time to appear in either DB.
