Apex's two serious weapon math tools represent two generations of the community. **ApexSeasons** is the modern web tool launched post-2023 with visual UX and granular filters. **Drybear's TTK Calculator** is the Google Sheet built by Christopher Mykles in February 2019, the historic authoritative source the community has used as reference since day one.

## Modern UX vs raw spreadsheet

ApexSeasons.online tools/weapon-stats is web UI: filterable tables, side filter by armor tier (white/blue/purple/red), side-by-side comparison view, visual charts. Designed for quick in-session lookups — 30 seconds to find "which weapon kills fastest against purple armor".

Drybear is Google Sheets: multiple tabs (by weapon class), visible formulas, exposed math. For quick lookup it's slower — you navigate tabs, read cells. But the math is bare — you can Make a Copy and customize for edge cases.

## Authority and community trust

Drybear is cited in Reddit posts, video guides, and balance discussions since 2019. "Source: Drybear TTK Calc" is shorthand the competitive community recognizes. Legacy credibility newer tools don't replicate easily.

ApexSeasons is solid but less established. Editorial team maintained vs single creator (Drybear), which gives more update reliability but less individual brand.

## Update cadence

ApexSeasons: editorial team with consistent post-patch cadence, usually 1-3 days to reflect balance changes.

Drybear: single maintainer. Christopher Mykles has projects besides the Sheet, so post-patch lag can be 1-2 weeks or more for minor changes.

For fresh post-patch data, ApexSeasons wins.

## Customization

Drybear lets you Make a Copy of the Sheet and edit formulas, attachments, modifiers manually. Useful for theorycrafters wanting to explore "what would happen if they nerf X stat by Y%" or calculate ability multipliers the base spreadsheet doesn't include.

ApexSeasons doesn't expose formulas directly — filters are the limits of what you can query.

## When each wins

| Case | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Quick TTK against armor tier | ApexSeasons |
| Verify a Reddit-cited claim | Drybear |
| Visual 2-3 weapon comparison | ApexSeasons |
| Customize math with rare modifiers | Drybear |
| Fresh post-patch update | ApexSeasons |
| Authoritative source for balance debate | Drybear |
| Mobile quick lookup | ApexSeasons |
| Deep theorycrafting | Drybear |

## Recommendation

For daily use, ApexSeasons as default for UX. For deep-dive theorycrafting or when you need to cite an authoritative source, Drybear stays as complementary bookmark.

Bookmarking both costs nothing. Choosing between one or the other depends on the moment and question — they're not direct substitutes.
