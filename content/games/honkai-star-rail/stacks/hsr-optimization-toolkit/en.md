Honkai: Star Rail rewards optimization in its endgame —Memory of Chaos, Pure Fiction, Apocalyptic Shadow— but doing it by eye is impossible: between relics, light cones, traces, and four-character teams, the combinations explode. This six-tool stack solves that end to end, without making you trust your gut.

## The optimization workflow

The core is **Fribbels HSR Optimizer**: you import your relics and it tells you, mathematically, which combination maximizes each character. But before optimizing it helps to know who they play with and what to prioritize: **Prydwen** gives you the character guides and tier lists —recommended relics, light cones, and teams.

To understand the why behind those recommendations, **KQM** brings the deep theorycraft: mechanics, math, and detailed guides. And when you're torn between two setups, the **MD-C Calculator** compares them with real damage numbers instead of by eye.

Two tools close the loop: **HoYoLAB** is the official layer —account progress, daily check-in, and data synced from the game— and **Enka.Network (HSR)** shows your builds from the in-game profile, useful for sharing them and for importing them into the optimizer.

## Why these six

Each covers a stage of the process:

- **Relic optimization** → Fribbels HSR Optimizer
- **Guides and tier lists** → Prydwen
- **Deep theorycraft** → KQM
- **Damage calculation** → MD-C Calculator
- **Official account** → HoYoLAB
- **Showcase and import** → Enka.Network

You don't need all six to enjoy the game, but together they're the standard set for the player who wants to squeeze their account. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
