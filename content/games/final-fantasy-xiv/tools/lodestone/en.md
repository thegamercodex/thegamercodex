## What it is

The Lodestone (na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone) is the official FFXIV web portal maintained by Square Enix. It's been around since the original 2010 launch and survived the reboot. Main functions:

- **Character profile**: your public Lodestone ID with achievements, current visible gear, mounts/minions owned (partial).
- **Free Company search**: directory of FCs with open recruitment, filters by server and datacenter.
- **Linkshell search** (more limited community use).
- **Character search**: find another player by name + server.
- **News & Patch Notes**: official Square Enix feed with complete patch notes, hotfixes, announced maintenance, and Lodestone-only stories.
- **Eorzea Database**: officially documented items, recipes, and NPCs (more limited than Garland Tools but canonical).

Free and login-free for most functions (login required to post on official forums or configure your profile).

## What problem it solves

For FFXIV, third-party tools (FFLogs, Etro, Universalis) depend on the Lodestone as source of truth — but there are flows where no third-party is reliable enough and you need the Lodestone directly:

- **Verify a player's achievement**: the Lodestone shows visible achievements and titles. Useful for applying to a static where "Ultimate Legend" or "World First" titles are required.
- **Confirm patch dates and maintenance schedules**: official news is the only authoritative channel. Third-party data may be lagging.
- **Free Company recruitment**: the official directory remains the channel where people search for FCs.
- **Cite official info**: when there's a debate about drop rate, exact gear ilvl, etc. — the Lodestone is the source that ends the argument.

## How it differs from Gamerescape and Garland Tools

- **Lodestone**: official, authoritative, but limited in browseable data (you can't easily filter items by sub-stat).
- **Gamerescape**: community-edited wiki with deep lore, quests, dungeon walkthroughs.
- **Garland Tools**: database with granular filters for items, recipes, gathering nodes.

All three complement each other: Lodestone for news and character verification, Gamerescape for deep-dive into quests/lore, Garland Tools for technical queries on items.

## What people actually use it for

**Read patch notes**: official patch notes publish on Lodestone before appearing in any third-party news. If you want the literal changelog of a balance change, you go here.

**Verify achievements and titles**: you're considering someone for your static. Search their name on Lodestone, see visible achievements, maxed jobs, and mounts.

**Free Company hunting**: filter by datacenter + your timezone + recent activity, get a list of recruiting FCs.

**Confirm news date / event start**: when a seasonal event like Heavensturn starts, the exact date is on Lodestone. Don't rely solely on third-party news.

**Show your character to a friend**: the Lodestone link is the standard way to show your char outside the game.

## Who it's NOT for

- **Combat analysis**: Lodestone has no parser or logs. That's FFLogs.
- **Deep database queries**: for "which items drop from the Lich + their sub-stats", Lodestone is poor. Garland Tools is much better.
- **Comparisons and planning**: Lodestone only shows current char state. You don't plan BiS here.
- **Free Trial-only players**: achievements may be limited in visibility for Free Trial chars depending on settings.

## How it's actually used

1. Go to `na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/` (swap `na` for your region: `eu`, `fr`, `de`, `jp`).

2. To verify another player: **Character Search** → enter name + server.

3. To find an FC: **Free Company Search** → filters by datacenter, recruitment tag, members.

4. To read news: the main page lists the latest. Filters for Topics, Updates, Notices, etc.

5. To see your own profile: log in with your Square Enix account, go to "My Page".

6. To post on official forums (rarely useful for community engagement — Discord/Reddit are more active): forums.finalfantasyxiv.com.

## Honest limitations

**Dated UI**: the Lodestone looks like 2010 and navigation is slow. Square Enix prioritizes game features over redoing the web portal.

**Limited visible data**: Lodestone shows a fraction of char state (main gear yes, full inventory no). For deep analysis you use third-party tools.

**Update lag post-patch**: sometimes takes hours for Lodestone to sync your achievements post-clear. Don't expect instant verification.

**No official public API**: third-party tools depend on HTML scraping of Lodestone, which is fragile — when Square Enix changes a selector, tools temporarily break.

**Weak search engine**: searching by specific achievement, particular jobs, or gear ilvl is hard. Primary search is by name + server.

**Not the primary communication channel**: although official, important announcements (Live Letter, balance previews) live on YouTube/Twitter, not in Lodestone forums.

## How to get started

1. Go to [na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/) (or your region).

2. Log in with your Square Enix account → my page. Verify your char is linked.

3. Configure your visible profile (achievements, jobs, etc.) if you want it public.

4. Bookmark it to check patch notes every time a patch drops.

5. To verify another player, **Character Search** → name + server. Compare with their FFLogs/Lodestone link.

6. If you're searching for an FC, **Free Company Search** → adjust filters and check the top options.

7. If your third-party tool (Etro, FFLogs) shows weird data, the Lodestone is the source of truth of what the game officially says.
