---
title: Warframe Market vs Semlar — where to trade and where to evaluate rivens
description: A short comparison between the general player marketplace and the riven-specialized toolbox.
---

Both live in Warframe's economy, but they cover different steps of the flow. **Warframe Market** is where you buy and sell; **Semlar** is where you evaluate a riven before trading it.

## Key difference

**Warframe Market** is the general marketplace: live orders for Prime parts, mods, arcanes, sets and rivens, with whisper templates to close the trade in-game. It's the price source the whole ecosystem references and where the transaction actually happens.

**Semlar** is the specialist. Its Riven Calculator shows you a riven's possible stat range and how good your roll is within it, and its crowd-sourced price guide gives a reference for rivens —the hardest item in the game to value. It's the evaluation step before the trade.

## When to use each

- **Buying or selling any item, mod or set** → Warframe Market. The general marketplace.
- **Evaluating how good a riven's roll is** → Semlar. The Riven Calculator.
- **Pricing a riven before trading it** → Semlar. Its price guide.
- **Checking prices for Prime parts and arcanes** → Warframe Market. Live listings.

## Recommendation

They don't compete: they're used in sequence. When you trade a riven, **Semlar** tells you whether it's worth it and how much to ask, and **Warframe Market** is where you close the deal. For everything else —parts, mods, arcanes— Warframe Market is enough on its own. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
