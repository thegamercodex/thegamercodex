---
title: Overframe vs RE:FRAMED — which Warframe build planner to use
description: A comparison between Warframe's consolidated build standard and the clean, modern alternative for building loadouts.
---

Planning builds in Warframe is nearly mandatory: the modding system is huge and getting it wrong costs formas, endo and credits. Two web tools cover this space: **Overframe**, the dominant build database with community curation, and **RE:FRAMED**, a newer, minimalist loadout builder that bets on a noise-free experience.

## Key difference

**Overframe** is the standard. It has spent years accumulating published builds, has the community's most active voting system and the reference tier lists —including the Helminth ability one used for planning subsumes. After warframe-builder.com died, it became the de-facto successor. In exchange, its interface is loaded: ads, votes, comments, sections. For many that signals community life; for others, noise.

**RE:FRAMED** was born as a tidy alternative. Its builder is clean and modern, without Overframe's clutter, and lets you build, explore and share full loadouts with a more focused experience. The cost is volume: it has far less accumulated community content and considerably lower adoption.

## Volume and curation vs user experience

The real decision is between content mass and tidiness. If what you want is to see how people build a frame, compare variants by votes and find the meta build the moment a new Prime drops, Overframe wins on volume — there's simply more, and more curated. Its Helminth tier list, moreover, has no direct equivalent in RE:FRAMED.

If instead you already know what you want to build and value an interface that doesn't distract you with ads or comment threads, RE:FRAMED feels better. It's an authoring tool more than a community feed.

## When each wins

- **Viewing popular builds and comparing by votes** → Overframe. Volume and community curation.
- **Planning Helminth subsumes** → Overframe. Its tier list is the reference.
- **Building distraction-free, clean interface** → RE:FRAMED. Minimalist design.
- **Sharing a loadout with a link** → RE:FRAMED. Direct authoring flow.
- **Builds for just-released content** → Overframe. It fills faster.

## Combined recommendation

For most people, **Overframe** is the default — it's where the community posts and where you'll find the build you're after. **RE:FRAMED** is worth it as a second tool when Overframe feels cluttered and you want to build something of your own calmly. They don't compete to replace each other: one is the community archive, the other the clean workbench. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
