Both are reference, but they aim at different needs. **WARFRAME Wiki** explains how the game works in human language; **browse.wf** gives you the exact raw values from game data.

## Key difference

**WARFRAME Wiki** is the primary reference, endorsed by DE: pages for every item, mechanic and quest with context, explanation and guides. When you want to understand how status works, how armor scales or what order to do quests in, the wiki explains it.

**browse.wf** is the raw-data explorer. It resolves the game's internal manifest and gives you exact values with no rounding or editor interpretation. It's for theorycrafters who need the precise number when the wiki's summary isn't enough.

## When to use each

- **Understanding how a mechanic works** → WARFRAME Wiki. Human context.
- **Getting the exact internal value of a stat** → browse.wf. Raw data.
- **Planning quests without spoilers** → WARFRAME Wiki. Readable documentation.
- **Verifying a dubious number for theorycraft** → browse.wf. Exact raw source.

## Recommendation

For almost everything, **WARFRAME Wiki** is the stop — you'll consult it constantly to understand the game. **browse.wf** is the precision tool you pull out when you do serious theorycraft and need the exact value the wiki summarizes or rounds. One explains, the other measures. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
