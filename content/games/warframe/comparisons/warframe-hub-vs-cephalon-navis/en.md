---
title: Warframe Hub vs Cephalon Navis — world state on web or on your phone
description: A short comparison between the web world-state dashboard and the Android app with push notifications.
---

They share the same data backend (`warframestat.us`) and show the same thing —cycles, fissures, Baro, Nightwave, sorties—, so the choice is about platform. **Warframe Hub** is web/desktop; **Cephalon Navis** is the Android app.

## Key difference

**Warframe Hub** lives in the browser. It's handy as a second monitor while you play on PC: you open it, see the whole world state on a large screen, and decide what to do.

**Cephalon Navis** is the native Android app, with system push notifications. It alerts you about Baro, cycles or invasions on your phone even without the PC or browser open. It's for staying current away from the computer.

## When to use each

- **Checking the world state in the browser** → Warframe Hub. Web/desktop.
- **Push notifications on your phone** → Cephalon Navis. Android app.
- **Checking timers away from the PC** → Cephalon Navis. Native mobile.
- **The full overview on a large screen** → Warframe Hub. Better on desktop.

## Recommendation

They don't exclude each other: many players use **Warframe Hub** in the browser while playing and **Cephalon Navis** on their phone for alerts when they're out. If you only play on PC, Hub is enough; if you want something to alert you without relying on the browser being open, Navis is the complement. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
