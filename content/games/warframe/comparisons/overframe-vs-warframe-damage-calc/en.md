Both live in the same build-planning cluster, but they solve different things. **Overframe** plans the full build and shows what the community uses; **Warframe Damage Calculator** concentrates on squeezing a weapon's exact DPS.

## Key difference

**Overframe** is holistic: you build the warframe, the weapon, the companion and see the resulting synergies and stats, with the option to copy community-voted builds. It's planning and discovery.

**Warframe Damage Calculator** is precision: it models the full damage formula —multishot, crit, status, elements, faction, riven— to give you a weapon's exact final number. It's pure damage min-maxing.

## When to use each

- **Planning a full warframe build** → Overframe. It covers the whole loadout.
- **Calculating a weapon's exact DPS with a riven** → Warframe Damage Calculator. More precise.
- **Copying a proven build** → Overframe. Its community database.
- **Comparing the damage ceiling between two weapons** → Warframe Damage Calculator. The final number.

## Recommendation

For 90% of cases, **Overframe** is enough: you plan, copy and tweak. When you reach the last mile of optimizing a weapon and want to know whether a given mod or riven actually adds, **Warframe Damage Calculator** gives you the precision Overframe's simulator doesn't target. They complement each other: Overframe for the what, Damage Calc for the exact how-much. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
