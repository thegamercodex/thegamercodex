---
title: AlecaFrame vs WFInfo — which companion overlay to use in Warframe
description: A comparison between the all-in-one companion suite and the single-purpose open-source overlay for relic rewards.
---

Warframe's two most-used desktop overlays both read the game to give you live information, but they aim at opposite philosophies. **WFInfo** does one thing —relic-reward price-checking— and does it free, open source and lightweight. **AlecaFrame** is the full suite: relics, inventory, foundry, rivens, trading and analytics, all in one overlay.

## Key difference

**WFInfo** is single-purpose. During a fissure, it OCRs the reward screen and shows each option's platinum and ducat value so you pick the best. It does nothing else, which is why it's lightweight, auditable (Apache-2.0) and free of paywalls or telemetry. It's maintained by WFCD.

**AlecaFrame** is the all-in-one companion. It also reads relics, but additionally manages your inventory with one-click Warframe Market listing, explores and snipes rivens, gives you foundry timers and market analytics. The cost: it runs on Overwolf (which some prefer to avoid), isn't open source, and its deeper analytics (ProAnalytics) sit behind Patreon.

## Focus vs breadth

The choice is between focus and breadth. If all you want is to not mess up picking a relic reward, WFInfo is more than enough — many players run it alone precisely because it's light and open source. There's no reason to load a whole suite for that task.

If instead you want a tool that replaces half a dozen tabs —inventory, market, rivens, foundry— and you don't mind the Overwolf overhead, AlecaFrame consolidates all of it into a panel that reads your actual state. For an active trader or someone managing a lot of inventory, that friction savings is real.

## When each wins

- **Picking a relic reward** → WFInfo. Free, perfect, lightweight.
- **Managing inventory and listing to the market** → AlecaFrame. WFInfo doesn't do that.
- **Wanting auditable open-source code** → WFInfo. Apache-2.0.
- **Sniping and evaluating rivens with analytics** → AlecaFrame. No equivalent in WFInfo.
- **Something lightweight without Overwolf or Patreon** → WFInfo. No heavy dependencies.

## Combined recommendation

They don't exclude each other: many players run **WFInfo** for its flawless, free relic reading and add **AlecaFrame** only if they want inventory and riven management. If you had to pick one, WFInfo is the universal floor —everyone benefits— and AlecaFrame is the upgrade for those who live in the game's economy. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
