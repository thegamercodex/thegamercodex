---
title: Warframe Hub vs Tenno Tools — panoramic dashboard or notifications
description: A short comparison between the full world-state dashboard and the alert-focused tracker.
---

Both track Warframe's world state, but with opposite approaches. **Warframe Hub** is the panoramic dashboard; **Tenno Tools** prioritizes active notifications.

## Key difference

**Warframe Hub** shows you everything at a glance: Cetus, Vallis and Cambion day/night cycles, active fissures, the sortie, Baro, Nightwave and invasions, on a single screen with precise timers. It's for viewing the overview and deciding your session.

**Tenno Tools** focuses on alerts: you configure what interests you and it notifies you via browser notifications when it appears, without you having to keep checking. It turns passive tracking into active alerts.

## When to use each

- **Seeing the whole world state at a glance** → Warframe Hub. The full dashboard.
- **Getting alerts for invasions or rewards** → Tenno Tools. Active notifications.
- **Checking the Cetus/Vallis cycle in detail** → Warframe Hub. More complete.
- **Not missing time-gated content without opening the page** → Tenno Tools. Configurable alerts.

## Recommendation

They share the same data backend (`warframestat.us`), so the choice is about format. **Warframe Hub** as a planning screen at login; **Tenno Tools** if you want something to alert you while you do something else. Many players use Hub to view and Tenno Tools for the alerts they don't want to miss. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
