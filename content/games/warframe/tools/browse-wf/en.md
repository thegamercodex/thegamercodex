## What it is

browse.wf is a power-user game-data explorer. It resolves Warframe's internal paths and manifest, giving you access to the exact raw values straight from game data. It also includes a live world-state view and a riven calculator. It's free and open source.

It's aimed at theorycrafters and data-diggers who want the exact numbers the game uses, without the interpretation layer a wiki applies.

## What problem it solves

Wikis and third-party tools sometimes round, simplify or are late to update values. For precision theorycraft —understanding exactly how a stat scales, what internal value a mod has, how a drop is defined— you need the raw source from the game's manifest.

browse.wf solves that by exposing that data directly. When a wiki's summary isn't enough or there's doubt about a number, browse.wf gives you the exact value as defined in game data.

## Differentiation

Versus the wiki, the difference is interpretation vs raw. The wiki explains mechanics with context and human language; browse.wf gives you the exact internal values with no explanation. Wiki to understand how something works, browse.wf for the precise figure when the summary isn't enough.

Versus the drop tables, browse.wf goes beyond drops: it covers the full game manifest, not just drop probabilities.

## What people use it for

**Precision theorycraft**: getting the exact internal values of stats, mods and mechanics.

**Verifying dubious numbers**: when a wiki or tool has a suspicious figure, browse.wf is the raw source.

**Investigating new content**: data-diggers explore the manifest after a patch to see what changed.

**Calculating rivens**: its riven calculator complements the data exploration.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you're just starting or only want to understand how something works, the wiki is far friendlier — browse.wf assumes you know what to look for in game data.

If you want ready builds or explanations, this doesn't apply: it's raw data for advanced use.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to `browse.wf`.
2. Browse or search the game manifest for the value you need.
3. Read the exact raw figure.
4. Optional: use the world-state view or the riven calculator.

## Honest limitations

**Advanced and raw**: it's not for beginners; it assumes familiarity with the game-data structure.

**English only**: the interface and paths are in English.

**No explanation**: it gives you the number, not the context. To understand what it means, the wiki.

## How to get started

Go to `browse.wf` when you need an exact game-data value the wiki doesn't specify or that you want to verify. It's not a daily-use tool for most — it's the precision source for serious theorycraft.
