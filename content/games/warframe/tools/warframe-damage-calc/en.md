## What it is

Warframe Damage Calculator is a web calculator dedicated to weapon damage. It models real per-shot damage accounting for multishot, crit chance/multiplier, status, elemental and faction bonuses, and the exact contribution of a riven. It's built for theorycrafters who want the final number, not an approximation.

It's free and updated with new content (as of May 2026 it already includes recent weapons and mods), so it supports the current game natively.

## What problem it solves

Damage in Warframe is calculated with many multiplicative layers: base, multishot, crit, status, elements, faction, riven. Eyeballing how hard a weapon hits with a given mod setup is error-prone. Overframe shows build stats, but not always the effective DPS against a specific target.

This calculator solves that by modeling the full formula. You input the weapon, the mods and the riven, and get the per-shot damage and resulting DPS, letting you compare configurations precisely and decide which mod or riven is actually worth it.

## Differentiation

Versus Overframe, the difference is focus: Overframe plans the holistic build (warframe + weapon + synergies) and shares community builds; this calculator concentrates on squeezing a weapon's exact DPS. Overframe to design and copy builds, Damage Calc for the fine min-max damage decision.

## What people use it for

**Comparing mod setups**: see which configuration gives more DPS before spending formas.

**Evaluating rivens**: quantify how much a specific riven adds to a weapon's damage.

**Deciding between weapons**: compare the damage ceiling of different weapons with an optimal build.

**Status/crit theorycraft**: understand the balance between crit, status or hybrid builds.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you're just starting and only want a build that works, Overframe gives you ready builds without diving into the math. This calculator is for those who already understand the system and want to optimize in detail.

If you care about the warframe's build (not the weapon's), this doesn't apply — it's specific to weapon damage.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to `warframe-damage.com`.
2. Pick the weapon and slot the mods.
3. Add the riven if you have one, with its stats.
4. Read the per-shot damage and resulting DPS.
5. Adjust mods and compare configurations to find the optimal one.

## Honest limitations

**Advanced**: it assumes you understand the damage formula and terminology; it's not beginner-friendly.

**English only**: the interface isn't translated.

**Weapon niche**: it focuses on weapon damage, not full warframe builds or survivability.

## How to get started

Go to `warframe-damage.com`, pick a weapon you already use and replicate its build to see the DPS. From there, experiment by changing mods and rivens to understand what moves the needle most. It's the tool for the last mile of damage optimization.
