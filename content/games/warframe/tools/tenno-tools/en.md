## What it is

Tenno Tools is a web world-state tracker for Warframe focused on notifications. It covers alerts, invasions, sorties and ongoing activities across all four platforms (PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch), and lets you set up browser notifications to alert you when something specific appears. It's free.

It's an alternative more focused on active alerts than Warframe Hub's panoramic dashboard, though both draw from the same world-state data.

## What problem it solves

Much of Warframe's content is rotating and time-limited: an invasion with a specific reward, a one-off alert, the daily sortie. If you're not watching, it passes. A dashboard like Warframe Hub shows the state, but requires you to open and check it.

Tenno Tools solves that with notifications: you configure what interests you (a certain invasion resource, a certain fissure type) and the site alerts you when it appears, without you having to keep checking. It turns passive tracking into active alerts.

## Differentiation

Versus Warframe Hub, the difference is focus. Hub is the full dashboard to view the whole world state at a glance; Tenno Tools prioritizes notifications so you don't miss time-gated rewards. Hub to review the overview, Tenno Tools to be alerted automatically.

Versus Cephalon Navis (a native Android app), Tenno Tools is web with browser notifications; Navis is the mobile app with push notifications.

## What people use it for

**Receiving invasion alerts**: notifies when an invasion with the reward you want appears.

**Following the sortie and daily activities**: the status of rotating tasks.

**Cross-platform notifications**: timers for PC and consoles.

**Not missing time-gated content**: the core use case — having the site alert you instead of having to check.

## Who this tool isn't for

If you prefer a full dashboard to see everything at a glance (including detailed day/night cycles), Warframe Hub is more panoramic.

If you want phone notifications without the browser open, Cephalon Navis (Android) fits better.

## How it's used in practice

1. Go to `tenno.tools`.
2. Pick your platform.
3. Configure which notifications you want (alert types, rewards).
4. Allow browser notifications.
5. The site alerts you when what you configured appears.

## Honest limitations

**English only**: the interface isn't translated.

**Browser-bound notifications**: it requires the browser available for alerts; for real mobile push, Navis.

**Less panoramic than Hub**: its strength is alerts, not showing the full world state in Hub's detail.

## How to get started

Go to `tenno.tools`, pick your platform and configure notifications for what interests you. Allow browser notifications and leave it running. From there, it alerts you when the rotating content you configured appears.
