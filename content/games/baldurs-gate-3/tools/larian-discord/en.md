## What it is

The official Larian Studios Discord (`discord.com/invite/larianstudios`) is the official community server of the studio behind Baldur's Gate 3. It's not a BG3-exclusive server — it also covers Larian's other projects — but BG3 is the central game, with channels dedicated to announcements, discussion, tech support, and player-to-player help.

As the official server, it's where Larian communicates directly: patches, hotfixes, events, and news go through its announcement channels before spreading to other sources.

## What problem it solves

Keeping up with BG3 means following several sources, each with its own lag. The official Discord collapses that: Larian's announcements hit `#announcements` first, and the discussion and support channels resolve questions in real time with an active community.

For someone who wants to hear about a patch the moment it lands or needs quick technical help, being on the official server is the most direct shortcut to the source.

## What people use it for

**Receiving official announcements**: patches, hotfixes, and news straight from Larian, before other sources.

**Asking for tech support**: using the help channels when something doesn't work, with community and sometimes team responses.

**Discussing the game in real time**: builds, quests, and reactions to changes in live chat.

**Connecting with the community**: finding people for co-op or just following the conversation of the moment.

## Who this tool is NOT for

If you don't like real-time chat or the message volume overwhelms you, an asynchronous forum like the subreddit will serve you better for discussion with history.

If you want structured, searchable information — mechanics, builds, data — a wiki is better; Discord is good for timing and conversation, not as a knowledge base.

## How it's used in practice

1. Join the server via the official invite link and accept the rules.
2. Read the channel guide to know what's discussed where.
3. Subscribe to the announcements role to get patch alerts.
4. Mute the channels you don't care about; the server is large and uncurated it's noise.
5. Use the support or discussion channels depending on what you need.

## Honest limitations

**High volume**: on patch or announcement days, channels move very fast and it's hard to follow substantive conversation.

**Not BG3-exclusive**: by covering all of Larian, part of the server isn't about Baldur's Gate 3.

**Volunteer moderation**: like any large Discord, discussion quality varies by the moment.

**Limited Spanish coverage**: most activity is in English.

## How to get started

Join with the official invite link, accept the rules, and enable the announcements role so patch alerts reach you. Mute what doesn't interest you and leave the server in the background while you play: when Larian posts something important, you'll know instantly. For deep discussion with history, pair it with the subreddit.
