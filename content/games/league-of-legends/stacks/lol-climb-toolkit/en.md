Climbing in League of Legends is rarely about playing more games: it's about playing games with better information and reviewing them afterward. The client won't tell you what to build against your matchup, or which part of your play is costing you. This five-tool stack covers that cycle —before, during, and after each game— without overlapping.

## A game's cycle

**Before playing**, you open **U.GG** to see your champion's optimized build, runes, and counters at your rank, and **OP.GG** to review your own history and, if you want, scout the lobby's players. You reach champ select knowing what to expect.

**During champ select and the game**, **Porofessor** analyzes the lobby live: it gives you your lane matchup, concrete tips, and the composition's win conditions. It's the real-time information layer that U.GG and OP.GG don't cover.

**After playing**, **Mobalytics** steps in with its GPI (Gamer Performance Index): instead of telling you "you played badly," it shows which dimension —farming, vision, aggression, positioning— you're falling short in, so you know what to practice. And to stay ahead of the meta, **Probuilds.net** shows what pros are building right now, especially valuable when a new patch doesn't have consolidated data yet.

## Why these five

Each attacks a different phase of the improvement cycle:

- **Pre-game prep** → U.GG + OP.GG
- **Live information** → Porofessor
- **Coaching and diagnosis** → Mobalytics
- **Getting ahead of the meta** → Probuilds.net

This isn't a stack for casual play; it's for the player who wants to climb with method. Each tool has its full analysis in the codex.
